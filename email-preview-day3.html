SLIDE 2 THE TRAP
Founders scale effort, not systems.
More hustle, more hours, more tabs open. It does not compound. A system runs whether or not you feel like it this week.
SLIDE 3 STAGE 1
Clarity.
A stranger should know what you sell, who it is for, and why it wins, in one sentence. Everything downstream is capped by this.
SLIDE 4 STAGE 2
Capture.
97% of visitors are not ready today. Give them one reason to leave an email or a DM, or you are paying for attention and throwing it away.
SLIDE 5 STAGE 3
Follow-up.
Most sales happen on the 5th to 12th touch. Automate the nurture once and it closes the people you would have lost to your inbox.
SLIDE 6 STAGE 4
Proof and speed.
Show results before they have to decide, and answer fast. Trust plus speed beats a bigger ad budget.

Clarity -> Capture -> Follow-up -> Proof. Run on repeat.
