Marketing Budget: The 2026 Guide

Most companies set marketing budget as "last year + 10%" - which guarantees missed growth or wasted spend. Below: how to build a marketing budget working backwards from revenue goal, channel allocation by stage, and benchmarks by industry.

The Short Answer

Work backwards from revenue goal, not forward from last year's spend. Required pipeline / win rate / lead-to-opportunity ratio gives required leads. Required leads × cost per lead by channel gives required spend. Add fixed costs + brand + experimental and you have a budget that ties to growth, not history.

Why MarkCMO Builds Better Marketing Budgets

  • Backwards-from-goal methodology. Revenue goal -> required pipeline -> required leads -> required spend.
  • Channel benchmarks by stage. Avoid over-rotating paid in early stage or under-investing in brand at maturity.
  • 20-30% brand investment standard. Most companies under-invest in brand and pay 3x more in acquisition.
  • 10-15% experimental budget. Always testing new channels for next year's primary channels.
  • Quarterly reallocation. Budget shifts every quarter based on ROI performance, not annually.
  • Board-ready reporting. Marketing spend tied to pipeline + revenue, not vanity metrics.
