Affiliate Marketing Agency: The 2026 Buyer's Guide

Affiliate marketing drives 15-30% of DTC revenue and 10-20% of B2B SaaS pipeline at mature brands. Below: top 9 affiliate agencies, platform decision matrix (Impact, PartnerStack, ShareASale, CJ, Awin), commission structures, and the operator-led alternative.

The Short Answer

Watch out for two affiliate traps: (1) affiliates gaming attribution via brand-keyword bidding + last-click attribution (you pay commission on customers who already knew you); (2) commission structures that reward first-order revenue without LTV alignment (low-LTV customers get acquired and churn). Fix: prohibit brand bidding, use multi-touch attribution, and structure commissions toward repeat purchase / multi-year retention.
