North Carolina Film Industry Marketing: The 2026 Guide

NC is a top-5 US film production state. 25% tax credit up to $20M per film. Outer Banks (Netflix), Iron Man 3, Hunger Games, Dirty Dancing all filmed here. Below: NC film tax credit mechanics, production company marketing, tourism leverage from productions.

The Short Answer

NC's film tax credit ($30M-$60M annual funding pool, 25% rebate up to $20M per production) is more predictable than Georgia's uncapped-but-politically-uncertain credit. NC production companies marketing to LA need LA representation + NC tax credit fluency + location portfolio + crew network depth.

NC Film + Entertainment Grant Mechanics

  • Rebate rate: 25% of qualifying NC expenses
  • Per-project caps: $20M maximum per feature film, $15M maximum per TV season, $5M maximum per commercial
  • Annual program funding: $30M-$60M annually, determined by NC General Assembly + Governor
  • Minimum spend: $3M for features, $1M per episode for TV, $250K for commercials
  • Qualifying expenses: NC-resident crew + cast wages, NC vendor purchases, NC location fees, NC accommodation
  • Application: NC Film Office (NCDOC), minimum 30 days before shooting
  • Post-production: Documentation + audit required for rebate payment

5 NC Film Industry Marketing Categories

  1. Production-attraction marketing. Selling NC + specific NC locations to LA + NY productions. NC Film Office, Wilmington Regional Film Commission, Charlotte Regional Film Commission, WNC Film Commission.
  2. Production-support vendor marketing. Rental houses, post houses, location managers, casting directors, crew agencies marketing to productions.
  3. Crew + talent recruitment. NC residents recruiting onto productions (key for tax credit qualification).
  4. Tourism leverage. Using productions for tourism marketing (Outer Banks tourism driven by Netflix series, Dirty Dancing tourism at Lake Lure).
  5. Final cut + festival publicity. NC-filmed projects entering festival circuit (Sundance, SXSW, TIFF) coordinating publicity.

NC Production Company Marketing Playbook

  1. LA + NY relationships. Production decisions come from LA + NY; NC production company needs LA representation.
  2. NC tax credit fluency. Sales materials lead with NC tax credit math, qualifying expense calculator.
  3. Location portfolio. Online portfolio showcasing NC location range (coastal, mountain, urban, rural).
  4. Crew + vendor network. Depth of NC crew + vendor relationships, especially key positions.
  5. Production case studies. Past productions, references from line producers + production managers.
  6. Industry events + festivals. AFCI, Locations Show, NAB, AFM, festival circuit.
  7. Industry trade press. Variety, Hollywood Reporter, Backstage, Film + Video coverage.
  8. Direct sales to LA studios + streamers. Relationship-driven outreach to studio physical production teams.
