Music Sync Licensing: The 2026 Buyer's Guide

Music sync drives album re-charts (Stranger Things' Kate Bush, Wednesday's Lady Gaga), brand campaigns ($500K-$10M+ per song), and indie film placements ($500-$5K). Below: sync fees by use, top sync agencies + libraries, music supervisor relationships, and how indies break in.

The Short Answer

Sync is a hidden revenue stream most artists ignore. $5K-$50K/year passive sync income is achievable for indie artists with sync-friendly catalogs placed in libraries. Serious sync careers earn $100K-$1M+ annually. Build the catalog + relationships + library placements first; the placements compound over years.

How Indie Artists Break Into Sync

  1. Sign with sync agency or publisher. They pitch your catalog to music supervisors actively.
  2. Submit to sync libraries. Songtradr, Musicbed, Marmoset accept indie submissions.
  3. Build direct music supervisor relationships. Industry events, mutual connections, AMPS membership.
  4. Make sync-friendly music. Mid-tempo, emotional, instrumental-leaning, vocal-light syncs more easily.
  5. Deliver high-quality stems. 24-bit/96kHz wavs + instrumental + vocal isolated tracks required.
  6. Own your rights cleanly. Clear chain of title with producers/co-writers contracted.
  7. Be available for fast turnaround. Sync requests often need 48-72 hour clearance.
