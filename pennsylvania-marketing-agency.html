Pennsylvania Marketing Agency: The 2026 Buyer's Guide

PA has 3 distinct hubs: Philadelphia (Comcast HQ, biotech, Vanguard $9T+ AUM, Penn), Pittsburgh (CMU AI capital, Aurora, Duolingo, UPMC), Hershey (chocolate + entertainment). Below: top 9 PA agencies + biotech + AI marketing specifics.

The Short Answer

Pennsylvania is really 3 marketing markets: Philly biotech + Comcast + Vanguard finance, Pittsburgh CMU AI + UPMC healthtech, Hershey + central PA logistics. Different agency strengths in each. Pick by region AND industry.

PA's 6 Dominant Industries

  1. Philly biotech + pharma. GSK Upper Providence, Pfizer Collegeville, Merck West Point, Spark Therapeutics, Iovance, Janssen J&J, CHOP gene therapy.
  2. Media + telecom. Comcast HQ (largest US media company), NBCUniversal.
  3. Finance. Vanguard HQ Malvern ($9T+ AUM second-largest asset manager), Independence Blue Cross, PNC.
  4. Pittsburgh tech + AI. CMU AI/robotics leader, Aurora Innovation HQ, Duolingo HQ, Astrobotic.
  5. Healthcare. UPMC (largest US health systems), University of Pittsburgh, Penn Medicine, Jefferson Health.
  6. Manufacturing + CPG. Hershey Company HQ, Air Products + Chemicals HQ, PPL Corporation, AmerisourceBergen/Cencora.
