The thesis

Stop renting your business. Own the whole machine.

The easy path for a relaunch is to bolt together Shopify, MailChimp, QuickBooks, and a marketing agency - then pay all four of them rent, every month, forever, while owning none of it. It is quick to stand up and it quietly bleeds margin as you grow. Worse, when an acquirer looks at the business, they see a pile of third-party subscriptions, not an asset.

BBQ'n Fools is not starting from zero. It is a brand people already love, with awards on the wall and a loyal list. What it needs now is the machine underneath it: one owned system where the store, the email, the inventory, and the books all talk to each other and run with almost no hands on the wheel. We build that machine as IP you own outright - then partner with you to fill the shelf and grow revenue toward the exit.

The build

One owned ecosystem. Four engines. Almost no hands on the wheel.

We are not reskinning Shopify. We build BBQ'n Fools a lean commerce, marketing, operations, and discovery stack on the same infrastructure that runs MarkCMO's own portfolio - Cloudflare at the edge, Supabase for data. It is fast, it is automated, and it is yours to keep.

Engine 01

Commerce + B2B

A storefront that loads in under a second worldwide, branded checkout, bundles, and subscriptions - plus a wholesale portal for retail and distributor accounts, built on the Albertsons-and-distributor playbook you already know. Your own processor, no platform fee per order.

Engine 02

Marketing + CRM

The MailChimp killer. Own your list and your data, with automated lifecycle flows - welcome, waitlist, abandoned cart, post-purchase, restock, win-back - billed by send, not by every "Fan of the Fool" you have ever collected.

Engine 03

Operations + Automation

The piece most brands never build: inventory and stock sync, orders routed automatically to your Stephen Gould 3PL and co-packers, and a live two-way QuickBooks feed - all in one dashboard. We wire it for your point-A-to-Z test run first, then scale. This is the engine that removes ~95% of the manual labor and lets the business run itself.

Engine 04

SEO + Content

Programmatic recipe and pairing pages, technical schema, and an editorial layer engineered to rank for "best brisket rub" and to get cited by AI answer engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity - the new top of funnel.

Launch strategy

Two rooms, two heroes - and a system that serves both.

The sharpest lever in the lineup is that your biggest winners split by audience. The chili-head room - the March Fiery Food & Barbecue Show - wants Spicy Teriyaki Pepper and Chipotle Lime. The everyday shopper who fills a grocery cart wants Signature Seasoning and Mild Sweet BBQ. Most brands pick one and quietly lose the other. The owned platform lets you merchandise, email, and waitlist by audience, so the right hero leads in the right room.

March · Fiery Food & BBQ Show

Lead with the heat

The room is chili heads and heat lovers. Front the line with Spicy Teriyaki Pepper, Chipotle Lime, Deadly Hot, and Burn Ya Twice - the SKUs that already win in that crowd.

DTC + retail · everyday shoppers

Lead with the crowd-pleasers

The grocery-cart shopper wants approachable flavor. Front Signature Seasoning, Mild Sweet BBQ, Signature Barbecue, and Grant's Blend Magic Dust - broad, award-winning, repeat-buy.

Demand control

Waitlist-gated drops

Release in controlled waves with a waitlist. Demand becomes a signal, not a fire drill - "sold out, join the list" builds urgency instead of refunds and bad reviews.

Reach

Influencer ignition

Seed launches with BBQ and hot-sauce creators who already have the audience. Trackable codes, affiliate links, and UGC feeding straight back into the owned site.

Sequencing

Bestsellers first

Open with 4-6 proven SKUs so production and Stephen Gould fulfillment stay sane, then phase in the rest. The "Fans of the Fool" list gets first access.
