Celebrities, executives, athletes, authors, creators - all need ongoing publicity, crisis response, and image management. Below: top 9 talent PR firms, pricing by talent tier, when you need one, and crisis communications playbook.

The Short Answer

Don't hire a publicist until you have something to publicize. The right time is when recurring press demand + active project pipeline justify the retainer. Hiring earlier produces zero results - publicists need underlying work to pitch.
