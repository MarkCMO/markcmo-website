What is the difference between a fractional CMO and a marketing consultant?

A marketing consultant delivers recommendations and reports. A fractional CMO delivers results and owns them. The distinction is accountability: a fractional CMO is a member of your leadership team, sits in your operating meetings, is measured on pipeline and revenue, and is responsible for the people and budget in the marketing function. A consultant gives advice and leaves. A fractional CMO builds the capability and is accountable for what it produces.

What is an interim CMO?

An interim CMO is a full-time or near-full-time marketing leader who serves in the CMO role during a transition — typically while you're conducting a permanent CMO search, recovering from a marketing leadership departure, or navigating a major business transition like a rebrand, merger, or new market entry. An interim CMO vs. a fractional CMO is primarily a question of time commitment and duration: interim is higher commitment for a defined transition period; fractional is ongoing, part-time, and designed for companies that don't need or can't justify a full-time hire.

Who needs marketing leadership consulting?

Companies that need marketing leadership consulting typically share one or more of these characteristics: revenue growth has stalled despite marketing spend, the CEO or sales leader is effectively serving as the head of marketing, the company has tried agencies and freelancers but can't get consistent pipeline results, investors or board members have flagged marketing as a gap, or the company is preparing for a fundraise, M&A process, or new market entry that requires credible marketing leadership. If any of these sound familiar, the answer is marketing leadership — not more marketing execution.
