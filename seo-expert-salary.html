SEO Expert Salary in the US

What an SEO expert earns in the US in 2026, by seniority and engagement model. Director, manager, fractional, agency partner, and freelancer rates compared.

Quick Answer

In-house SEO salaries in the US in 2026: SEO Specialist $60,000-$85,000, SEO Manager $90,000-$140,000, SEO Director $140,000-$220,000 all-in (base, bonus, equity, benefits), VP of SEO or Head of Organic $180,000-$280,000 at growth-stage companies. Fractional SEO experts running their own practice typically clear $200,000-$500,000 per year on 4 to 8 client engagements at $4K-$12K per month each. Top-tier agency principals earn $250,000-$750,000 between salary and equity.

Salary by Seniority

SEO Specialist (1-3 years): $60K-$85K. SEO Manager (4-7 years): $90K-$140K. SEO Director (8+ years): $140K-$220K all-in. VP of SEO or Head of Organic: $180K-$280K at growth-stage, more at enterprise.

Fractional Rates

Senior fractional SEO experts running their own practice: $4K-$20K per month per client. A typical fractional operator with 4-8 active engagements clears $200K-$500K per year revenue, $150K-$400K net after operator costs. Top fractional SEO experts with branded practices clear $500K-$1M per year.

Agency Compensation

Agency SEO Manager: $80K-$120K. Agency SEO Director: $120K-$180K plus bonus. Agency Principal or Partner: $200K-$500K base plus equity. Top-tier agency founders (like Single Grain founder Eric Siu) clear seven figures.

Geographic Adjustment

San Francisco, New York, Boston: add 10-20 percent to base salary ranges. Austin, Denver, Atlanta, Chicago: market rate. Lower-cost metros: subtract 10-15 percent. Remote-first companies (most SEO roles) pay closer to national median regardless of candidate location.
