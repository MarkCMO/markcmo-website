Wilmington NC Marketing Agency: The 2026 Coastal Carolina Guide

Wilmington is NC's largest coastal city + the third-largest US film production hub. Screen Gems Studios, Live Oak Bank (technology-forward community bank), nCino (cloud banking software), tourism. Below: top firms, film industry marketing, fintech marketing specifics.

The Short Answer

Wilmington marketing has 3 specialty paths: (1) Film production marketing - Hollywood-fluent + production-aware. (2) Fintech marketing - Live Oak Bank + nCino + Apiture tier banking software. (3) Tourism + hospitality. Each requires different agency type. Generalist Wilmington agencies handle local services + retail well but miss the specialty work.

Wilmington's 6 Dominant Industries

  1. Film + TV production. Screen Gems Studios, NC film tax credit (25% rebate up to $20M per project), continuous TV + film production.
  2. Banking + fintech. Live Oak Bank ($14B+ assets), nCino (cloud banking software, 2020 IPO), Apiture (digital banking platform).
  3. Tourism + hospitality. Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach, downtown river walk, USS North Carolina battleship.
  4. Port + maritime. Port of Wilmington logistics + shipping.
  5. Healthcare. Novant Health, New Hanover Regional Medical.
  6. Defense + military. Camp Lejeune + MCAS Cherry Point nearby.

Wilmington Film Industry ('Hollywood East')

Wilmington is one of the largest film production hubs in the US, often called 'Hollywood East' or 'Wilmywood.' Screen Gems Studios (EUE/Screen Gems) operates 9 soundstages + backlot + full production support.

Major productions filmed in Wilmington: Iron Man 3, Cape Fear, Dawson's Creek (1998-2003), One Tree Hill (2003-2012 entirely Wilmington), Sleepy Hollow, Outer Banks (Netflix), Eastbound & Down, Under the Dome, Banshee, multiple Lifetime + Hallmark productions.

Film marketing involves: location scouting marketing (selling NC + Wilmington to LA productions), crew + talent recruitment, vendor + supplier marketing to productions, post-production services, tourism leverage (Outer Banks tourism driven by Netflix series).
