Paid Search Agency: The 2026 Buyer's Guide

Paid search in 2026 is AI-driven via Performance Max + Smart Bidding. Manual bid optimization is largely automated. The new edge: creative quality, conversion data quality, and landing page CRO. Below: top 9 paid search agencies, real pricing, the media markup trap, and the operator-led alternative.

The Short Answer

Always ask: "Is media spend marked up?" Agencies that mark up 15-25% on media add hidden cost the CEO often does not see. On $50K/mo media, 20% markup = $10K/mo extra. MarkCMO/WETYR charges 0% markup as standard. That alone saves $60K-$240K/yr at typical paid search budgets.
