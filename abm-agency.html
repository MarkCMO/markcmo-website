ABM Agency: The 2026 Buyer's Guide

Account-based marketing targets named accounts instead of broad demand generation. The economics: 80% of B2B revenue comes from 20% of accounts - so concentrating marketing + sales effort against the right named accounts produces 3-5x higher pipeline efficiency than channel campaigns. Below: top 9 ABM agencies, 1:1 vs 1:few vs programmatic tiers, real pricing, and the operator-led model.

The Short Answer

ABM is not lead gen. It is the discipline of concentrating marketing + sales effort against named target accounts with shared pipeline ownership. Companies that confuse the two run "ABM" by buying a list and running display ads - that fails 100% of the time. Real ABM requires sales-marketing alignment, buying committee mapping, and persona-tailored plays per account.

The 3 Tiers of ABM

Tier Account Count Cost/Account Contract Size
1:1 (Strategic)Top 10-30 accounts$5K-$20K$500K-$5M+
1:few30-200 accounts$500-$2K$50K-$500K
Programmatic200-2,000+ accounts$10-$50$10K-$100K

Best practice: blend all three tiers. 1:1 for top 20 enterprise targets, 1:few for 150 mid-market accounts grouped by industry, programmatic for 1,500 long-tail accounts with intent signals. The mix is calibrated to ACV distribution and sales capacity.

ABM Platform Selection

  • 6sense - predictive intent + technographics. $50K-$200K/yr. Best when buying signals matter most.
  • Demandbase - full-funnel ABM, strong personalization + ad targeting. $60K-$250K/yr.
  • RollWorks - mid-market ABM, simpler implementation. $30K-$120K/yr.
  • HubSpot ABM - built into HubSpot. Best for small/mid B2B on HubSpot.
  • Terminus - mid-market with strong campaign orchestration.
