What gets generated

    
All nine deliverables are live. Run breakdown first - downstream tools depend on it.

    

      
Script Breakdown
Scenes, chars, props, wardrobe, VFX, stunts, weapons, animals, minors, vehicles - every tagged element.

      
Shooting Schedule
Stripboard, shoot days grouped by location + INT/EXT + TOD, cast day-out-of-days.

      
Budget Top Sheet
ATL / BTL-Production / BTL-Post / Other, with line items, contingency, tier comparison.

      
Call Sheets
Per-day auto-generated from schedule + breakdown with cast statuses + safety notes.

      
Shot List
Director-style coverage per scene: type, angle, lens, movement, special-equipment flags.

      
Purchase Orders
Department-level POs: props, wardrobe, set dressing, vehicles, weapons, SFX, catering, equipment rentals.

      
Location Scouting
Per-location scout report: permit type, fees, lead time, parking, power, sound, scout checklist.

      
Safety + Insurance
Risk memos per flag (firearms, stunts, animals, water, vehicles, minors) + master COI checklist.

      
Post-Production Plan
Editorial schedule, VFX shot list, color, sound, music, distributor deliverables.
