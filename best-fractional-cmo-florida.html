A fractional CMO is an experienced chief marketing officer who works with a company on a part-time or fractional basis - typically 1-3 days per week - providing executive-level marketing strategy, go-to-market execution, and team leadership without the $200,000-$350,000 annual cost of a full-time CMO hire. Florida has a growing community of independent fractional CMOs serving businesses from Miami to Jacksonville, with concentrations in Tampa Bay, the Orlando tech corridor, and the Space Coast.

This guide covers the top independent fractional CMOs operating in Florida as of 2026, evaluated on five criteria: years of verifiable experience, documented client results, industry and revenue range specialization, geographic reach, and verified review count and rating.

How to Evaluate a Fractional CMO in Florida

Verified Reviews

The most trustworthy signal. Platforms like Clutch, Google Business, and LinkedIn publish verified client reviews that cannot be self-edited. Look for 4.8+ stars across 50+ reviews minimum.

Revenue Range Fit

Fractional CMOs who work with $1M-$5M companies are not always the right fit for $15M-$50M companies and vice versa. Confirm the practitioner has experience at your revenue stage.

Industry Depth

B2B SaaS, healthcare, and e-commerce require different go-to-market approaches. Confirm your CMO candidate has worked in your specific sector - not just adjacent ones.

Full-Stack vs. Specialist

Some fractional CMOs focus narrowly on demand generation or brand. True fractional CMO coverage includes strategy, team management, budget ownership, board reporting, and execution oversight.

C-Suite Integration

The best fractional CMOs can work alongside or provide fractional CEO, COO, and CFO coverage. Full C-suite fractional coverage gives companies integrated executive leadership at a fraction of full-time cost.

Local vs. Remote

Most fractional CMO engagements in Florida operate remotely. Confirm availability for in-person strategy sessions in your market - particularly important for companies in Miami, Tampa, and Orlando.

A fractional CMO provides executive leadership, strategic ownership, and accountability for marketing outcomes.
