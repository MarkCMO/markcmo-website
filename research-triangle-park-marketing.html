Research Triangle Park Marketing: The 2026 RTP Company Guide

RTP is the largest research park in the US: 300+ companies, 60,000+ employees. GSK, Eli Lilly, Biogen, BASF, Cisco, IBM, NetApp, NVIDIA, Apple, Google presence. Below: how RTP companies market, biotech + tech marketing playbooks, top specialist firms.

The Short Answer

RTP marketing is heavily B2B + regulated-industry + scientific-audience compared to most US metros. Marketing in RTP requires industry expertise (biotech compliance, enterprise IT sales motion, scientific content) that generalist agencies don't have. Pick specialists.

RTP Biotech Marketing Requirements

  1. FDA-compliant content workflows. Medical affairs + regulatory + legal review before publication. ISI on every promotional asset. PI appended to consumer-facing.
  2. Peer-reviewed publication strategy. Clinical trial data + RWE published in NEJM, JAMA, Lancet, therapeutic area journals.
  3. Conference + congress presence. HIMSS, ESMO, ASCO, ASH, AHA, ESC, EASD for therapeutic area engagement.
  4. KOL + advisory board management. Key opinion leader relationships drive clinical opinion + adoption.
  5. Sales force enablement. Specialty + community sales reps need educational materials, congress booth assets, peer-to-peer programs.
  6. Patient + advocacy engagement. Disease state awareness, treatment access advocacy.
  7. Digital + omnichannel HCP marketing. Programmatic to physician audiences, conference virtual integration.
