B. One-Time Setup Tasks

  1. Create a SendGrid account → verify [email protected] as sender domain
  2. Create a Zoom or Riverside webinar link → update WEBINAR_CONFIG.zoomLink in webinar-signup.js
  3. Update WEBINAR_CONFIG.date in the Netlify function with your first session datetime (UTC)
  4. Create a JSONBin bin for webinar leads → set bin ID in env vars
  5. Upload the Revenue Leak Playbook PDF to /pdfs/markcmo-revenue-leak-playbook.pdf on your site
  6. Add "Webinar" to the main nav in index.html (optional, already done on webinar-specific pages)
  7. Set up Mailchimp audience + automation for attendee drip sequence
  8. Set up Stripe product for CMO Audit ($997) and CMO Accelerator Kit ($47)

C. Webinar Date Configuration

To update the webinar date, open netlify/functions/webinar-signup.js and change:

date: "20260401T180000Z",   // e.g. April 1, 2026 at 6:00 PM UTC
endDate: "20260401T193000Z", // 75 min later
zoomLink: "https://zoom.us/j/YOUR_MEETING_ID",

4. The Full Funnel Architecture

Stage 1: Discovery → webinar.html

SEO, social, LinkedIn posts, email blasts, podcast mentions, direct referrals → webinar.html registration page

Stage 2: Registration

Form submit → netlify/functions/webinar-signup.js → stores lead → sends confirmation email with ICS calendar file → redirects to webinar-confirmation.html

Stage 3: Confirmation Page (webinar-confirmation.html)

Personalized greeting → next steps → immediate upsell: CMO Audit ($997) → digital product: CMO Kit ($47) → referral partner program → COI program

Stage 4: Pre-Webinar Drip (Mailchimp / Email)

T-7 days: welcome + prep questions | T-2 days: reminder + tease | T-1 day: reminder + agenda | T-2 hours: final link + "bring your questions"

Stage 5: Live Webinar (Riverside / Zoom)

75-minute session → mid-session: CMO Audit offer mentioned once → end: playbook announced + call-to-action for strategy call

Stage 6: Post-Webinar Email (T+0 hours)

Playbook delivery email with download link → webinar-playbook-delivery.html → free strategy call CTA → paid engagement options

Stage 7: Follow-Up Sequence (T+1 to T+14 days)

T+1: "What did you think?" | T+3: case study email | T+7: hard CTA for strategy call | T+14: last chance for CMO Audit offer

5. Email Sequence (Full Copy Framework)

Email 1: Immediate Confirmation (auto-sent by Netlify function)

Subject: ✅ You're registered: "The Revenue Leak Audit", here's your calendar invite

Content: Webinar details, ICS attachment, prep questions, upsell offer. This email is fully built into the Netlify function.

Email 2: T-2 Days (Mailchimp automation)

Subject: 48 hours out, what most people miss about audits

Teaser content about one of the 7 revenue leaks. Short, punchy. Drives curiosity. No links except "add to calendar" and "invite a colleague."

Email 3: T-1 Day

Subject: Tomorrow at [TIME], your prep checklist

Bulleted checklist: have your revenue numbers handy, think of one thing you can't figure out, block 90 minutes. Replay will be available 48 hours only.

Email 4: T-2 Hours

Subject: Starting in 2 hours, Zoom link inside

One-click join button. "Bring your biggest marketing question." No fluff.

Email 5: Post-Webinar Playbook Delivery (T+0 hours)

Subject: Your Revenue Leak Playbook is ready (+ a note from Mark)

Playbook download link → webinar-playbook-delivery.html. Personal note from Mark. Strategy call CTA. CMO Audit offer with deadline.

Email 6: T+3 Days

Subject: The $800K in pipeline they didn't know they had

Short case study (anonymized). One company. What we found. What we fixed. What it cost. What it returned. Closes with: "If that sounds familiar, the call takes 30 minutes."

Email 7: T+7 Days

Subject: Real talk: did you use the playbook?

Low-key, personal tone. "If you've been meaning to but haven't had time, here's the fast-start version." 3-bullet version of the audit checklist. Strategy call link.

Email 8: T+14 Days (Last Chance)

Subject: Closing this out

"The CMO Audit offer from the webinar closes on Friday. After that, regular pricing applies." Clear scarcity. No fake urgency, set a real deadline and honor it.

7. Referral Partner Program

The Pitch: "Know a founder, CEO, or VP who's struggling with their marketing? Make one intro. If they sign, you earn 20% of their first 90 days, with no cap."

Terms

  • Commission: 20% of first 90 days of any referred client who signs a fractional engagement
  • Example: $5,000/month client → $3,000 paid to referrer within 30 days of client's first payment
  • Qualification: Lead must be new to Mark (no existing contact), and referrer must make a warm introduction (email or phone)
  • Payout: PayPal, Stripe transfer, or check, referrer's choice
  • Tracking: Honor system + email confirmation. Sign the Referral Partnership Agreement (already on the site: documents/referral.html)
  • Expiry: 90-day cookie, if the referral converts within 90 days of intro, commission applies

Who to Target as Referral Partners

  • Attendees of this webinar (they've seen Mark's quality firsthand)
  • Past clients (best source, already trust Mark)
  • Executive recruiters (often know companies needing CMO-level help)
  • Business brokers and M&A advisors
  • CPAs and business accountants (see their clients' P&Ls)
  • Business coaches and consultants who don't offer marketing services

9. Digital Product Strategy

Product 1: Revenue Leak Playbook (Free → $97)

The 20-page PDF delivered free to webinar attendees. The expanded 40-page version with case studies, industry benchmarks, and video walkthroughs sells for $97 as a standalone product via Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy.

Where to sell it: On-site landing page, Gumroad, LinkedIn posts, post-webinar emails.

Product 2: CMO Accelerator Kit ($47-$97)

17 templates in Notion + PDF format: ICP builder, sprint tracker, audit checklist, channel ROI calculator, board deck template, and more. Delivered via Gumroad automated delivery.

Upsell placement: Webinar confirmation page, post-webinar email, social media.

Product 3: Mini-Course (Future, $297-$497)

4-module video course: "The 90-Day CMO Sprint." Each module is 20-30 minutes. Hosted on Teachable or Gumroad. Webinar replay clips become free preview content.

Product 4: Live Workshop ($997-$1,997)

Small-group (8-12 people) intensive. 3 hours live. Each participant gets a personalized audit during the session. Seats sold via the webinar funnel. Premium tier above the solo audit.

10. The Live Webinar (Hosting Options)

Option A: Riverside.fm (Recommended, You Already Use It)

Use Riverside for both recording and webinar delivery. Pros: Professional recording quality, multi-track audio, easy replay export, integrates with your existing podcast workflow. Create a "Studio" link for the webinar.

Action: In webinar-signup.js, set zoomLink to your Riverside studio URL.

Option B: Zoom

Free tier: 100 participants, 40-minute limit (upgrade prompt at 40 min is actually a good natural break). Paid: $15.99/month for up to 100 attendees, no time limit.

Option C: Demio ($49/month)

Purpose-built webinar platform. Built-in registration (could replace the custom form), email reminders, attendee analytics, automated replay. Worth it at 200+ monthly attendees.
