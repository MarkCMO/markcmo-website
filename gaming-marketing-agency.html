Gaming marketing covers indie $5K campaigns to AAA $25M+ launches to live ops $200M+/year programs. Below: Steam Wishlist strategy, streamer activation playbook, top 9 firms, and the indie marketing path under $50K.

The Short Answer

For indie PC games, Steam Wishlists are the most important pre-launch metric. Launch with under 30K Wishlists and Steam's algorithm buries you. Launch with 100K+ and the algorithm amplifies you. Optimize the Steam page + Wishlist campaign 6-18 months before launch.

The Indie Game Marketing Playbook (Under $50K)

  1. Steam page live 6-18 months before launch. Capture Wishlists early.
  2. Devlog content. YouTube + TikTok BTS development builds organic following.
  3. Gamedev community engagement. Twitter/X, share progress GIFs, follow + engage.
  4. Steam Next Fest participation. Major Wishlist-driving event 3x/year.
  5. Streamer + content creator outreach. Genre + size matched, quality over quantity.
  6. Indie press outreach. PC Gamer indie, Rock Paper Shotgun, Indie Game Plus, IGN indie.
  7. Discord community building. Developer interaction creates super-fans.
  8. Demo participation. Steam Next Fest, Day of the Devs, Indie Cade.
  9. Convention presence (if budget). PAX, GDC indie showcase.
  10. Launch trailer optimized. Shareability + Reddit community posting.
