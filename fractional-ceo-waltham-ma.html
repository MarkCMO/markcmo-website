Why Waltham Businesses Hire a Fractional CEO

Waltham is a major biotech hub on Boston's Route 128 technology corridor home to Brandeis University and hundreds of life sciences companies. The local business community spans biotechnology, technology, financial services, creating a competitive market where companies that invest in senior executive leadership outgrow those that don't. A Fractional CEO gives you the executive-level expertise to strategic direction, leadership transitions, board relations, and business growth - without the overhead of a full-time hire.

For businesses in the Greater Boston area, the fractional model makes particular sense: you get a senior executive who has built and scaled functions like yours before, available for 10-20 hours per week, at a fraction of the full-time cost. You keep the capital for growth.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a Fractional CEO cost in Waltham, MA?

A Fractional CEO in Waltham typically costs $3,000 to $15,000 per month depending on scope and engagement hours. Most Greater Boston businesses engage at 15-20 hours per week. This compares to $200,000 to $450,000+ per year for a full-time executive.

Do you work on-site in Waltham or remotely?

We work in the model that best fits your business - remote, hybrid, or on-site in Waltham and the Greater Boston area as needed. Most engagements are primarily remote with on-site sprints for key milestones and team sessions.

How is a Fractional CEO different from a consultant?

A Fractional CEO is an embedded executive who owns the executive leadership function - they lead your team, manage vendors, and are accountable to outcomes. A consultant advises from the outside and delivers reports. The fractional model means direct execution responsibility, not recommendations you have to implement yourself.

What industries do you serve in the Greater Boston area?

In Waltham and the broader Greater Boston market, we serve biotechnology, technology, financial services. We bring experience from 15+ years of C-suite engagements across multiple industries and apply that cross-sector pattern recognition to your specific market context.
