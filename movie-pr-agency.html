Movie PR Agency: The 2026 Producer's Guide

Movie PR runs $25K-$2M per title. The press tour, premieres, festival positioning, and talent media bookings either build the release or kill it. Below: top 9 firms, when to hire, awards strategy integration, and the operator-led alternative for indie producers.

The Short Answer

Hire PR 12-16 weeks before festival premiere or theatrical release - not the week of. PR firms need runway to book press, secure premiere positioning, coordinate with talent reps, and stage the rollout. Last-minute PR hires get last-minute coverage.
