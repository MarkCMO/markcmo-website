What We Heard

The Three Questions Phase 1 Should Answer.

You described an engagement that establishes a baseline of how technology, systems, and organizational practices currently support the library's goals — and then identifies where the gaps are between current state and what your continued growth will require.

Per Your RFQ
  1. Where things stand today. How technology is structured, how services are delivered, how teams are organized to support that work.
  2. How well current roles and systems are aligned to support the organization's mission.
  3. What a practical future state could look like in support of the library's goals.

Your stated preference for a rough order of magnitude over a lengthy formal proposal is reflected in the structure here: three engagement options, three flat prices, no contingencies, no credits, no escalators.
