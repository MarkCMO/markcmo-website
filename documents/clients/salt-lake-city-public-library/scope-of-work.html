SLC-DA-002 · Scope of Work

Discovery & Alignment Engagement Scope.

The reference document. What's delivered, the methodology used, what's required from the library, what's explicitly out of scope, and how changes to scope are handled. Phase 1 and Phase 2 SOWs will be issued separately if and when those engagements are initiated.

Engagement Summary

The Two-Week Engagement.

Engagement code: SLC-DA · Engagement type: Fixed-fee discovery · Duration: 2 calendar weeks from kickoff · Fee: $12,500 USD · Payment terms: Net 10 from Square invoice issuance · Deliverable format: PDF memo + 60-minute leadership readout (in-person or video, library's choice)

The engagement establishes a baseline read of how technology, systems, and organizational practices currently support the library's mission — deep enough to scope a Phase 1 engagement properly, light enough to deliver in 2 weeks without disrupting library operations.

Module 01
Module 01 · Stakeholder Interviews

6–8 Leadership Conversations

Inputs
Org chart, leadership directory, library board contact (if applicable), department head list. Provided by Tyler Bahr or designated point of contact.
Activities
45-minute structured interviews with: Executive Director, CFO, IT lead, Operations lead, Public Services lead, Branch Manager (one), Collections / Programs lead, board liaison if available. Standard interview protocol covering technology, decision rights, hand-offs, and stated vs. lived workflows.
Outputs
Anonymized interview synthesis (no quotes attributed to individuals), preserved as appendix to the final memo.
Library responsibilities
Schedule interviews on calendar; provide 1 brief warm-up email to interviewees from library leadership.
Module 02
Module 02 · Technology Inventory Snapshot

What's In Use, What's Not, What's Stuck

Inputs
Current vendor list, ILS / catalog system, network & identity systems, learning / programming systems, AV/streaming, point-of-sale (if applicable), HR / finance / payroll, and any patron-facing apps.
Activities
Light desk research and 1–2 IT-side conversations. Catalogs systems by category (patron-facing, staff-facing, admin/finance, infrastructure), notes integration points and known pain points, identifies orphaned systems and shadow IT.
Outputs
1-page system inventory in the final memo. Not a full technical audit — that is Phase 1 territory.
Library responsibilities
Provide read-only access to the existing vendor / system list. No system access or credentials needed for this engagement.
Module 03
Module 03 · Decision-Flow Snapshot

How Technology Decisions Get Made

Inputs
Recent (last 18 months) examples of technology decisions: a new-system buy, a vendor renewal, a deferred initiative. Provided informally during interviews.
Activities
Map who initiates, who recommends, who approves, who is informed. Identify where decisions speed up vs. stall. Note governance gaps.
Outputs
RACI-style summary embedded in the final memo's "alignment risks" section.
Library responsibilities
2–3 example decisions referenced anonymously by participants during interviews.
Module 04
Module 04 · Lay of the Land Memo

The Primary Deliverable

Format
5-page PDF memo, plain-English, executive-readable. Prepared in markcmo.com brand styling, delivered as a private password-protected URL plus PDF attachment.
Sections
(1) Current State Read — what we observed, summarized. (2) Alignment Risks — the top 3–5 places where roles, systems, and goals don't line up. (3) Three Framed Phase 1 Options — lighter / standard / deeper, each with scope, fee, and tradeoffs.
Confidentiality
Memo is delivered to Tyler Bahr only. Distribution to other parties (board, executive director, etc.) is at the library's discretion.
Revisions
One round of revision included if requested within 14 days of delivery.
Module 05
Module 05 · Leadership Readout

60-Minute Walkthrough

Format
In-person if Mark is in Salt Lake City for the engagement, or by video conference. Library's choice. Recording optional and at library's discretion.
Audience
Whoever Tyler Bahr designates — typically CFO + Executive Director + IT lead. Up to 8 attendees.
Agenda
Walk through the memo, answer clarifying questions, discuss the three Phase 1 options. No decision required at the readout — the library can take time to consider next steps.
Follow-up
Up to 14 days of email Q&A clarification on the memo included. Phase 1 sign-up is a separate signed engagement at the library's option.
Out of Scope

What This Engagement Does Not Include.

Explicit Exclusions
  • No implementation. No tooling changes, no vendor procurement, no staffing changes.
  • No technical audit. No code review, network pen-test, security audit, or compliance review.
  • No staff interviews beyond leadership. Branch staff, programming staff, and front-line personnel are interviewed in Phase 1, not Discovery.
  • No vendor outreach on library's behalf. No talking to current or potential vendors during this engagement.
  • No patron data review. Discovery does not require access to patron records, circulation data, or PII. If incidental patron data is observed, it is not retained or referenced.
  • No public deliverable. All work product is confidential to the library.

If the library wants any of the above, it is in scope for Phase 1 (Operational Assessment) or Phase 2 (Implementation), with separate fees and timelines.

Library Responsibilities

What We Need From You.

Required from Salt Lake City Public Library
  • A single point of contact (default: Tyler Bahr) for scheduling, intake, and clarifications.
  • Org chart, current vendor list, system list. Provided by start of Week 1.
  • Calendar access for 6–8 leadership interviews during Week 1.
  • One warm-up email from library leadership to interviewees explaining the engagement and asking for cooperation.
  • Designated audience for the 60-minute readout in Week 2.
  • Payment of the $12,500 invoice within 10 business days of issuance, per public-procurement norms.
Confidentiality & Data Handling

How Information Is Protected.

All information shared during the engagement — verbal, written, observed, or inferred — is treated as confidential to Salt Lake City Public Library. Information is held by Mark Gabrielli (sole engagement lead) and not shared with subcontractors, partners, or any third party. Discovery work product is stored encrypted at rest in markcmo.com's Supabase + Storage infrastructure (US-based). On engagement close, Mark retains the executed proposal and the final memo for the standard 7-year business records period; all interview notes and ancillary materials are destroyed within 30 days of memo delivery unless the library requests otherwise.

If the engagement transitions to Phase 1 or Phase 2, data handling is re-stated in those engagement documents and may include additional protections (e.g. signed BAAs or DPAs) at the library's direction.

Change Control

If Scope Needs To Change.

The fixed fee covers the modules above. If the library wants to add interviews, expand modules, or accelerate the timeline, a written change order is issued before the additional work starts. Change orders carry a separate fee, are signed by both parties, and do not affect the original $12,500 fee or the original 2-week timeline unless explicitly agreed.

Conversely, if the library wants to descope (e.g. fewer interviews, no readout), the engagement fee can be reduced via written change order. There is no minimum modules requirement, but the integrity of the deliverable depends on at least Modules 01, 02, and 04.

Acceptance Criteria

When This Engagement Is Done.

The engagement is considered complete when (a) the 5-page Lay of the Land memo has been delivered to Tyler Bahr, (b) the 60-minute readout has been held and either recorded or summarized in writing, and (c) the 14-day Q&A clarification window has closed. The library does not need to "approve" findings — the deliverable is an independent assessment, not a co-authored document. Disagreement with findings is documented as part of the readout and does not affect engagement closure.
