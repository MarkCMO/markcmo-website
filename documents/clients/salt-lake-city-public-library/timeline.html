Discovery & Alignment
SLC-DA · 2 Weeks · $12,500 USD Fixed

Discovery & Alignment Engagement

The 10 working days from kickoff to delivery. Scheduled to minimize disruption to library operations: leadership interviews bunched in Week 1, synthesis and writing in Week 2.

Week 1 · Days 1–3

Kickoff + Interview Sprint Begins

30-min kickoff call with Tyler Bahr. Vendor list and org chart received. First 3 leadership interviews completed.

Week 1 · Days 4–5

Interview Sprint Continues

Remaining 3–5 interviews completed. Light desk research on technology inventory begins. End-of-week informal status note to Tyler.

Week 2 · Days 6–8

Synthesis + Decision-Flow Mapping

Interview synthesis. Technology inventory completed. Decision-flow patterns identified. Three Phase 1 options framed.

Week 2 · Days 9–10

Memo Delivery + Readout

5-page Lay of the Land memo delivered Day 9. 60-minute leadership readout scheduled Day 10. 14-day Q&A clarification window opens.

Phase 1 (If/When Initiated)
SLC-P1 · 8 Weeks · $52,500 USD Fixed

Operational Assessment

Phase 1 is presented here for context. It is a separate fixed-fee engagement with its own contract, not a continuation of Discovery & Alignment. The library may or may not initiate Phase 1; there is no obligation either way.

Weeks 1–2

Stakeholder Mobilization

Full interview schedule built (15–25 interviews). System inventory deepens with read-only access. Branch + program staff outreach begins.

Weeks 3–4

Interview Wave

Leadership re-interviewed (deeper). Department heads, branch managers, programming staff, IT, finance, HR. Process maps drafted as interviews complete.

Week 5

Process & Decision-Flow Mapping

Cross-team workflow maps completed. Technology decision rights documented (RACI). Service-delivery models inventoried.

Week 6

Alignment Scoring

Each major service line scored against current technology, current org structure, and stated goals. Gap matrix produced.

Week 7

Future-State Design

Working session with library leadership. 3-year future-state vision drafted. Priority initiatives identified. Phase 2 options scoped.

Week 8

Final Readout + Phase 2 Proposal

Board-ready presentation delivered. Full Phase 1 deliverable (3 reports, 1 presentation, 1 future-state document) handed over. Phase 2 proposal issued separately.

Phase 2 Paths (If/When Initiated)
SLC-P2A · 12 Weeks · $95,000 USD Fixed

Phase 2A · Implementation Roadmap Build

Fixed-scope. Builds the actual playbook the library will execute against, with vendor RFPs, governance changes, and a 24-month tech roadmap. Output is a buy-list, a sequence, and a way to measure progress.

Weeks 1–3

Roadmap Skeleton

Initiative briefs drafted. Sequencing logic documented. Capacity model built (what library staff + budget can absorb in any 6-month window).

Weeks 4–7

Governance + RFP Drafting

Decision-rights playbook drafted. RFP scopes drafted for each major buy. Vendor evaluation rubric set.

Weeks 8–10

Roadmap Refinement

Working sessions with leadership to validate sequencing, dependencies, and trade-offs. Final 24-month roadmap locked.

Weeks 11–12

Final Delivery + Handover

Roadmap, governance playbook, and RFP-ready scopes packaged and delivered. Optional: standalone board / staff briefings included.

Critical Path

What Could Slip The Timeline.

Risks To Discovery & Alignment Schedule

Interview availability. Discovery requires 6–8 leadership conversations in Week 1. If 2 or more interviewees can't be scheduled within a 5-day window, Week 1 slips and Week 2 delivery slips with it. Mitigation: library schedules interviews during contract execution week, before kickoff.

Risks To Phase 1 Schedule

Branch + programming staff availability. Phase 1 reaches deeper into the org — 15–25 stakeholders. If the library's seasonal calendar (summer programming, school-break events, fundraising drive) collides with interview weeks 3–4, expect a 1–2 week extension at no additional cost. Mitigation: identify the right kickoff window during Discovery readout.

Risks To Phase 2A Schedule

Vendor & RFP cooperation. The Roadmap build assumes the library wants RFP-ready scopes for vendor procurement. If the library's procurement office requires internal approvals to share RFP language with consultants, weeks 4–7 can slip. Mitigation: Phase 1 surfaces this early; we route around it in scope if needed.
