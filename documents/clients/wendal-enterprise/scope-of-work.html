01 · Engagement Definition

What This Engagement Is, And What It Isn't.

The Business Discovery Audit is a structured, fixed-fee diagnostic engagement designed to produce an honest, evidence-based snapshot of the current state of Wendal Enterprise Inc. and its operating brand, Unique Tours and Rentals Ltd., and to translate that snapshot into a sequenced 12-month roadmap. It is diagnostic, not implementation. The deliverables are documentation, analysis, and strategy, not operational execution.

02 · Modules & Activities

Six Modules. Same Order. Every Engagement.

The audit runs through six modules in fixed sequence. Each module has defined inputs (what we need from you), activities (what we do), and outputs (what gets written into the deliverables).

01
Module One

Workflow Discovery & Mapping

~10 hours

Document the full operating workflow of Unique Tours and Rentals from customer awareness → booking → confirmation → dispatch → on-site execution → tour completion → photo/video delivery → review request → repeat-booking funnel. Identify which steps are automated, which are manual, which are owner-dependent, and which are tribal-knowledge-only.

Inputs Required From Client

  • Read-only access (or screenshares) of booking platform
  • Sample reservation flow walk-through (live or recorded)
  • Org chart or staff/role description
  • Standard operating procedures, if any exist
  • Sample customer email / SMS confirmations

Activities Performed

  • Step-by-step current-state workflow documentation
  • Owner-dependency tagging (Wendal-only vs. delegable)
  • Automation gap identification
  • Handoff/communication-break mapping
  • Visual current-state workflow diagram
02
Module Two

Systems & Tech Stack Inventory

~8 hours

Build a complete inventory of every tool, platform, and account in use today. For each: cost, owner, integration status, redundancy, and risk level. We also flag tools that should exist but don't.

Stack Categories Reviewed

  • Website + DNS + hosting (uniquetoursandrentals.com)
  • Reservation / booking engine
  • Payment processor(s), card-present + card-not-present
  • OTA channels: GetYourGuide, Viator, TripAdvisor Experiences
  • CRM / customer database
  • Email + SMS + WhatsApp comms
  • Photo & video delivery (guide-to-guest)
  • Fleet, jet-ski, and horse-management records
  • Accounting / bookkeeping
  • Analytics (Google Analytics, Meta, OTA dashboards)
  • Social media + scheduling tools

For Each Tool We Document

  • Vendor + plan/tier
  • Monthly/annual cost (USD)
  • Internal owner / admin
  • Integrations (in & out)
  • Redundancy (is anyone else doing this?)
  • Single-point-of-failure rating
  • Replacement / consolidation opportunity
03
Module Three

Bottleneck & Pain-Point Diagnostic

~10 hours

Identify, classify, and rate every bottleneck that is currently costing the business time, money, attention, customer trust, or staff retention. Each is documented with an evidence anchor (review, dataset, observation, or owner interview quote) and a severity rating.

Bottleneck Domains

  • Booking-to-cash cycle latency
  • Manual / double-entry between systems
  • Owner-dependent decisions blocking velocity
  • Refund & dispute handling (inc. partial-refund cases visible in TripAdvisor reviews)
  • Pre-ride / pre-tour rider-assessment process
  • Safety briefing & incident-protocol gaps
  • Staff onboarding & cross-training
  • OTA dependency vs. direct-booking %
  • Cash flow / FX exposure (USD revenue, TC operating cost)

Severity Rating Framework

  • Critical: risks revenue, safety, or brand survival within 30 days
  • High: limits ability to scale to second/third location
  • Medium: efficiency loss, fixable in 90 days
  • Low: cosmetic, future-quarter cleanup
04
Module Four

Competitive & Market Analysis

~12 hours

Two-part analysis: Turks & Caicos competitive benchmark, plus a US market-entry preliminary read for West Palm Beach and Atlanta.

Part A · Turks & Caicos Benchmark

  • Hydro Charters (jet ski tours)
  • Mako Watersports (Leeward jet ski)
  • Caribbean Cruisin' (Seafari combo)
  • Five Cays Watersports (rentals + tours)
  • Floe Lux Tour Company (private jet ski)
  • Turks & Caicos Flyboard (Jet Ski Safari)
  • Side-by-side: pricing, durations, OTA presence, ratings, review volume, positioning
  • Where Unique Tours wins vs. loses on each lens

Part B · US Market Penetration Read

  • Source-market data: ~81% of TC visitors are US-based
  • Atlanta (ATL → PLS): direct lift on Delta & Frontier, ~3 hr flight
  • South Florida (MIA / PBI / FLL → PLS): feeder economics
  • West Palm Beach competitive set (boat / jet ski / experiential rentals)
  • Atlanta storefront/satellite-office logic vs. pure marketing presence
  • Pre-trip booking funnel: capture US customers before they board the plane
  • Cruise-passenger overlap (~1.3M annually) vs. stayover (~640K)
Important Scope Note

The US market analysis in this engagement is a preliminary read built from public data, OTA signal, and competitive benchmarking. It is not a full market-entry study, real-estate scout, or licensing/legal feasibility report for FL or GA operations. Those are recommended as separate Phase 2 engagements if the audit indicates green-light economics.

05
Module Five

Roadmap Construction (30 / 90 / 6mo / 12mo)

~12 hours

Translate the diagnostic into a sequenced, owner-tagged, cost-tagged execution plan across four horizons. Built so individual line items can be handed to staff or contractors without re-explanation.

Horizon Themes

  • 30 Days: stop the bleeding: critical fixes, safety/liability gaps, owner-dependency band-aids, low-effort revenue lifts
  • 90 Days: stabilize and document: systems consolidation, SOPs, direct-booking lift, review-velocity program, staff cross-training
  • 6 Months: West Palm Beach launch readiness: entity, licensing path, real-estate criteria, fleet/staff plan, marketing pre-launch
  • 12 Months: Atlanta + multi-location operating model: HQ vs. local-ops split, brand architecture, cross-location booking, financial reporting cadence

Each Roadmap Item Includes

  • Action description (one sentence, plain English)
  • Owner (Wendal / staff role / contractor / TBD)
  • Estimated cost band (USD)
  • Estimated time investment (hours/days)
  • Dependencies (what must happen first)
  • Definition of "done"
  • Risk if skipped
06
Module Six

Deliverable Packaging & Walkthrough

~6 hours + 1 hour live

Final QA, formatting, packaging, secure delivery, and live walkthrough call. The walkthrough is recorded and the recording is delivered along with the documents.

Deliverables Produced

  • Executive Findings Brief (1-page PDF)
  • Business Discovery Audit Report (25 to 35-page PDF)
  • Workflow & Systems Map (PNG + editable file)
  • Competitive & Market Analysis (8 to 12-page PDF)
  • 30/90/6mo/12mo Roadmap (10 to 14-page PDF)
  • 60-minute walkthrough recording

Delivery Method

  • Secure email link to PDF bundle
  • Optional Google Drive / Dropbox folder mirror
  • Walkthrough call: Zoom or Google Meet
  • 14-day post-delivery email window for clarification questions
03 · Out Of Scope

To keep this engagement honest and the price stable, the following are explicitly out of scope. Any of them can be scoped as a separate engagement after the audit is delivered.

Out Of Scope

Hiring, Operations Execution & Ongoing Retainer

  • Recruiting, interviewing, or hiring staff for any location
  • Day-to-day operations, dispatch, or customer service
  • Vendor management or contractor oversight
  • Ongoing fractional CMO/COO retainer (separately scoped if desired)

05 · Assumptions

What We're Assuming Going In.

This scope, timeline, and price are built on the following assumptions. If any prove materially false, scope and timeline will be re-discussed in writing before continuing.

Assumption A · Single Operating Brand

All operations under review fall under Wendal Enterprise Inc. and its operating brand Unique Tours and Rentals Ltd. No additional sister entities, joint ventures, or franchise relationships are reviewed in this engagement.

Assumption B · Single Physical Location Today

Today's operating footprint is a single Providenciales, TC location (Bay Road, Blue Hills). West Palm Beach and Atlanta are future locations, not currently operating.

Assumption C · English-Language Documentation

All deliverables are produced in US English. Translation is out of scope.

Assumption D · Read-Only System Access

Client provides read-only or shared-screen access to relevant systems. Consultant does not require, and will not request, admin/edit credentials.

Assumption E · Confidentiality Both Ways

All findings, screenshots, financials, and customer data shared during the engagement are treated as confidential under standard professional-services norms. A separate Mutual NDA is available on request and can be executed prior to kickoff at Client's option.

06 · Change Control & Acceptance

How We Handle Changes Mid-Engagement.

Because the audit is fixed-fee and fixed-window, changes to scope mid-engagement require a written change order signed by both parties. Minor clarifications or re-orderings within the existing scope do not require a change order.

Acceptance criteria: The engagement is considered complete when (a) all six deliverables listed in Module Six are delivered to the Client's specified email address, and (b) the 60-minute walkthrough call has been held (or formally waived in writing by Client). The 14-day clarification window begins on the later of those two events.
