01 · Why This, Why Now

You're Not Buying An Audit. You're Buying Confidence Before You Triple Your Footprint.

Wendal, what you've built in Turks and Caicos is real. Unique Tours and Rentals is a Travellers' Choice operator with a 4.7★ rating, ~158 reviews on TripAdvisor, signature horseback-and-swim, jet-ski, and safari snorkel experiences, and you're on GetYourGuide as a verified supplier. That's a credible foundation.

But a credible operation in one location is not the same business as a multi-location, US-and-Caribbean tour-and-rental brand. You're about to find that out. The systems that got you here, the booking flow, the dispatch logic, the staff communication, the reservation-to-revenue cash cycle, the way tour photos and follow-ups are handled, were built around one location, one team, one set of vehicles, one set of horses. They will not survive contact with West Palm Beach and Atlanta without intentional re-architecture.

Strategic Context

The U.S. is already ~81% of Turks and Caicos' source market. 2025 saw ~640K stayover arrivals plus ~1.3M cruise passengers, and Atlanta-PLS has consistent direct lift on Delta and Frontier (≈3 hr flight time). Your existing customer base is already American. Opening West Palm Beach and Atlanta isn't an unrelated expansion, it's building a customer-acquisition funnel on the front end of a vacation that ends at your dock in Providenciales. That changes the economics. It also changes the operational complexity. The audit is about making sure you walk into that with eyes open.

02 · What The Audit Does

Five Lenses. One Business. No Blind Spots.

The Business Discovery Audit is a structured, fixed-scope engagement. We look at the same five layers in the same order every time, because that's how operators get clean comparisons across locations, partners, and time. No tool sales. No upsell wrapped in a deliverable. Just an honest map.

01

Workflow Map

Booking → confirmation → dispatch → on-site execution → post-tour follow-up → review request → repeat-customer flow. Documented step-by-step.

02

Systems & Tech Stack

Reservation engine, payments, CRM, communications, fleet/horse management, photo delivery, OTA integrations (GetYourGuide, Viator), website + analytics.

03

Bottleneck Diagnostic

Where time, money, attention, and trust are leaking: staffing constraints, manual handoffs, double-entry, refund disputes, no-shows, comms gaps.

04

Pain Points & Risks

Owner-dependent processes, single points of failure, safety/liability exposure, brand-reputation risks, OTA-dependency risk, FX/cash-cycle exposure.

05

Competitive & Market

TC competitor benchmark (Hydro Charters, Mako, Caribbean Cruisin', Five Cays, Floe Lux, Flyboard) + West Palm and Atlanta market entry analysis.

06

30 / 90 / 6mo / 12mo Roadmap

What to fix this month. What to build this quarter. What to launch in West Palm by month 6. What Atlanta + the multi-location operating model looks like at month 12.
