How The Clock Actually Works.

The 72-hour clock starts at Hour 0, defined as the moment both of the following are true: (1) the $2,500 USD Square invoice is paid and funds have cleared, and (2) the Client Intake Worksheet has been returned with required information. If those happen at different times, the clock starts at the later of the two. From Hour 0, the engagement runs across four phases, Intake, Diagnostic, Build, and Deliver, each with explicit milestones and explicit owners.

If a client-side action is delayed, the timeline extends 1:1 by the duration of the delay. Consultant-side delays do not extend the timeline, they are absorbed within the fixed fee.

Timeline Risks & What Happens

What Could Slow This Down.

Risk · Client-Side Delay

If intake or system access is late

  • Hour 0 doesn't start until both payment and intake are received
  • If system access is delayed past Hour 4, the entire timeline shifts 1:1 by the duration of the delay
  • Consultant will notify Client in writing of any timeline shift within 1 business hour of the delay being known
Risk · Mid-Engagement Scope Change

If new questions or asks come up

  • Items that fit inside the existing six modules are absorbed without scope change
  • Items outside the existing scope (e.g., financial modeling, full feasibility study, brand redesign) require a written change order
  • Change orders may extend the 72-hour window or add fixed fees; both parties must agree in writing
Risk · Walkthrough Call Conflict

If Wendal can't take the call inside Hour 60 to 72

  • Deliverables are still sent at Hour 64 to 66 as planned
  • Walkthrough call is rescheduled to a mutually-available time within 5 business days
  • Engagement is considered delivered at the time of file delivery; the walkthrough is considered a separately-scheduled event
