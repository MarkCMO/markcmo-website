Section 03 / The Blueprint

The Lead-Gen System

Seven interlocking engines. Each one feeds the others. The paid engines buy leads today; the organic engines make ROC un-outspendable in twelve months.

Engine 01

The Review Engine

Automated SMS and email review requests at every job close, QR cards on every invoice and yard sign, crews trained to ask at the handshake, fast public responses to every review. Target a sustained 8 to 15 new 5-star reviews per month. Recency beats a rival's stale total.

Engine 02

The GBP Network

One fully optimized Google Business Profile per county: correct primary category, complete services, weekly geotagged job photos, weekly posts, seeded Q&A. Legitimate location coverage to win on proximity. No fake pins, ever.

Engine 03

Local Service Ads + Paid Search

LSA and Google Guaranteed live per county for day-one, badge-backed leads, plus non-brand Google Search once landing pages convert, plus geo-fenced Meta lead ads. This is what hits 20 to 30 leads per month while SEO matures.

Engine 04

Site + Conversion Layer

Fix the credibility flags, put license and certifications site-wide, add a live review widget, real before-and-after gallery, schema markup, fast mobile load, and the five missing material pages.

Engine 05

The Local Content Machine

Rewrite the 15 thin city pages into genuinely unique local pages, expand to top cities across all three counties, and restart the blog as Florida topic clusters (insurance claims, HB 715 straps, the 15-year roof rule, cost guides). Wins organic and AI-search citations.

Engine 06

The Storm-Response Kit

Pre-built geo-fenced Meta creatives, reserved LSA budget headroom, and door-knock scripts ready to fire within 24 to 48 hours of any named-storm landfall (June to November). Florida demand spikes reward whoever moves first.

Engine 07

The Authority Layer

Pursue an elite manufacturer certification (GAF Master Elite or Owens Corning Platinum) plus citations from chambers, FRSA, NRCA, BBB, and manufacturer locators. Trust badge, backlink, and a directory listing in one move.
