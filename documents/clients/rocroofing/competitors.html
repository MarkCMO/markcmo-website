Section 03 / The Paid Layer

Who Is Buying The Top Of The Page

Above the Map Pack sit Local Service Ads (Google Guaranteed) and Search ads. This is where challengers leapfrog the review-volume leaders, and where ROC can buy visibility on day one.

  • Confirmed LSA / Google Guaranteed in-market: Thomas Roofing (running ads in Deltona at 592 reviews), plus Daytona players Integrity Roof Services and Mr Roofr.
  • Florida roofing cost per lead: $45 to $120 on LSA (storm spikes $150 to $250+), $110 to $228 on Google Search, $30 to $80 on Meta.
  • The lever: LSA leads convert ~31% vs ~12% for traditional PPC, and sit above the entire organic pack. ROC currently runs none.
  • Certification gap: the leaders carry GAF Master Elite or Owens Corning Platinum. ROC holds standard GAF only, no elite tier.
Section 04 / The Opening

Why ROC Can Win This

The core insight

The giants only own their home-pin cities and broad "Orlando" terms. Janney's 2,700 reviews do not appear in the Lake Mary or Oviedo packs, which are won at 18 to 81 reviews. Seven of twelve city packs have a slot ROC can take this year. ROC is a top-4% Florida contractor by permit volume; the capacity is proven. The only gap is marketing.

Gap 1: Reviews

ROC has 46. The soft suburb packs are won at 17 to 87. A sustained 8 to 15 new reviews a month puts ROC in the pack across its core within 2 to 3 quarters, no multi-year climb required.

Gap 2: The legacy operators are digitally quiet

60-year shops like Bert Faircloth and Leonard Clark have deep local trust but thin Google footprints. ROC can out-market them quickly and take their search share.

Gap 3: No elite certification

Every leader carries GAF Master Elite or Owens Corning Platinum. ROC pursuing one closes a trust gap and unlocks a backlink + locator listing.

Gap 4: Zero paid presence

Competitors buy the top of the page with LSA. ROC running LSA captures leads above the entire pack from day one while the review moat compounds.

Gap 5: Dormant content + no social

ROC's blog died in late 2025 and there is no Instagram, LinkedIn, or YouTube. The content + social engines are wide open to reclaim.

Gap 6: Proximity is winnable per metro

Because the giants are anchored to their home cities, legitimate per-county presence lets ROC compete on proximity where they cannot.

The Bottom Line

The Field Is More Beatable Than It Looks

ROC does the roofs at top-4% volume. The companies beating it online are better marketers, not better roofers. Reviews, an elite cert, content, and paid ads close the gap, and seven city packs are open right now.
