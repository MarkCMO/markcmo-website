## CALL SCRIPT (cold call or returned call)

**Opener (first 15 seconds, earn the next 30):**
"Chuck, this is Mark with MarkCMO. I am not selling you roofing software and this is not a robocall. I spent time studying ROC Roofing specifically and found something I think you will want to see. Ninety seconds and you can tell me to get lost. Fair?"

**The hook:**
"Here is the thing. ROC is a serious operation, 154 permits, A+ BBB, top 4% of Florida contractors. The roofing is not the problem. But online, ROC has 46 Google reviews, and right now a company with 17 reviews is sitting in the Deltona top-3 map pack, in your own backyard. Lake Mary is being won with 18, Titusville with one single review. I pulled twelve cities across your three counties and seven of them have a slot ROC could take this year."

**The reframe (handle the "we get business from referrals" objection):**
"That referral business is great and it is exactly why this works. You already do the hard part, the roofs. The companies beating you online are not better roofers, they are just better at asking for reviews and running a couple of ads. That is a marketing fix, and it is faster than you would think. The gap is reviews, two ad channels, and some cleanup, not years."

**The ask:**
"I built a one-page scoreboard with the real numbers and a plan to get ROC to 20 to 30 roof leads a month. Can I grab 20 minutes this week to walk you through it? No charge, no obligation. If it is not useful you have lost 20 minutes."

**If yes:** "Great, what is better, mornings or afternoons? I will send a calendar link and the one-page brief so you can see it before we talk."

**If "send me something first":** "Done. I will email a one-page brief and a short proposal. Read the first page, that is the whole story. I will follow up Thursday."

**If "what does it cost":** "Depends how fast you want to grow. There are three lanes, from a single-county proof of concept up to full market domination. The middle one is built to hit the 20 to 30 leads number and runs a management fee plus an ad budget that is billed at cost. I will show you all three on the call so you pick the one that fits your goal. Most important thing first is whether the opportunity is real, and the scoreboard answers that in two minutes."

**Common objections:**
- "I already tried marketing / got burned by Angi." -> "I hear that constantly. Angi resells the same lead to 8 contractors, that is why it feels like a treadmill. This is the opposite, it builds assets you own: your reviews, your map rankings, your site. Let me show you the difference."
- "I'm too busy." -> "That is exactly the point, you are busy because you are good. This runs in the background and feeds you better leads, it does not add to your plate. Twenty minutes is all I need."
- "Send me an email." -> use the "send me something first" path above, then the email sequence.
