2.Term

The initial term is twelve (12) months beginning on the Effective Date below, comprising a 6-week Foundation Build Sprint followed by the ongoing managed program. After the initial term, the Agreement continues month to month and either party may end it with 30 days written notice. The Foundation Build is a one-time charge and is non-refundable once work begins.

3.Scope of Services

Foundation Build Sprint (weeks 1 to 6): Google Business Profile network setup and optimization per county, review-generation system, website conversion and credibility fixes, schema markup, five material and service pages, Local Service Ads setup and verification, call tracking and lead attribution, and the pre-built storm-response kit.

Ongoing Managed Program (months 1 to 12): paid media management (Local Service Ads, and at Growth and above, Google Search and Meta), local SEO and content production, unique city landing pages, ongoing review-engine operation, per-county GBP management, monthly reporting and strategy call, and storm-response readiness. Exact inclusions follow the selected tier.

4.Fees, Marketing Fuel, and the Scale-Up Schedule

Fees are split into two separate buckets so Client always sees where every dollar goes: the management retainer (Provider's fee) and the marketing fuel (ad spend, billed at cost). For the selected Launch-to-Scale structure, the management retainer starts low and increases automatically as the program delivers qualified leads.

How the scale-up works (binding)

The management retainer adjusts the month after a lead threshold is reached and is sticky: once a stage is achieved it does not step down for a single slow month (it adjusts down only after two consecutive months below the prior stage's floor). When the program reaches the Scale stage (30+ qualified leads per month for two consecutive months), the retainer holds at $10,500 per month and the $7,000 balance of the engagement fee becomes due, bringing the one-time engagement fee to the standard $12,000. A "qualified lead" is a new prospective customer delivered through a program-tracked channel (Local Service Ads, Google or Meta ads, program landing-page forms, or tracked Google Business Profile calls and messages), counted on the shared monthly dashboard. Lead counts are measured on Provider's delivery, independent of Client's close rate.

Beyond Scale: shared upside on overage volume

The schedule above is sized for an anticipated maximum of about 30 qualified leads and roughly 8 booked jobs per month. When the program exceeds that anticipated volume, the additional upside is shared: in any month at the Scale stage, for each program-sourced job beyond the 8th in that month, Provider earns a performance fee of 8% of that job's collected revenue, or a flat $1,000 per job, whichever Client prefers, in addition to the $10,500 monthly retainer. The performance fee is invoiced monthly on collected revenue (after Client is paid) and only on new, program-sourced jobs tracked on the shared dashboard. The parties further acknowledge that for volume sustained materially above the Scale stage (for example 40 or more qualified leads per month), the monthly retainer and ad-spend management scope may increase by mutual written agreement to reflect the added work at that volume. Either party may propose, at the quarterly review, converting the overage share into a higher fixed retainer.

Marketing fuel is required for the schedule to work

Marketing fuel is the ad budget paid to Google and Meta to buy the leads. It is separate from the management retainer, billed at cost with no markup, and funded by Client from day one at a minimum of $3,500 per month (scaling up in hurricane season). The lead targets that drive the scale-up assume the fuel is funded at or above this minimum; underfunding the fuel reduces lead volume and shifts the timeline. Target: enough lead volume in the first 60 to 90 days to reach the Ramp and Growth stages.

Fixed-fee alternatives. In place of Launch-to-Scale, Client may instead select a fixed tier: Foothold ($9,000 engagement + $6,000/mo), Growth ($12,000 + $10,500/mo), or Enterprise ($18,000 + $15,000/mo), each with marketing fuel separate and at cost.

5.Client Responsibilities

  • Provide timely access to the Google Business Profile, website, and FL license and insurance documents needed for Local Service Ads verification.
  • Respond to leads quickly. The fastest responder usually wins the roof, and lead-to-job results depend on Client follow-up speed.
  • Run the review system in the field: crews ask at job completion, and Client supports the review request cadence.
  • Approve marketing fuel budgets, storm-season surges, and any new location decisions.
  • Provide job-site photos and project details for content and gallery use.

6.Performance Expectations

Lead targets are good-faith estimates based on the live June 2026 competitive scoreboard and Florida roofing benchmarks. Provider commits to the work, the system, and the spend discipline. Provider does not guarantee specific search rankings, a specific number of leads, or a specific number of booked jobs, because Google ranking, ad auctions, storm timing, and Client follow-up speed are outside Provider control. Results compound over the term as the review and organic moat builds and blended cost per lead falls.

7.Confidentiality and Ownership

Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential. Marketing assets, content, and accounts created for Client (website pages, GBP, ad accounts, review profiles) belong to Client and remain with Client at the end of the engagement. Provider may reference the engagement and anonymized results as a case study unless Client requests otherwise in writing.
