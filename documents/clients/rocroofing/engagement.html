2.Term

The initial term is twelve (12) months beginning on the Effective Date below, comprising a 6-week Foundation Build Sprint followed by the ongoing managed program. After the initial term, the Agreement continues month to month and either party may end it with 30 days written notice. The Foundation Build is a one-time charge and is non-refundable once work begins.

3.Scope of Services

Foundation Build Sprint (weeks 1 to 6): Google Business Profile network setup and optimization per county, review-generation system, website conversion and credibility fixes, schema markup, five material and service pages, Local Service Ads setup and verification, call tracking and lead attribution, and the pre-built storm-response kit.

Ongoing Managed Program (months 1 to 12): paid media management (Local Service Ads, and at Growth and above, Google Search and Meta), local SEO and content production, unique city landing pages, ongoing review-engine operation, per-county GBP management, monthly reporting and strategy call, and storm-response readiness. Exact inclusions follow the selected tier.

How marketing fuel works

Marketing Fuel is the advertising budget paid to Google and Meta to buy leads. It is billed to Client at cost with no markup and is fully separate from the management retainer. Fuel may be paid by Client directly on Client billing accounts, or advanced by Provider and reimbursed. Storm-season surges are deployed only with Client approval.

5.Client Responsibilities

  • Provide timely access to the Google Business Profile, website, and FL license and insurance documents needed for Local Service Ads verification.
  • Respond to leads quickly. The fastest responder usually wins the roof, and lead-to-job results depend on Client follow-up speed.
  • Run the review system in the field: crews ask at job completion, and Client supports the review request cadence.
  • Approve marketing fuel budgets, storm-season surges, and any new location decisions.
  • Provide job-site photos and project details for content and gallery use.

6.Performance Expectations

Lead targets are good-faith estimates based on the live June 2026 competitive scoreboard and Florida roofing benchmarks. Provider commits to the work, the system, and the spend discipline. Provider does not guarantee specific search rankings, a specific number of leads, or a specific number of booked jobs, because Google ranking, ad auctions, storm timing, and Client follow-up speed are outside Provider control. Results compound over the term as the review and organic moat builds and blended cost per lead falls.

7.Confidentiality and Ownership

Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential. Marketing assets, content, and accounts created for Client (website pages, GBP, ad accounts, review profiles) belong to Client and remain with Client at the end of the engagement. Provider may reference the engagement and anonymized results as a case study unless Client requests otherwise in writing.
