Questions

What operators usually ask

What size of business do you fund?

We focus on US businesses doing roughly 5 million dollars and up in annual revenue across manufacturing, distribution and warehousing, logistics, construction, and multi-unit restaurant groups. Funding amounts are tailored to your situation.

How fast can you move?

Faster than a bank. Many situations move from first call to real terms in days, and to funding shortly after, depending on the structure and documentation.

Is this a bank loan or a cash advance?

Neither. We provide customized corporate debt and private credit, structured around your business. It is not a one-size-fits-all product and it is not a predatory advance.

What do I need for the first call?

Nothing formal. The 15-minute diagnostic is a conversation about your business and your need. If it makes sense to go further, we will tell you exactly what is required.

Can you replace or work alongside my current lender?

Yes. We regularly replace senior lines that no longer fit, or structure capital that sits alongside existing debt.
