Game Plan

Your ramp to 20 to 30 calls a month, reverse engineered.

This document does one thing: it starts from the number of calls you want and works backward to the activity that produces them, then shows how that activity ramps up over the first 90 days. The technical build, the deliverability engineering, and the sequencing logic are ours to run; what follows is the part that matters to you, the numbers and the timeline.

4. The Ramp: How It Increases Over 90 Days

Cold outreach is not a switch. The first weeks build and warm the system, then volume and calls climb steadily to full. Here is the increase, week by week.

Why month one is build, not calls

The first 30 days are setup and warming, so the calls ramp in starting late month one to early month two and reach the full 20 to 30 a month by Day 90. That is the correct, durable way to do this; pushing for volume on day one is what gets a program flagged and killed. It is also why the monthly retainer does not start until the system goes live (around Day 30): you pay the build cost to begin, and the monthly only kicks in once outreach is actually running.

6. What the Outreach Feels Like

You asked that the emails be professional, substantive, and land in the inbox, not look like the spam every lender blasts. That is exactly the standard we hold. Without getting into our methods, here is what a prospect experiences:

The experience

  • A short, relevant first email that reads like a real person wrote it, about their specific capital need.
  • A professional signature with your name, title, direct line, and a one-click way to book.
  • As they engage, more substance: a funding playbook, a relevant case, your materials, delivered cleanly.
  • The same person showing up thoughtfully on LinkedIn and by phone, in sync, never spammy.

Two ways to say yes

  • Diagnostic Call (15 min): the easy first yes, a quick read on their capital position and fit.
  • Funding Assessment Call (30 min): the deeper conversation for engaged prospects, options and structure.
  • Both route to your calendar; a phone number is always there for those who would rather call.
  • The system pays the most attention to the prospects showing the most interest.
The standard, in one line

Professional, relevant, inbox-first outreach that earns the right to send your branded materials, timed to when a prospect is actually looking. The engineering that makes that happen reliably is what you are hiring us to own.
