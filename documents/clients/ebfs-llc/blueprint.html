Outbound Blueprint

Why a built-right outbound engine books 20 to 30 calls a month.

This is the case and the math: the gap in the market, exactly who we target, how we time the outreach to buying intent, and the conservative funnel that turns a monthly list into 20 to 30 qualified calls. The technical build and the deliverability engineering are ours to run; what follows is the strategy and the numbers behind your results.

The Mandate

Book 20 to 30 qualified calls a month for Executive Business Funding Solutions, feeding Terry Stavropoulos' goal of 20 closes a month, by reaching US businesses doing 5M dollars and up in revenue in manufacturing, distribution, warehousing, logistics, construction, and large restaurant groups (15 to 25 locations), on liquidity and senior-lender-replacement needs, across email, LinkedIn, and phone, timed to buying intent. Real estate is excluded. The whole engine is built in your name and owned by you.

1. Market & Competitive Landscape

Commercial lending is one of the most heavily prospected categories in B2B. Owners and CFOs of 5M-dollar-plus companies get pitched constantly by cash-advance shops, equipment-finance brokers, factoring companies, and bank relationship managers. The inbox is crowded, and most of it is low quality and untargeted. That is the problem, and it is also the opening.

The White Space

The credentialed end of the market, flexible corporate debt and private credit, exactly what EBFS does, runs almost no systematic cold outbound. The high-volume end runs nothing but spam. There is an open lane for a credible, well-targeted, multi-touch program that reaches the right operator about liquidity or replacing a senior lender at the moment they are looking, and books a call.

2. Who We Target

We target the company first, then the right people inside it, on a focused list, not a mass blast.

Company filters

  • US-based, annual revenue 5M dollars and up
  • Manufacturing
  • Distribution & warehousing / inventory-based
  • Logistics & transportation
  • Construction
  • Large restaurant groups (15 to 25 locations)
  • Real estate excluded (enough deal flow already)

Decision-makers & pain points

Titles: Owner / CEO / CFO, the people who own the capital decision.

What we lead with:

  • Liquidity needs (working capital, inventory, equipment, expansion)
  • Senior-lender replacement (a bank line that no longer fits)

3. Timed to Buying Intent

The biggest reason most cold outreach fails is timing: it reaches people who are not in the market. We solve that by leaning on your own intelligence stack to reach prospects when they are actually looking for capital, not at random.

  • Firmographic and contact data to build a clean, accurate target list.
  • Intent signals that flag which companies are researching financing and which are heating up over time.
  • Engagement signals from the outreach itself, so the prospects showing the most interest get the most attention.

Reaching the right operator with a relevant message the week they are looking is what turns a cold list into booked calls. The competitors blasting static lists never do this.

5. What Makes This Different

  • It reads like a person, not a blast. The cash-advance shops send obvious mass mail. Ours opens like a real note from a principal with a real phone number.
  • Value before the ask. A funding playbook and a relevant case, not "apply now." The first call is a low-pressure diagnostic, not a pitch.
  • Timed to intent. We reach out when the signals say they are looking, so the message is relevant the day it arrives.
  • Multi-channel and coordinated. Email, LinkedIn, and phone working the same prospect in sync.
  • Engineered for the primary inbox. The thing competitors get wrong. Built right so your mail is seen, not buried in spam.
  • Owned by you. The entire engine is built in EBFS' name. If we ever part ways, it stays with you.
