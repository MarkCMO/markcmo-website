90-Day Build & Launch Roadmap

From signature to a calendar full of calls, in 90 days.

Cold outreach is not a switch you flip. Domains have to warm. Lists have to be built and validated. Copy has to be written and tested. This roadmap sequences all of it so the first sends go out clean and the program is at full volume, booking 20 to 30 calls a month, by Day 90.

The shape of the 90 days

Weeks 1 to 2: stand up the infrastructure and start warmup. Weeks 2 to 4: build and validate the first lists, write the sequences. Weeks 4 to 6: soft launch as warmup completes. Weeks 6 to 12: ramp to full volume and optimize into the 20 to 30 call range.

Phase Plan

Phase 0 · Kickoff
Day 0
Everything we need to start building, captured in one 90-minute session.
  • Kickoff with Terry: confirm ICP filters (manufacturing, distribution, warehousing, logistics, construction, large restaurant groups; real estate excluded), target titles (owner / CEO / CFO), and the intent sources.
  • Confirm access to your data and intent sources (ZoomInfo, Bombora, Prospero) and your calendar.
  • Capture EBFS positioning, the liquidity and senior-lender-replacement angles, proof points, and any flyers / promotional assets for the branded touches.
  • Confirm the calendar / booking tool and where booked calls hand off to your close process.
  • Agree on suppression rules (existing clients, do-not-contact, active deals).
Phase 1 · Infrastructure
Days 1 to 14
The sending backbone goes up and starts warming on day one. This is the long pole, so it starts first.
  • Stand up your outreach infrastructure, built in your name and owned by you.
  • Warmup starts immediately (the 3 to 4 week clock begins now). This is what earns the system its place in the inbox later.
  • Connect your data and intent sources, your calendar, and the CRM.
  • Stand up the reporting dashboard so you can see the funnel from day one.
Phase 2 · List & Copy
Days 10 to 30
While domains warm, we build the ammunition: intent-scored validated lists and tested sequences.
  • Build and validate the first target lists so outreach starts clean.
  • Write the full multi-channel sequence and create your prospect materials (funding playbook, case pieces, professional signature).
  • Wire the booking links and the behavior-based follow-up so interested prospects self-schedule and the hottest get fast attention.
  • Set up reply handling and routing so booked calls reach you cleanly.
Phase 3 · Soft Launch
Days 28 to 45
First real sends as warmup completes. Low volume, watch everything.
  • Begin sending at reduced volume as each domain clears warmup.
  • Monitor deliverability, bounce, open, and reply closely. Hold bounce under 2 percent.
  • Launch the parallel LinkedIn motion and intent-triggered cold-call tasks on the same contacts.
  • First booked calls start landing. Tune copy, ICP, and intent thresholds against live reply data.
Phase 4 · Ramp & Optimize
Days 45 to 90
Scale to full volume and settle into the 20 to 30 call range.
  • Ramp to full daily send volume.
  • Double down on the winning subject lines, angles, segments, and intent thresholds. Cut what underperforms.
  • Expand list pulls to keep ~800 fresh validated contacts (intent-scored) flowing each week.
  • Weekly reporting, monthly optimization review. Steady-state 20 to 30 booked calls a month.

A note on month one expectations

Because warmup runs 3 to 4 weeks, the first full sends land around week 5. That means the first 30 days are build, not booked calls. Calls begin in the back half of month one to early month two, and climb into the 20 to 30 range as volume reaches full and copy is tuned to live data. This is normal and it is the right way to do it. Rushing warmup is how programs die. It is also why the monthly retainer does not start until the engine goes live around Day 30: you pay the at-signing build cost to start, and the monthly only begins when sending is actually running.
