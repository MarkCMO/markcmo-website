How It Works

The Whole System, In Plain English

No jargon. Each piece does one simple job, and together they turn the right strangers into booked calls and funded deals. Here is the flow, start to finish.

1

Find

We build a clean list of the right companies and the decision-makers inside them.

2

Reach

We contact them by email, LinkedIn, and phone with a message about their real need, timed to when they are looking for capital.

3

Land

Interested prospects land on your professional page that proves EBFS is real and the right fit.

4

Book

They book a quick call onto your calendar, a 15-minute diagnostic, then a funding assessment.

5

Close

You take the call and fund the deal. We report the numbers and keep improving.

The growth add-ons (SEO, Google Ads, and retargeting) simply pour more of the right people into the top of this same flow.
