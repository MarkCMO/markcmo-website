The add-ons in detail

1. Credibility Landing Page INCLUDED IN BUILD

What it is: A professional, conversion-built web page on a dedicated domain that all of your outreach and ads point to. Not a brochure, a page engineered to turn an interested click into a booked call.

Why it matters: The moment a 5M-dollar-plus owner or CFO is interested, they look you up. If they find a thin or missing site, the deal cools instantly. A sharp, credible page validates EBFS in about ten seconds, answers "is this real and right for me," and converts curiosity into a call. It is the single biggest multiplier on every other channel: outbound, SEO, PPC, and retargeting all convert better when they land somewhere credible.

What is included: messaging and positioning, design and copy, the Diagnostic and Funding Assessment call CTAs, the funding-playbook capture, SEO foundations, mobile optimization, and a lender-appropriate compliance footer, all on a domain you own.

What to expect: live at launch, and a measurably higher booking rate from every click you send it.

2. Organic SEO ~$1,500 to $2,500 / MO

What it is: Optimizing your site and publishing content so you rank in Google for the searches your buyers actually make: "working capital for manufacturers," "inventory financing," "replace business line of credit," and dozens more, by industry and use case.

Why it matters: Outbound reaches the people you choose. SEO captures the people already searching for funding, the highest-intent leads there are, and they come to you. And it compounds: rankings you earn this quarter keep producing leads next year at almost no marginal cost, steadily lowering your blended cost per booked call. It is the long-term asset that makes EBFS less dependent on paid channels over time.

What is included: keyword and competitor research, on-page and technical SEO, a content plan (industry and use-case pages, guides), authority and link building, and monthly reporting on rankings, traffic, and leads.

What to expect: a deliberate build. Meaningful organic traffic and inbound usually arrive in 6 to 12 months and accelerate from there. This is a moat, not a quick fix.

3. PPC / Google Ads ~$1,000 to $1,500 / MO + AD SPEND

What it is: Paid search ads that put EBFS at the top of Google the instant someone searches for funding, with the landing page and tracking wired to convert those clicks.

Why it matters: This is the fastest way to turn on high-intent inbound. While SEO is still building, PPC buys you the same searchers today. You only appear for people actively looking for capital, you control the volume by adjusting spend, and every dollar is measurable, you see cost per click, cost per booked call, and can scale exactly what works.

What is included: campaign strategy, keyword and negative-keyword build, ad copy, conversion tracking, landing-page alignment, bid and budget management, and ongoing optimization with monthly reporting. Ad spend (a recommended starting budget of roughly $2,000 to $5,000 a month) is billed to you directly at cost, no markup.

What to expect: leads within weeks of launch. It is an ongoing cost (you pay per click), and it works best alongside SEO so that paid demand gradually gives way to free organic demand.

4. Retargeting ~$500 to $750 / MO + AD SPEND

What it is: Inexpensive display and LinkedIn ads that follow the people who visited your landing page but did not book, keeping EBFS in front of them across the web and LinkedIn.

Why it matters: Most visitors, even genuinely interested ones, do not act on the first visit. Retargeting recovers those near-misses for pennies and reminds them to come back and book. Because you already paid (through outbound, PPC, or SEO) to get them to the page, retargeting squeezes more booked calls out of traffic you already bought, usually the cheapest leads in the entire system.

What is included: audience setup, ad creative, platform configuration (Google display plus LinkedIn), frequency management, and reporting. Ad spend (a small budget, roughly $300 to $800 a month) is billed at cost.

What to expect: it works as soon as traffic starts flowing, runs on a small budget, and is one of the most efficient add-ons, which is why we recommend turning it on early.

How to sequence growth (our recommendation)

1. Outbound + landing page first. The engine creates the calls, the landing page makes you credible. This is the engagement.

2. Add retargeting early. It is cheap and recovers clicks you already paid for through outbound. Easy win.

3. Layer PPC when you want more volume fast. Paid search turns on high-intent inbound in weeks, on top of outbound.

4. Build SEO in parallel for the long game. It is slower but compounds, and over time it lowers your blended cost per booked call. Start once the landing page is live.
