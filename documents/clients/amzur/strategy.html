AMZ-GTM-001 · Go-to-Market & Channel Strategy

You have channels. What you do not have is a strategy or an owner.

The audit explained why the site underperforms. This explains the bigger problem behind it: marketing is being executed tactically with no one above it owning the push to market, reverse-engineering from revenue, and deciding what to double down on. Here is the channel strategy, the structure, and the operating model that fixes it.

The Root Cause

Six Symptoms, One Disease

The audit found six things wrong with the digital footprint. They are real, but they are symptoms. The disease underneath is simpler and more expensive: no one owns the go-to-market. You have a capable team in India producing content, running Google Ads and LinkedIn, sending some cold email. That is execution. What is missing is the layer above it, the strategist who sets the plan, reverse-engineers the numbers from your revenue goals, decides which channels and services to bet on, kills what is not working, and is personally accountable for pipeline.

The Core Finding

A tactical team without a strategic owner produces motion, not pipeline.

Your team is doing the things. Posting, running ads, sending email. But activity is not strategy. Nobody is asking the hard questions: which campaigns actually produced a closed deal last quarter, which service line has the best return on ad spend, why are we spending attention on staffing when leadership wants to grow Solution Engineering and the AI-first story, and where should the next dollar go. When no one owns those answers, every channel runs at half power and the budget leaks.

That is the role you are actually hiring for. Not another set of hands. The owner of the push to market.

Here is the layer that is missing, drawn out:

Layer 1 · Present

Execution

The India team. Content, social, ad operations, email sends. Capable hands doing the work.

Layer 2 · Missing

Strategy & Ownership

The plan, the channel bets, the budget decisions, the accountability for pipeline. The reverse-engineering from revenue back to daily activity.

This is the gap
Layer 3 · Present

Leadership Goals

You want qualified leads and revenue growth, especially in the high-margin services. Clear destination, no map to it.

Layers 1 and 3 exist. Layer 2 is empty, so the goals at the top never translate into the right work at the bottom. That is why effort has not turned into revenue.

The Method

Find What Works. Double Down. Cut The Rest.

Strategy is not a guess. It is a loop. Before adding a single new dollar of spend, the first job is to instrument everything and find out what is actually producing pipeline today, because something is, and right now no one can tell you what. Then the budget moves toward the winners and away from the waste. This is the discipline the engagement runs on.

1

Instrument

Proper tracking and attribution across every channel, tied to real pipeline, not clicks. Most of this is dark today.

2

Find the winners

Which channels, campaigns, keywords, and segments produced actual opportunities and closed deals. The signal is in your accounts, unread.

3

Double down

Reallocate budget and effort to the proven winners. Scale what converts, by service line and by margin, not by volume.

4

Cut the waste

Kill the campaigns and keywords burning spend with nothing to show. The savings fund the winners.

This is the honest part: I cannot tell you today which Amzur campaigns are winners, because the data lives in your ad and analytics accounts and I have not seen it yet. Getting that access is step one of the engagement, and the reason the information request matters. The framework below is how each channel gets run once we know.

Channel By Channel

The Strategy & Structure For Each Channel

Each channel has a job. Run together with a strategy on top, they compound. Run in isolation as tasks, they leak. Here is the role, the structure, and how we find and scale the winners for each.

Google Ads / Paid Search

Capture high intent
Strategic role

Catch buyers at the moment they are searching with intent, NetSuite implementation, custom software development, managed services, IT staffing. This is your highest-intent, fastest-converting channel and it should be pointed at your highest-margin services, not the cheapest clicks.

How it should be structured

Structured by service line and buyer intent, pointed at the high-margin services, and measured to real pipeline. The detailed build is part of the engagement.

Find and double down

Pull the search-term and conversion report, find the keywords and campaigns that produced actual opportunities, shift budget to them, and cut the terms spending with no pipeline. High-intent service terms get scaled. Broad, cheap, low-intent traffic gets cut.

LinkedIn Ads & ABM

Create demand + land target accounts
Strategic role

Google captures people already searching. LinkedIn creates demand and lands the named accounts that fit your best-fit profile, and it is the right home for the India Dedicated Teams model and the larger Solution Engineering deals that do not have obvious search demand. This is where you build the pipeline that Google cannot find.

How it should be structured

Built on a defined ideal-customer profile and a named target-account list, aimed at decision-makers in the best-margin segments, and run in concert with outbound so touches reinforce. The detailed build is part of the engagement.

Find and double down

Identify which industries, account tiers, and creative actually generate meetings and opportunities, then concentrate budget on those segments and retire the audiences that only generate cheap leads that never close. Quality of account over volume of leads.

Cold Email & Outbound

Proactive pipeline
Strategic role

Your current 500 to 1000 sends a month is a toe in the water, not a system. Done right, outbound is the most controllable pipeline source you have, you decide exactly which accounts to pursue rather than waiting to be found. It is also the natural channel to sell the India Dedicated Teams model directly to the people who would buy it.

How it should be structured

Run as a deliverability-safe system against the same target accounts as ABM, with multi-touch sequences tied to each prospect's real problem and engaged prospects routed to a person fast. The detailed build is part of the engagement.

Find and double down

Measure reply, meeting, and opportunity rates by segment and message, double down on the lists and angles that book meetings, and scale send volume only as the inbox-placement and reply data earn it. Scale what books calls, drop what gets ignored.

Organic, AI Search & Retargeting

Lower cost + recover demand
Strategic role

The connective tissue. Organic and AI-search visibility (the audit's SEO and GEO work) lowers your blended cost per lead over time and feeds the top of every other channel. Retargeting recovers the buyers who visited but did not convert, which in a long B2B sales cycle is most of them.

How it should be structured

Organic and AI-search visibility built around the winnable, high-intent terms from the audit, with retargeting sequenced to the buying stage so slow-cycle prospects are recovered and every channel compounds the others. The detailed build is part of the engagement.

Find and double down

Track which content and terms assist pipeline, invest in the clusters that pull qualified traffic, and weight retargeting toward the pages and segments that actually re-engage and convert.

PR, Earned Media & Digital Footprint

Build authority
Strategic role

PR and earned media plus a deliberately expanded online digital footprint that build credibility and surface Amzur where buyers and AI engines look, planned by me and executed by your 9-person team at my direction.

Find and double down

Double down on the placements and footprint that actually drive authority and inbound interest.

Tie It To Revenue

Lead With The Growth Engine, Not The Cash Base

Your brief is explicit: Solution Engineering is the primary growth engine, USA Staffing is the largest revenue line today, and the whole company is being rebuilt AI-first. Marketing effort should follow that intent, not default to whatever is easiest to sell. Staffing is the cash base and does not need the marketing budget. The spend and the story belong on Solution Engineering and the AI-first identity, with the dedicated-team model and NetSuite as strong supporting plays. Look at the split you shared: the two lines you most want to grow, Solution Engineering and NetSuite, are about 4 percent of revenue each today, while staffing is 75 percent. That is not a problem, it is the opportunity. The growth priorities are tiny because marketing has never been pointed at them. Point it there, and there is enormous room to run. This is the kind of call that only gets made when someone owns the strategy, because left to tactical execution, effort flows to the biggest line, not the one leadership wants to grow.

The Operating Model

Someone Owns It. The Team Executes It.

The fix for "no one owns the push to market" is not to replace your team. It is to put a strategic owner above them. I set the plan, make the channel and budget calls, run the find-and-double-down loop, and carry the number. Your India team becomes the execution engine for a real strategy instead of a tactical unit guessing at priorities. Clear lines, one owner.

Mark owns (the missing layer)

  • The go-to-market strategy and channel plan
  • Budget allocation across channels and service lines
  • The reverse-engineering from revenue to activity
  • The find-what-works and double-down decisions
  • Attribution, reporting, and accountability for pipeline
  • ICP and target-account definition for ABM
  • Channel structure and the priority service mix

The India team executes

  • Content and creative production against the brief
  • Ad operations and campaign build to the structure
  • Email sends and sequence operation
  • Reporting inputs and data hygiene
  • Design and landing-page production
  • Day-to-day channel management to plan
The Difference

Tactical output becomes strategic output

Same team, completely different result. Today they produce activity with no one deciding whether it is the right activity. With a strategic owner setting direction and holding the number, that same capacity gets pointed at the channels, services, and accounts that actually move revenue. You are not buying more hands. You are buying the brain that tells the hands what matters.
