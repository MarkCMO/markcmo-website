AMZ-SOW-001 · Scope of Work

Two phases. Fix the floor, then build the authority.

Phase One repairs the technical floor and consolidates the architecture so every page can compete. Phase Two builds the authority and AI visibility that gets Amzur ranked and cited. Every work item below maps back to a finding in the audit.

How This Engagement Works

Strategic Oversight, Plus Hands-On Execution

I run this as a short, high-impact engagement, not an open-ended retainer. I do the forensic work, set the strategy, and either execute directly or direct your India marketing team against a clear plan so they shift from tactical output to strategic output. You always see the numbers. Nothing is a black box.

The work splits into two phases. Phase One is finite and front-loaded: the technical and architecture repairs that unlock everything else. Phase Two is the ongoing growth engine. You can stop after Phase One with a materially healthier site, or continue into the authority and AI-visibility work where the compounding pipeline lives.

Phase One · Weeks 1 to 12

Technical Floor & Architecture Repair

Finite, front-loaded. Makes every page able to compete.
✓

CDN and Core Web Vitals remediation

Cloudflare in front of the site, edge caching and Brotli on, render path cleaned up, CSS and JavaScript consolidated and deferred, unused Elementor add-ons removed.

Maps to Finding 1 (Critical)
✓

Homepage and on-page signal fix

Single keyword-driven H1, corrected heading hierarchy, title and meta alignment on the priority pages, slogan demoted to styled design.

Maps to Finding 2 (High)
✓

Service-page standardization

Roll the winning NetSuite-page pattern (depth, schema, FAQ, clean structure) across the other money pages, then wire internal links from the blog into them.

Maps to Finding 3 (Strength)
✓

URL architecture consolidation

Resolve the /industry/ versus /industries/ split, 301 the orphans, consolidate the cluster, fix internal links, and audit for other refocus URLs leaking equity.

Maps to Finding 4 (High)
✓

Technical hygiene and schema rollout

Fix the robots.txt sitemap directive, validate indexation, and roll out Service, Breadcrumb, and Organization schema sitewide.

Maps to Finding 5 (Medium)
✓

Baseline and keyword strategy

Full backlink and Search Console pull to set the baseline, map the 15 to 30 winnable high-intent terms, and identify the page-2 queries closest to breaking onto page 1.

Quantifies the audit's open items
Phase Two · Ongoing

Authority & AI Visibility

The compounding growth engine, organic plus AI search.
✓

Generative Engine Optimization (AI search)

Place and optimize Amzur on the exact sources AI engines cite (G2, Clutch, Goodfirms, ERP Research, SoftwareConnect, the official Oracle NetSuite partner directory), pursue top-partner listicle inclusion, and restructure key pages for clean machine extraction.

Maps to Finding 6 (Critical)
✓

Authority and backlink building

Targeted earned links and citations against the competitor authority gap, focused on the terms and clusters where Amzur can realistically win.

Closes the Gate 3 leak
✓

Topical content clusters

Hub-and-spoke content around the winnable terms, built on the standardized page pattern, directing your India team's output against a strategic brief instead of scattered tactics.

Builds Gate 2 depth
✓

Marketing the hard-to-position services

A positioning and demand approach for the high-margin team-engagement model that you said is the most profitable and the hardest to market, so the work also feeds the revenue you most want to grow.

Ties SEO to your margin goals
