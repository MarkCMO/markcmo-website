AMZ-SOW-001 · Scope of Work

The plan. Foundation first, then the demand engine.

The Foundation Sprint sets strategy, positioning, and the instrumented base, fixing the technical floor and standing up the first demand-engine plays. The ongoing phase runs and scales the engine across every channel. Every work item maps to a finding in the audit or to one of the six leadership areas your brief asked for.

How This Engagement Works

Strategy I Own, Execution Through Your Team

I own the strategy layer and the push to market, and I direct your India marketing team against a clear plan so they shift from tactical output to strategic output, upskilled into an AI-augmented function as we go. You always see the numbers. Nothing is a black box. This is the engine your brief asked for, built around the capacity you already have.

The work splits into two phases. The Foundation Sprint is finite and front-loaded: strategy, ICP, and positioning, the technical and architecture fixes that unlock everything else, channel instrumentation, and the first demand-engine plays. The ongoing phase runs and scales the engine to a measurable pipeline over the 6 to 12 month horizon, covering all six leadership areas from your brief.

Foundation Sprint · Weeks 1 to 12

Strategy, Floor & First Plays

Finite, front-loaded. Sets the strategy and unlocks every channel.

GTM strategy, ICP, and AI-first positioning

Define the ideal customer profile for the US mid-market, sharpen the positioning around your real, provable AI-first identity, and set the repeatable go-to-market strategy. This is the strategy layer your brief said has never existed, and everything else hangs off it.

Your ask 1: GTM strategy & positioning

CDN and Core Web Vitals remediation

Cloudflare in front of the site, edge caching and Brotli on, render path cleaned up, CSS and JavaScript consolidated and deferred, unused Elementor add-ons removed.

Maps to Finding 1 (Critical)

Homepage and on-page signal fix

Single keyword-driven H1, corrected heading hierarchy, title and meta alignment on the priority pages, slogan demoted to styled design.

Maps to Finding 2 (High)

Service-page standardization

Roll the winning NetSuite-page pattern (depth, schema, FAQ, clean structure) across the other money pages, then wire internal links from the blog into them.

Maps to Finding 3 (Strength)

URL architecture consolidation

Resolve the /industry/ versus /industries/ split, 301 the orphans, consolidate the cluster, fix internal links, and audit for other refocus URLs leaking equity.

Maps to Finding 4 (High)

Technical hygiene and schema rollout

Fix the robots.txt sitemap directive, validate indexation, and roll out Service, Breadcrumb, and Organization schema sitewide.

Maps to Finding 5 (Medium)

Baseline and keyword strategy

Full backlink and Search Console pull to set the baseline, map the 15 to 30 winnable high-intent terms, and identify the page-2 queries closest to breaking onto page 1.

Quantifies the audit's open items

Channel instrumentation and attribution

Proper tracking and attribution across Google Ads, LinkedIn, and outbound, tied to real pipeline rather than clicks, plus offline conversion import from the CRM. This is the audit that finally answers which channels and campaigns actually produce opportunities, the foundation for doubling down.

Powers the find-what-works method (Strategy)
Phase Two · Ongoing

Channels, Authority & AI Visibility

The compounding growth engine: paid channels, organic, and AI search, run on the double-down loop.

Paid channel strategy and budget reallocation

Run the per-channel plan from the strategy doc: Google Ads structured by service line and intent, LinkedIn and ABM against the target-account list, outbound scaled on real deliverability, retargeting to recover demand. Reallocate budget to the proven winners, cut the waste, and weight spend toward the high-margin services and the team-engagement model, not staffing.

Runs the Go-to-Market & Channel Strategy

Generative Engine Optimization (AI search)

Place and optimize Amzur on the exact sources AI engines cite (G2, Clutch, Goodfirms, ERP Research, SoftwareConnect, the official Oracle NetSuite partner directory), pursue top-partner listicle inclusion, and restructure key pages for clean machine extraction.

Maps to Finding 6 (Critical)

Authority and backlink building

Targeted earned links and citations against the competitor authority gap, focused on the terms and clusters where Amzur can realistically win.

Closes the Gate 3 leak

Topical content clusters

Hub-and-spoke content around the winnable terms, built on the standardized page pattern, directing your India team's output against a strategic brief instead of scattered tactics.

Builds Gate 2 depth

Marketing the hard-to-position services

A positioning and demand approach for the high-margin team-engagement model that you said is the most profitable and the hardest to market, so the work also feeds the revenue you most want to grow.

Ties SEO to your margin goals
