AMZ-TML-001 · Engagement Timeline

What lands, and when you will see it.

From the day you send access to the ongoing growth phase. Milestones at Day 14, 30, 60, and 90, including the early wins you can put in front of the board. Week numbers start from kickoff.

At A Glance

Two Phases, One Continuous Build

Phase One · Weeks 1 to 12

Technical Floor & Architecture

Finite and front-loaded. Fix what blocks every page, then set the keyword and authority baseline.

Phase Two · Month 4 onward

Authority & AI Visibility

The compounding engine. Rankings, citations, AI-answer presence, and content against your margin goals.

The Schedule

Milestone By Milestone

Pre-Kickoff · Now

Discovery and access

You send Search Console, analytics, ad accounts, WordPress and DNS access, plus the marketing and tech-stack detail I requested. This unblocks the real numbers behind the audit's two open items.

Week 0 · Working Session

Findings walkthrough and sign-off

45-minute session. We walk the quantified audit, confirm the 15 to 30 priority terms and the services to push, and lock the plan. You sign and we set the kickoff date.

Week 1 to 2

Technical sprint begins

CDN in front of the site, caching and compression on, render-path cleanup started. Homepage H1 and on-page signal fixes shipped. Robots.txt and crawl hygiene corrected.

Milestone · Day 14
End of Week 2

Core Web Vitals moving, baseline set

Mobile load measurably improved with before-and-after numbers, and the full backlink and Search Console baseline is delivered so every later result is measured against a fixed starting line.

Milestone · Day 30
End of Month 1

Architecture consolidated, first GEO placements live

URL split resolved with 301s in place, service pages standardized on the winning pattern, schema rolling out. First directory and review placements (G2, Clutch, Goodfirms, NetSuite partner directory) submitted, the first screenshottable AI-visibility step.

Milestone · Day 60
End of Month 2

Page-2 terms moving, citations landing

The page-2 queries closest to the fold start climbing, directory and listicle placements are live and indexed, and content clusters are in production against the priority terms.

Milestone · Day 90
End of Month 3

Phase One closed, AI answers shifting

Technical floor and architecture done, healthier site across the board, and the first measurable change in what AI engines say about your category. Decision point: continue into the full Phase Two growth engine or pause with a materially stronger foundation.

Month 4 onward

Authority and AI-visibility phase

Ongoing earned links and citations, expanded GEO presence, topical content clusters, and a positioning push on the high-margin team-engagement model. Reported monthly against rankings, citations, AI-answer presence, and pipeline-relevant terms.

Honest Note On Timing

Technical and AI-visibility wins show up fast, often inside 30 to 60 days, because they do not depend on Google re-crawling and re-ranking the whole site. Ranking gains on competitive terms compound over months, not weeks. Anyone promising page-1 for hard keywords in 30 days is selling you something. The sequence here is deliberate: bank the fast, visible wins early to build confidence, while the slower authority work compounds underneath.
