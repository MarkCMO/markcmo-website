Month 4 to 12

Scale to a measurable pipeline

The ongoing fractional-CMO phase: run and scale the demand engine across paid channels, ABM, authority, and AI visibility, with the India team upskilled to execute it. A positioning push on Solution Engineering and the dedicated-team model. Reported monthly against pipeline and conversion quality, the functioning, measurable pipeline your brief set as the 6 to 12 month goal.

Honest Note On Timing

Technical and AI-visibility wins show up fast, often inside 30 to 60 days, because they do not depend on Google re-crawling and re-ranking the whole site. Ranking gains on competitive terms compound over months, not weeks. Anyone promising page-1 for hard keywords in 30 days is selling you something. The sequence here is deliberate: bank the fast, visible wins early to build confidence, while the slower authority work compounds underneath.
