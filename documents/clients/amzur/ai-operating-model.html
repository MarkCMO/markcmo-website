The Principle

AI Amplifies Expertise. It Does Not Replace It.

You wrote that AI should be embedded in how you deliver, not bolted on as a buzzword, and that you hold a strong principle against inflated claims. I work the same way, and I will say plainly where the line is. AI is only as good as the subject-matter expertise pointing it. Hand a model a vague brief and you get confident, generic, often wrong output. Hand it a sharp strategy and real constraints and it becomes a force multiplier. The judgment stays human. The leverage is AI.

  • Forensic audits and research at speed and depth

This is not a traditional playbook with a few tools added. The execution layer is rebuilt around agents and automation, which is why a forensic audit that would take a traditional consultant weeks took days, and why the marketing function I build for you can run at a scale your current headcount could not reach on its own.

Where AI Does Not Belong

The honest boundary

Your principle against inflated claims is also mine, and AI makes overclaiming easier, so the discipline matters more, not less. Strategy, taste, truth, and the number stay with a human.
