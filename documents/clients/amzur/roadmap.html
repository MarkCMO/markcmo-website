AMZ-RMP-001 · National Growth Roadmap

Found and trusted in every US city. The 24-month plan.

A 3, 6, 9, 12, and 24 month roadmap to make Amzur the most-found, most-cited, and most-trusted provider of its services in every state and city in the country, built on the team, tools, and budget you have today. Grounded in the real numbers you shared, and honest about what those resources can and cannot do.

The Finding That Reorders Everything

You have no sales team and no sales follow-up

You named it yourself under what has not worked: no sales follow-up, no sales team. That is why the funnel collapses from 10% meetings to a 1% close. Until that is fixed, spending more to generate leads just fills a leaking bucket faster. So the roadmap fixes the follow-through motion in the first 90 days, before it pours national volume into the top. Coverage without conversion is vanity. We build conversion first.

The North Star

The Destination, Stated Honestly

24-Month Goal

When a US buyer searches or asks an AI for your services in their city, Amzur is the answer.

Across Solution Engineering, NetSuite, Cloud, and Extended Teams, in all 50 states and the cities that matter, Amzur becomes the provider that gets found, gets cited, and gets trusted, proven with real case work like the 10-year Khameleon partnership, never with inflated claims. An honest word on the word "best": you do not buy national dominance at a $1,500 monthly ad budget, and no one becomes number one everywhere in 24 months against firms like BairesDev and RSM. You earn category leadership by being the most useful and most present, built on programmatic content, AI-search visibility, and targeted outbound, the channels that scale with your team and tools rather than your ad spend.

The Mechanism

How You Cover Every State And City

Paid ads at $1,500 a month cannot reach every city, and at $50 to $200 a click they should not try. National coverage is won organically, with a programmatic engine your 9-person team and AI toolkit can actually produce at scale: a genuinely useful page for each service in each market, engineered to rank and to be cited by AI answer engines, backed by outbound and ABM for the high-value deals search cannot capture.

The Roadmap

Five Horizons, One Compounding Engine

Months 0 to 3 · Foundation

Fix the bucket before filling it

Fix conversion and follow-through, set strategy and positioning, repair the technical and SEO floor, and prove the coverage engine in a pilot.

Key moves

  • Stand up the missing sales follow-up motion: SLAs, sequences, and an SDR-style cadence in RevUp and Freshworks so no qualified lead goes dark
  • Lock the ICP ($10M to $200M B2B in Retail, Healthcare, Manufacturing, IT/SaaS) and the AI-first positioning around real, provable strengths
  • Fix the technical floor and reverse the NetSuite URL/SEO damage from the restructure (per the Audit)
  • Instrument the full funnel in GA, Clarity, and the CRMs so every later number is measured honestly
  • Launch the programmatic pilot: top 10 to 15 metros for Solution Engineering and NetSuite, and submit the GEO directory placements
Target
15 to 20 qualified leads/mo and a working follow-up motion (no more lead leakage)
Owner / resources
Mark sets strategy; the 9-person team plus AI tools execute; sales motion built with leadership
Coverage: 10 to 15 metros, 2 services
Months 3 to 6 · Beachhead

Win the priority services in the top markets

Scale the proven pilot across the top US metros for the two growth services, and turn on ABM and outbound for the high-value deals.

Key moves

  • Expand programmatic location pages to the top 50 to 75 US metros for Solution Engineering and NetSuite, on the standardized, AI-assisted page pattern
  • Launch ABM against named ICP accounts (LinkedIn Sales Navigator plus outbound on amzurtech.com and amzurconsulting.com), aimed at the Extended Teams and larger Solution Engineering deals
  • Rebuild outbound on real deliverability and tighter Apollo lists, lifting reply rate off the current 1 to 2%
  • Fix or formally kill the dead channels (content syndication, untargeted email), and reallocate that effort to what converts
  • Stand up the case-study flywheel: Khameleon, Ashley Stewart, iTPM, IFB, the Public Health agency, as proof assets, not just logos
Target
Qualified leads holding 20+/mo with rising quality; first pipeline attributable to programmatic and ABM
Owner / resources
Team runs production to brief; Mark owns channel strategy, budget, and the double-down calls
Coverage: top 50 to 75 metros, 2 services
Months 6 to 9 · Expansion

All four services, every state

Extend coverage to all four service lines and all 50 states, and deepen AI-search citation so Amzur appears in the answer, not just the results.

Key moves

  • Roll the location engine out to all four service lines and across all 50 states plus secondary cities
  • Deepen GEO: get cited in AI answers for service-plus-city queries through directories, listicles, reviews, and structured, extractable content
  • Build vertical hubs for the four ICP industries (Retail, Healthcare, Manufacturing, IT/SaaS) with industry proof and language
  • Pursue the AI-platform and Oracle/NetSuite partnerships you are already chasing, as credibility anchors
  • Tighten marketing-to-sales handoff with scoring and routing so the higher volume still converts
Target
Organic and AI-sourced share of leads rising toward parity with paid; cost per qualified lead falling
Owner / resources
AI-augmented team at full production; Mark on strategy, partnerships, and attribution
Coverage: 50 states, all 4 services
Months 9 to 12 · Coverage

National presence, measurable pipeline

Complete national coverage, hit the trusted-partner positioning goal, and run a measurable, predictable pipeline.

Key moves

  • Full national coverage of state and major-city pages across all four service lines, continuously refreshed
  • The AI-first, trusted-IT-partner narrative consistent across every page and channel, the positioning goal from your brief
  • Predictable demand engine: organic plus AI plus ABM plus outbound, reported monthly on pipeline and conversion quality, not vanity volume
  • The India team running the AI-augmented marketing function with reduced dependence on outside direction
Target
A functioning, measurable pipeline (your 12-month brief goal); reduced reliance on high-CPC paid

Coverage: national, all 4 services
Months 12 to 24 · Category Leadership

The default answer, and a moat

Move from present everywhere to preferred, build defensible advantages, and own the verticals where Amzur genuinely wins.

Key moves

  • Extend to long-tail cities and become the cited default for service-plus-city queries in the markets that convert
  • Vertical dominance: be the recognized AI-first modernization and NetSuite partner in Retail, Healthcare, Manufacturing, and IT/SaaS
  • Build a moat: proprietary data and benchmarks, original research, and a thought-leadership engine competitors cannot easily copy
  • Productize the Extended Teams story (the Khameleon-style 38% cost reduction outcome) into a repeatable, marketed offer that grows the high-margin recurring line
  • Compounding flywheel: every win becomes proof, every proof feeds content and AI citations, lowering cost and raising trust
Target

Owner / resources
Mark as fractional CMO on strategy and moat; mature AI-augmented team running the machine
Coverage: national + vertical category leadership
The Honest Caveat

This is the path, not a guarantee

Rankings and AI citations compound over months, and they move at Google and the model-makers' pace, not ours. The 24-month destination is realistic with consistent execution and the sales motion fixed early. What I will not do is promise number-one-everywhere on a timeline no one can honestly hold, that is the inflated claim your brief warned against. The roadmap front-loads the fast, provable wins (conversion, technical, pilot coverage, AI placements) so you see real movement inside the first two quarters while the national engine compounds underneath.
