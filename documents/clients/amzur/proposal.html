AMZ-PRO-001 · Engagement Proposal

A short, high-impact engagement. Not a retainer treadmill.

Two ways to engage. Both start by fixing the technical floor and consolidating the architecture. One stops there with a healthier site. The other carries through into the authority and AI-visibility work where pipeline compounds.

How I Work

Forensic First, Then Build

I am a fractional CMO who leads with organic lead generation and forensic digital audits, then reverse-engineers the plan from your revenue goals back to the work. I bring both strategic oversight and hands-on execution, and I keep engagements short and high-impact rather than billing you forever. Where it makes sense, I direct your India marketing team against the plan so they convert from tactical output to strategic output you can measure.

The Math

One recovered deal pays for the engagement

Amzur's higher-margin software, managed-services, and ERP work carries deal values that dwarf this fee. The engagement does not need to manufacture dozens of wins to pay back. It needs to move a handful of high-intent terms onto page 1, get you cited where buyers now research, and convert traffic the slow site is currently losing. One or two recovered engagements covers the entire investment, and the technical and architecture work keeps paying long after the engagement ends.
