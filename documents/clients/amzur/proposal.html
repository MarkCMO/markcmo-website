The Math

Senior marketing leadership without the full-time cost

A senior B2B marketing leader who could own strategy, build a demand engine, and run an AI-augmented team would cost well into six figures fully loaded, and would still take months to hire. A fractional engagement gives you that leadership now, at a fraction of the cost, with the execution leverage of your existing team. Set against Amzur's deal sizes in Solution Engineering, NetSuite, and the dedicated-team model, a single additional engagement won per quarter covers the investment many times over.
