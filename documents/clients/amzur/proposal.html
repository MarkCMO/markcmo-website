The marketing leadership your brief asked for. Owned, not outsourced.

A fractional CMO engagement where I lead and direct the strategy, and your existing 9-person marketing team plus a marketing manager execute it. I am the marketing leadership above the team you already have, not an outsourced build shop. A 90-day foundation to set strategy, ICP, and positioning, then ongoing leadership to a measurable pipeline over 6 to 12 months. Here is what it covers, the model, and the terms.

How I Work

Own The Strategy Layer. Build The Engine.

Your brief asked for a marketing leader to own strategy, positioning, and demand generation, working with the execution capacity you already have. That is exactly the engagement: I own the US-market strategy and the push to market, set the plan and the priorities, and direct your 9-person team and a marketing manager to execute it, upskilling them into an AI-augmented marketing function as we go. I lead and direct rather than running a separate build shop. It starts with a deeper discovery scope that maps what is already in place and where the real opportunities are, grounded in honest positioning from real strengths.

What It Covers

Your Six Asks, Owned End To End

The engagement maps directly to the six areas of leadership your brief asked for. I own all six, with your team as the execution engine underneath.

  • Go-to-market strategy & positioning, ICP definition and an AI-first identity sharpened around real strengths
  • Demand generation engine, a measurable, repeatable pipeline motion built from the right mix of outbound, inbound, ABM, and partnerships, scoped to what discovery surfaces
  • Brand & messaging, real engineering and AI capability translated into a credible, honestly-positioned narrative
  • Marketing-sales alignment & enablement, connecting output to the sales motion and building the enablement assets and feedback loop that discovery identifies as gaps
  • Team leadership & capability build, directing and upskilling your India team into an AI-augmented marketing function
  • Metrics & accountability, pipeline and conversion quality over vanity volume, on a reporting cadence you can hold me to

On top of the six, I plan and direct a PR and earned-media play and an expanded online digital footprint to build authority and demand, all executed by your 9-person team at my direction.

The Model

The Engagement, Plainly Priced

I provide the marketing leadership and direction across all six areas, plus the PR and digital-footprint play, and your 9-person team and a marketing manager execute. A one-time engagement fee covers the first 90 days of strategy, ICP, positioning, and standing up the engine, then a monthly retainer for ongoing leadership over the 6 to 12 month horizon.

The Math

Senior marketing leadership without the full-time cost

A full-time CMO who could own strategy and lead an AI-augmented team at this level would cost $250,000 to $400,000 or more fully loaded, and would still take months to hire. This engagement gives you that leadership now, at a fraction of the cost, leading the 9-person team you already have rather than adding headcount. Set against Amzur's deal sizes in Solution Engineering, NetSuite, and the dedicated-team model, a single additional engagement won per quarter covers the investment many times over. The flat line is the expensive part. This changes it.
