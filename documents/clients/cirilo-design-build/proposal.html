The Opportunity in Charlotte

Charlotte's luxury pool market has two competitor archetypes and one open seat.

The volume archetype (Anthony & Sylvan, CPC Pools, Blue Haven, Premier Pools & Spas) wins on machine-marketing. Big ad budgets, national playbook, commodity feel. They convert the mid-tier customer who shops on price.

The credentialed archetype (Lombardo, Aloha, Tidal, Charlotte Pool Co.) wins on prestige. Master Pools Guild, AquaTech, Genesis CBP. They convert the wealthy buyer who finds them through a referral. They have almost no marketing presence.

What is missing is a Charlotte builder who pairs genuine design-led luxury craft with a modern marketing presence. That seat is open. Cirilo's positioning ("Luxury Design. Precision Build.") is already aimed at it. The brand exists. The digital footprint that earns it does not.

THE THESIS

Cirilo can credibly occupy "design-led modern luxury with a modern marketing presence" inside 9 months because no Charlotte builder currently does. The opportunity is taking, not creating.

The Strategy

Three moves in sequence. Each unlocks the next.

Move 1. Build the trust scaffolding (Days 0 to 60)

Reviews, Google Business Profile, service-area pages, financing, warranty, analytics, video portfolio. No paid spend converts in the $150K+ pool category until the buyer can verify the business is real, credentialed, and respected. This is the part most marketing firms skip. We start here.

Move 2. Capture demand the premium tier ignores (Days 30 to 120)

Google Local Service Ads, long-tail geo Search on suburb terms (Waxhaw, Marvin, Myers Park, Lake Norman, Davidson), Houzz Pro, and a Meta video creative engine. Lombardo, Aloha, Tidal, and Charlotte Pool Co. do not bid on any of these surfaces. The volume tier does, but on commodity head terms. We take the high-intent long-tail.

Move 3. Open the referral channel nobody is working (Days 60 to 180)

A formal partnership program with Charlotte's top 5 luxury custom-home builders, top 10 luxury real estate teams, top 6 landscape architects, and select country clubs. The standard builder referral fee is 2.5 to 3 percent of project value. On a $250K build that is $6,250 to a partner, materially cheaper than acquiring the same customer cold. No Charlotte pool builder runs this systematically.

Why Tier 2 Is the Recommendation

Tier 1 funds the trust scaffolding but lacks the partnership program and the content engine that produce compounding returns past Month 6. Tier 3 makes sense if Cirilo has capital and wants to compress 18 months of category leadership into 9, which is genuinely possible but is not the safer path on a first engagement.

Tier 2 funds all three strategic moves. The trust scaffolding ships in 90 days. The partnership program turns on in Month 3. The content engine compounds from Month 4. Break-even sits at 27 signed pool contracts in Year 1, which is conservative against a realistic 22 to 32 outcome on a well-instrumented luxury operation in this market.

What Is "Systems Build" (And Why It Differs Per Tier)

The systems build is the one-time foundation we ship in the first 90 days. It is everything that has to exist before any monthly retainer or media spend converts at scale: tracking, trust signals, service-area pages, portfolio video, lead-capture infrastructure, partnership collateral. Each tier ships a strictly larger systems build because each tier turns on more channels that have prerequisite scaffolding. Below: what each tier delivers, and why the next tier costs more.

Included in every tier: the full operating dashboard

The systems build is not just the public website. It is a complete, custom-built operating platform that Cirilo owns outright:

  • Admin console for the business: CRM and lead pipeline, proposal builder with e-signature, invoicing and milestone draw billing, document vault, analytics and KPIs, and QuickBooks accounting sync.
  • Customer Owner Suite for each homeowner: live project status, progress photos, documents, draw payments by check or ACH, and a personal referral link.
  • Vendor portal for trades: post jobs out for bid, receive and compare vendor bids, award the job, schedule by build stage, and track vendor payments and lien waivers.

Customer, admin, and vendor all run from one connected dashboard. This is a full software build, not an off-the-shelf template.
