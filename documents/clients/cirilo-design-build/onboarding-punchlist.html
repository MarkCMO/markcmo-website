2 Brand and assets

The raw material we turn into pages, ads, and proof. High quality in, high quality out.

  • Logo files vector (SVG, EPS, or AI) and a high-res PNG if you have them
  • Photos of completed projects 15 to 30 of your best pools, outdoor spaces, and builds, highest resolution available
  • Any video walkthroughs, drone footage, before-and-afters, even phone clips
  • Brand colors and fonts if you have a brand guide or specific preferences; if not, we will guide it
  • Existing materials brochures, current proposals, anything you hand clients today

3 Access and accounts

So we can publish, advertise, and measure. If an account does not exist yet, just note that and we will create it in your name. You always own everything.

  • Domain access registrar login or DNS access for cirilodb.com (and the new domain when chosen)
  • Google Business Profile add us as a manager, or share the login
  • Google Analytics and Search Console access, or permission to create them
  • Meta Business (Facebook and Instagram) access to the business page and ad account
  • Existing ad accounts Google Ads or Meta Ads, if you have run anything before
  • Current leads and contacts any list or spreadsheet of past inquiries and clients to export

4 Business details

The inputs that make the targeting and the messaging accurate. A few quick answers on the kickoff call covers most of this.

  • Service area the towns and neighborhoods you most want to win work in
  • Top services to push the 3 you want more of, with rough price ranges for qualifying leads
  • Ideal client who your best projects come from, so we target more of them
  • Reviews and testimonials your 3 to 5 best, with permission to use names or initials
  • License, insurance, warranty NC license number, insured and bonded status, warranty terms, for trust signals
  • What makes Cirilo different in your words, why clients choose you over the other builder

  • Banking for invoicing we will collect this securely and directly, never by email or in this document
  • Current and upcoming projects so we can seed the dashboard with your real pipeline on day one
