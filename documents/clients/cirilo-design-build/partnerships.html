CHANNEL STRATEGY

The referral channel
nobody is working.

Target lists, email templates, activation calendar, and PR pitch angles for the partnership and earned-media program that no Charlotte pool builder is running systematically.

THE STRATEGIC PREMISE

Standard luxury builder referral fee is 2.5 to 3 percent of project value. On a $250K build, that is $6,250 to a partner. Materially cheaper than acquiring the same customer cold ($10,000 to $15,000 paid CPA). Done well, partnerships become Cirilo's largest single channel by Month 12, with the highest close rate and the lowest CPA.

Activation menu

CRITICAL RULE FOR CLUBS

Never cold-pitch a country club. Every activation must be member-introduced. The first 90 days of country club outreach is asking Tiffany's existing client list "who is your member sponsor at Carmel / Peninsula / Charlotte CC, and would they be willing to introduce us?" Without the introduction, the General Manager will not return a call.

The 5 PR pitch angles to rotate

  1. "The economics" angle: "What a $250,000 pool actually buys you in Charlotte" - data-led, transparent pricing breakdown. Earns trust, attracts buyers, irritates competitors who keep prices hidden.
  2. "The local builder" angle: Husband-wife founder story, family operation, design-first philosophy. Charlotte loves a local entrepreneur story.
  3. "The trend" angle: Wellness pools + cold plunge integration; vanishing edges on Lake Norman; minimalist modern pool design for tech-money buyers. Rides cultural waves.
  4. "The case study" angle: Single signature install profiled in detail - architecture, engineering, finishes, photography. Earns design press.
  5. "The expert" angle: Tiffany quoted in any home, garden, or real estate piece that touches outdoor living. Build the journalist Rolodex.

Big-Idea Activations (Tier 3 add-ons)

"Charlotte's Most Beautiful Backyards" Annual Awards + Coffee Table Book

Cirilo becomes the curator of luxury outdoor living in Charlotte. Annual nominations open in April, judged by a panel including the founders, a landscape architect, a local magazine editor. Winners featured in a 60-page hardcover coffee table book Cirilo produces and distributes free to partners, country club libraries, and luxury real estate offices. The book is the marketing asset. Cost: $35K to $60K Year 1, drops to $20K to $35K Year 2+. Returns: 30+ premium PR placements, brand equity Anthony & Sylvan cannot manufacture, recurring lead source from every other home featured in the book.

The "Build My Dream Pool" Interactive Lead Tool

Style quiz, feature picker, budget calculator, lead capture, automated follow-up. Pre-qualifies budget so Tiffany only books real consultations. Lifts lead quality 2 to 3x vs generic contact form. Differentiator vs every Charlotte competitor. Build cost: $25K to $35K one-time. Ongoing: $1K/mo. Promote via Meta + Pinterest + Google Display.

YouTube Design-Reveal Series

12 episodes per year, one per finished build, 8 to 12 min each. Cinematic, drone, dusk LED, owner reveal moment. Builds proprietary library that doesn't depend on ad spend, compounds in search, and serves as a sales tool inside consultations. $3K to $5K per episode production. Single biggest brand asset Cirilo can build.

The "Charlotte Pool Index" Annual Data Report

Cirilo publishes an annual data report on Charlotte luxury pool market: average cost per neighborhood, average build time, top finishes, top features, ROI on resale. Picked up by Charlotte Business Journal, Axios Charlotte, Realtor blogs. Positions Cirilo as the data authority in market. Cost: $8K to $15K to research + design. Returns: 10+ press placements, evergreen reference cited in every Charlotte pool article that year.

Wellness Pool + Cold Plunge Niche Ownership

Riding Andrew Huberman wellness trend. Cirilo positions as the only Charlotte builder doing integrated luxury cold plunge + gunite spa + pool. Single signature install with full photo + video documentation. PR push to wellness, fitness, biohacker media on top of Charlotte luxury press. Niche moat that takes 18 months for a competitor to copy.
