MODEL C · LONG-TAIL PARTNERSHIP

Lowest upfront,
longest commitment.

The smallest cheque to start. The largest monthly retainer to sustain. Best when Cirilo wants to preserve cash at launch and is committed to a 24-plus month engagement.

HOW MODEL C DIFFERS FROM MODEL A

Identical systems build scope, capabilities, marketing fuel, and Year 1 targets as Model A (Fixed-Fee Premium). The only differences: engagement fee is cut by 60 percent, systems build is cut by 50 to 58 percent, and monthly retainer is increased by 45 to 60 percent. Cirilo pays roughly $30 to $80 thousand less upfront in exchange for paying a larger retainer each month. Total Year 1 cost is $30 to $40 thousand higher than Model A, but cash flow at signing is dramatically easier.

HOW TO READ THE PRICING

Each tier shows two separate numbers. The headline is "Paid to Mark" – the engagement fee, systems build, and monthly retainer that go to MarkCMO / WETYR. The green "Marketing Fuel" block is ad spend and media production (Google, Meta, Houzz, video production) billed directly to Cirilo's card on each platform. Marketing fuel never passes through Mark.

The Three Tiers (Model C)

The trade-off is straightforward: cash flow relief at launch in exchange for a larger ongoing commitment. Best fit when Cirilo is allocating capital to other parts of the business (showroom, equipment, hires) and wants the marketing engagement to be predominantly an operating expense rather than a large initial outlay.

What's Identical to Model A

  • Systems build scope and capabilities (see Model A proposal for the per-tier breakdown of what's delivered).
  • Monthly media budget composition (Google Search + LSA + Meta + Pinterest + Houzz + videography production).
  • 12-month engagement duration, 90-day mutual kill clause, account ownership terms, reporting cadence.
  • Target signed contracts and Year 1 pool revenue projections.

When to Choose Model C

  • When Cirilo wants to preserve cash at signing (capital allocated to other business needs: showroom, equipment, deposits, crew).
  • When Cirilo is committed to a 24-month-plus relationship and prefers a higher operating expense over a large upfront capital outlay.
  • When Cirilo expects predictable monthly cash flow from existing operations to support the larger retainer.
  • When Cirilo wants zero rev-share exposure (unlike Model B, no variable cost per signed deal).

When NOT to Choose Model C

  • When Cirilo wants the lowest total Year 1 cost. Choose Model A.
  • When Cirilo prefers Mark's compensation tied to signed contracts. Choose Model B.
  • When Cirilo wants the option to terminate after Day 90 without large remaining commitment. Model C's commitment is heavily back-loaded into the monthly retainer.

Engagement Terms (Model C-Specific)

  • Duration: 12-month initial engagement, auto-renewing month-to-month after. Recommended commitment beyond Year 1 to amortize the lower upfront.
  • Engagement fee: Paid in full at signing. Non-refundable.
  • Systems build payment: 50% at Day 0 kickoff, 25% at Day 30, 25% at Day 60. Smaller absolute amounts than Model A.
  • Retainer payment: Net 5, invoiced first business day of each month. Larger amounts than Model A.
  • Media spend: Identical to Model A. Billed directly to Cirilo's card on each platform.
  • 90-day mutual kill clause: Same as Model A. Engagement fee non-refundable, remaining build milestones voided pro-rata.
  • Post-Year-1 commitment: Strongly suggested 24-month minimum. Cirilo is welcome to exit month-to-month after Day 365, but the value proposition of Model C is realized over a longer relationship.
