HOW TO READ THE PRICING

The green "Marketing Fuel" block is ad spend and media production (Google, Meta, Houzz, video production) billed directly to Cirilo's card on each platform. Marketing fuel never passes through Mark.

How Rev Share Is Tracked, Earned, and Paid

  • Earned event: Rev share earns the moment a pool contract is signed by the homeowner and a deposit clears via Square. No earn on leads, consults, proposals not signed, or cancelled contracts.
  • Profit basis: Calculated on the signed pool contract value at the 30 percent profit margin assumption stated in this proposal. Cirilo and Mark can true up to actual margin annually at the Year-end QBR if material variance.
  • Payment: Invoiced monthly, net 30 from invoice. Itemized list of signed contracts with Square invoice IDs accompanies each invoice. Cirilo retains 30-day audit window.
  • Verification: Mark's reporting dashboard pulls signed-contract events from the Square invoice ledger. Both parties see the same source of truth.
  • Attribution-agnostic: Rev share earns on every signed pool during the engagement period, not only marketing-attributed deals. This keeps the math simple and aligns incentives (referral pools also count).
  • Tail clause: If Model B engagement terminates, rev share continues for 90 days on signed contracts (because pipeline built during the engagement may close after termination).

When to Choose Model B

  • When Cirilo wants Mark's compensation directly tied to signed contracts, not just retainer.
  • When Cirilo wants downside protection: if results lag, Cirilo pays less than Model A.
  • When Cirilo is cash-constrained at launch and wants lower upfront / lower monthly.
  • When Cirilo expects to hit or beat target and is comfortable with Mark earning more if so.

When NOT to Choose Model B

  • When Cirilo prefers fully predictable cost (no variable rev share). Choose Model A.
  • When Cirilo wants to keep 100 percent of upside above plan. Choose Model A.
  • When Cirilo wants the lowest possible upfront and is willing to pay a larger monthly retainer instead. Choose Model C.
