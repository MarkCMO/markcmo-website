Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1 to 30)

WEEK 1
Kickoff + instrumentation
Owner: Mark + WETYR build team
  • 90-minute kickoff with Ramon and Tiffany: capture brand voice, project archive, partnership wishlist, sales process, CRM/intake state
  • Install GA4, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel, CallRail on Wix
  • Audit current Wix admin, request domain registrar access
  • Pull NC GC license number, COI, bond doc
  • Past-client list export (target 30 to 80 names)
End-of-week deliverable  Working analytics dashboard, kickoff brief, past-client list cleaned
WEEK 2
Trust stack + GBP launch
Owner: Mark + WETYR
  • Claim and verify Google Business Profile, populate 25+ photos, full service-area config, categories, booking link
  • Port Charlotte 704 or 980 tracking number; route to (910) line
  • Publish warranty page (10-year structural minimum), financing page (Lyon Financial application), license + bonded/insured page
  • Delete or noindex /copy-of-new-residential-construction orphan
End-of-week deliverable  GBP live, trust stack pages published, orphan URL killed
WEEK 3
On-page SEO + schema
Owner: WETYR
  • Rewrite title tags + meta descriptions on all 9 live pages with geo + service modifiers
  • Add schema.org LocalBusiness, Service, Product, FAQ, Review markup
  • Add OG and Twitter card tags
  • Image-sitemap addition
  • Rewrite About page with founder story, project count, year founded, certifications
End-of-week deliverable  All pages indexed correctly, link previews fixed, About relaunched
WEEK 4
Reviews ignition + intake screener
Owner: Mark + Tiffany
  • SMS + email outreach to past-client list with personalized review request and one-tap Google review link
  • Target 10 Google reviews by Day 30, 25 by Day 45, 40 by Day 60
  • Add screening questions to Wix Bookings consultation: property address, lot size, target budget, timeline
  • Begin Houzz Pro setup, request reviews from past clients on Houzz
End-of-week deliverable  Reviews flowing, calendar protected from tire-kickers, Houzz profile under construction

Phase 2: Acquisition (Days 31 to 90)

WEEKS 5 TO 8
Service area page build + portfolio reshoot
Owner: WETYR + photo/video team
  • 14 service area pages built with neighborhood case studies, local schema, embedded Google Maps, neighborhood photos: Charlotte, SouthPark, Myers Park, Ballantyne, Waxhaw, Weddington, Marvin, Davidson, Cornelius, Mooresville, Huntersville, Matthews, Tega Cay, Fort Mill SC
  • Drone + dusk LED + finish close-up + build-reel reshoot of 6 completed projects
  • Portfolio rebuilt: 14 detail pages with neighborhood, pool type, features, finish, equipment brands, budget tier, "Build me one like this" CTA
  • Houzz Pro Premium live with 40+ photos uploaded
End-of-sprint deliverable  14 service area pages indexed, 6 reshot projects published, Houzz live
WEEKS 9 TO 10
Paid acquisition turn-on
Owner: Mark + WETYR ads team
  • Google Local Service Ads + Google Guarantee enrollment (requires GC license, COI, background check)
  • Google Search campaign build: 40+ ad groups, 200+ keywords on long-tail geo + suburb terms (avoid head-term bidding war)
  • Meta video creative engine launch: first 6 cinematic reels (build films, drone reveals, founder-on-camera)
  • Pinterest setup: 30 boards organized by design style
  • Instagram launch: 12 portfolio posts queued
End-of-sprint deliverable  All paid channels live, first leads tracking in GA4
WEEKS 11 TO 12
Partnerships + PR ignition
Owner: Mark
  • Formal realtor referral program rolled out: co-branded one-pager, 3D site-fit rendering offer, agreement template, quarterly payout cadence
  • Outreach to top 10 luxury real estate teams (Dickens Mitchener, Cottingham Chalk, Allen Tate Luxury, Premier Sotheby's LKN)
  • Outreach to top 5 custom home builders (Arthur Rutenberg, Bonterra, Plattner, Grandfather Homes, Simonini)
  • First PR pitches to Charlotte Magazine, Charlotte Business Journal, LKN Magazine, QC Exclusive, SouthPark Magazine
  • File Genesis CBP and PHTA CBP applications
End-of-sprint deliverable  5 partner agreements signed, 5 PR pitches in market, 2 credential apps filed

Phase 3: Authority (Days 91 to 180)

MONTH 4
Content + video engine in production
Owner: WETYR content team
  • YouTube design-reveal series production starts: Episode 1 ("Inside a Lake Norman build")
  • Cornerstone content cadence: 2 long-form pieces per month (permit guide, $250K-pool breakdown, finishes catalog, equipment guide, LKN engineering, pool resale value)
  • "Build My Dream Pool" interactive tool development begins
  • Google Search budget scaled to $12K-$15K/mo as landing pages convert
  • First country club outreach (Carmel CC, Charlotte CC, The Peninsula at LKN)
End-of-month deliverable  YouTube Ep 1 live, 2 cornerstone pieces published, tool in design review
MONTH 5
PR placements + credential earn
Owner: Mark
  • Charlotte Business Journal founder profile pitched and confirmed
  • LKN Magazine outdoor living feature pitched
  • Genesis CBP and PHTA CBP exams scheduled
  • "Build My Dream Pool" tool launches
  • YouTube Episodes 2-3 shoot + edit
End-of-month deliverable  Tool live, 1 magazine feature confirmed, credentials in progress
MONTH 6
90-day mid-year review
Owner: Mark + Ramon + Tiffany
  • Full Year-Half QBR: P&L impact, deal velocity, channel mix, CPA/CPL, ROAS
  • Decision: scale budget on best-performing channels for H2
  • YouTube Episode 4 live
  • BBB accreditation, NARI membership filed
  • Best of Houzz 2027 submission prep
End-of-month deliverable  QBR deck, H2 budget reallocation, 8 to 12 signed contracts year-to-date

Phase 4: Dominance (Days 181 to 365)

MONTHS 7 TO 9
Compounding + brand expansion
Owner: Mark + WETYR
  • Scale Meta video budget as creative library deepens
  • Direct mail sequence 1-2-3 drops to affluent zip households (Tier 3 only)
  • YouTube Episodes 5-9 (cinematic build reveals, founder Q&A, design philosophy)
  • 6 more cornerstone content pieces (12 total)
  • Second tier channel partnerships activate: Sub-Zero/Wolf dealer co-marketing, RH Outdoor partnership, smart home brand
  • Genesis CBP and PHTA CBP earned, site updated
End-of-sprint deliverable  Top-3 organic locked on 6+ keywords, 15 to 25 qualified leads/month sustained
MONTHS 10 TO 12
Year 1 close + Year 2 plan
Owner: Mark + Ramon + Tiffany
  • Charlotte Magazine "Best Of" 2027 finalist push
  • "Charlotte's Most Beautiful Backyards" annual awards program launched
  • Wellness pool / cold-plunge niche content + PR push (Huberman trend)
  • YouTube Episodes 10-12 (year-end best-of, 2027 design trends, founder Q&A)
  • Year 1 final QBR + 2027 strategic plan
  • Tier upgrade / hold / step-down decision for Year 2
End-of-year deliverable  100 to 150 reviews at 4.9, 22 to 32 contracts signed, Year 2 plan locked
