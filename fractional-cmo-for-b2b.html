What does a fractional CMO do for B2B companies?

For B2B companies, a fractional CMO defines the ICP, builds the demand generation engine, creates the account-based marketing program, aligns marketing and sales on pipeline definitions, and installs the attribution system that proves marketing's revenue contribution. The output is measurable pipeline — not impressions, clicks, or MQLs that sales ignores.

How do you measure B2B marketing ROI?

Real B2B marketing ROI is measured in pipeline influenced, pipeline created, win rate on marketing-sourced deals, and CAC payback period. MQLs are a leading indicator at best — a lagging one if sales and marketing disagree on what qualifies. A fractional CMO installs a shared revenue model between marketing and sales, so both teams are accountable to the same pipeline number.

What B2B marketing channels produce the highest ROI?

The highest-ROI B2B channels depend entirely on your ICP, ACV, and sales motion. For enterprise (ACV above $50K), outbound ABM, strategic content, and in-person events typically outperform. For mid-market ($10K-$50K ACV), a combination of SEO, paid search, and sales-assisted outbound usually produces the best CAC. For PLG motions, product virality and bottom-up expansion beat traditional top-down outbound. A fractional CMO runs the diagnosis before committing budget to any channel.
