SEO Services: 2026 Pricing + What's Actually Included

Real SEO services in 2026 include 10 specific components. Below: the full inclusion list, honest pricing by tier ($1.5K-$30K/mo), top 9 providers reviewed, the GEO + AI search additions that most agencies still skip, and the operator-led model that bundles SEO with content, paid, and analytics.

The Short Answer

Real SEO services in 2026 are SEO + GEO. Traditional SEO targets Google rankings. GEO targets AI-engine citation (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews). Any agency selling SEO without GEO is operating 2022 playbooks. The bar is now SEO + GEO + structured data + entity authority - integrated.

The 10 Components of Real SEO Services

  1. Technical SEO audit + remediation. Crawlability, indexability, site speed (Core Web Vitals - LCP under 2.5s, INP under 200ms, CLS under 0.1).
  2. Keyword research + intent mapping. Stage and intent-aware. Commercial vs informational vs transactional queries.
  3. On-page optimization. Titles, meta descriptions, headers (H1-H6 hierarchy), internal linking, definition-lead content.
  4. Content production for SEO. SEO without strong content is dead. Quality > volume.
  5. Link acquisition. Digital PR, original research linkable assets, broken-link building, guest posting (still works if done right).
  6. Local SEO + GBP. For businesses with physical locations or service areas.
  7. Schema markup. FAQPage, HowTo, Article, Product, LocalBusiness, Organization, Person. Critical for rich results and AI extraction.
  8. Core Web Vitals optimization. Ranking signal + UX signal.
  9. GEO / AI search optimization. The 2026 differentiator. llms.txt, entity consistency, definition-lead content, statistical anchors, AI crawler permissions.
  10. Reporting against conversions + revenue. Not just traffic and rankings. Most agencies skip this.

The GEO Additions Most Agencies Skip

By 2026, SEO without GEO is incomplete. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) optimizes content for AI citation. Most SEO agencies are still on 2022 playbooks and miss these signals:

  • Definition-lead content. First sentence states what the topic is in a quotable format.
  • Quotable blocks. 2-3 sentence authoritative statements.
  • Statistical anchors. Specific numbers, percentages, dates.
  • Structured data. FAQPage, Article, HowTo, Person, Organization schema.
  • llms.txt compliance. Published llms.txt declaring AI-friendly content.
  • AI crawler permissions in robots.txt. GPTBot, ClaudeBot, anthropic-ai, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended explicitly allowed.
  • Entity consistency across the web. Same brand description on LinkedIn, Crunchbase, Wikipedia, your site.
  • Authorship + E-E-A-T. Named authors with credentials and sameAs links.
