Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a Fractional COO cost in West Des Moines, IA?

A Fractional COO in West Des Moines typically costs $3,000 to $15,000 per month depending on scope and engagement hours. Most Greater Des Moines businesses engage at 15-20 hours per week. This compares to $200,000 to $450,000+ per year for a full-time executive.

Do you work on-site in West Des Moines or remotely?

We work in the model that best fits your business - remote, hybrid, or on-site in West Des Moines and the Greater Des Moines area as needed. Most engagements are primarily remote with on-site sprints for key milestones and team sessions.

How is a Fractional COO different from a consultant?

A Fractional COO is an embedded executive who owns the operational leadership function - they lead your team, manage vendors, and are accountable to outcomes. A consultant advises from the outside and delivers reports. The fractional model means direct execution responsibility, not recommendations you have to implement yourself.
