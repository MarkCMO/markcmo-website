Quick Answer

A fractional CEO is a part-time Chief Executive Officer who provides senior executive leadership -- strategic direction, investor relations, operational oversight, and team alignment -- on a retainer engagement rather than as a full-time $400,000 to $700,000 annual hire. Growth-stage companies typically engage a fractional CEO during leadership transitions, between funding rounds, or when scaling from $5M to $30M ARR requires more executive bandwidth than the founding team can provide.

Why Jacksonville Companies Hire a Fractional CEO

Jacksonville is Florida largest city with a major port and financial services sector. Companies in logistics, finance, healthcare, military need senior executive leadership to compete and scale -- but the fully-loaded cost of a full-time Chief Executive Officer runs $280,000 to $500,000+ per year.

A Fractional CEO eliminates this tradeoff. You get C-suite executive leadership at a fraction of the cost, with results that start in the first 30 days and compound month over month.

What a Fractional CEO Delivers for Jacksonville Businesses

  • Set and own the company vision, culture, and strategic direction in partnership with investors and the board
  • Build, develop, and hold accountable the full executive leadership team -- CMO, COO, CFO, CTO
  • Own investor relations, board reporting, and fundraising narrative for Jacksonville-area capital sources
  • Drive the annual operating plan, resource allocation, and M&A or partnership strategy
  • Represent the company externally -- customers, press, industry events, and strategic partnerships
  • Identify and close organizational blind spots before they become existential risks

Fractional vs. Full-Time Chief Executive Officer in Jacksonville

A full-time Chief Executive Officer in Jacksonville costs $280,000 to $500,000+ in year one including benefits, equity, and recruiting fees. A fractional engagement runs $8,000 to $25,000 per month -- a difference of $150,000 to $350,000 annually that can be reinvested directly into operations, product, or growth.

For most Jacksonville companies between $1M and $20M in revenue, fractional executive leadership delivers stronger results at lower total cost than a full-time hire. The savings fund the execution.

The 90-Day Fractional CEO Quick Start for Jacksonville Companies

  • Days 1 to 30: Full operational or strategic audit, leadership team assessment, priority identification, and a clear 90-day action plan
  • Days 31 to 60: Implement top-priority systems, restructure where needed, establish operating cadence and reporting infrastructure
  • Days 61 to 90: Optimize what is working, build the 12-month roadmap, and transition the organization to run the new operating model independently

What Fractional CEO Actually Involves

When companies hire a fractional ceo, they are buying senior executive accountability -- not advisory reports. Here is what every engagement covers from day one:

  • Strategic Vision and Direction - Define the 3-5 year company vision, the strategic priorities that drive it, and the operating model that makes it achievable.
  • Operational Leadership - Run the executive team, establish performance accountability, and build the operating cadences that translate strategy to execution.
  • Fundraising and Investor Relations - Lead capital raises, investor communications, and board relationships -- with the credibility that moves deals forward.
  • M&A and Partnerships - Identify, negotiate, and integrate strategic acquisitions and channel relationships that accelerate non-organic growth.
  • Crisis and Turnaround Management - Stabilize distressed companies, reallocate resources, and rebuild executive team confidence when the business needs a reset.
  • Exit and Transition Readiness - Prepare the narrative, management team, and financial story for acquisition, PE exit, or leadership succession.

Who This Is Right For

Companies in transition: CEO departure, founder burnout, rapid scale-up requiring professional management, or a turnaround situation. PE-backed companies that need an experienced operator between the exit and the next hire. Founders who are visionaries but need an executive partner to build the execution engine around them.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a Fractional CEO cost in Jacksonville?
A Fractional CEO engagement in Jacksonville typically runs $8,000 to $25,000 per month for 20 to 40 hours of senior executive leadership. Cost varies based on company complexity, team size, and scope of the engagement.
Does the Fractional CEO need to be in Jacksonville in person?
Most engagements run effectively remotely with regular video check-ins and monthly strategic reviews. On-site sessions in Jacksonville can be arranged for board meetings, team workshops, or critical milestones.
How quickly will I see results?
Jacksonville companies typically see measurable operational or strategic improvements within the first 30 to 60 days. Structural changes and culture shifts take 90 to 180 days to fully take hold.
Is a Fractional CEO right for my stage of business?
The Fractional CEO model works best for companies between $1M and $20M in revenue that need senior executive leadership but cannot yet justify the full-time cost. It also works well during transitions -- new investment, rapid growth, leadership changes, or acquisition integration.
