How to Hire an SEO Expert

A six-step checklist for hiring an SEO expert in 2026 - from confirming stage fit to negotiating engagement terms. Avoid the three expensive hiring mistakes that sink most SEO programs.

Quick Answer

Use a six-step checklist. (1) Confirm stage fit - $2M-$25M revenue is the band where fractional SEO leadership delivers the best economics. (2) Verify portfolio proof - ask for three sites with before-and-after data. (3) Test technical depth - they should explain Core Web Vitals, schema, indexation, and AI search in plain English. (4) Confirm AI search literacy - GEO and AEO competence is now required. (5) Insist on execution capacity - strategy without execution does not ship. (6) Negotiate quarterly renewable terms - reject long lock-in.

Step 1: Confirm Stage Fit

$2M-$25M revenue is the band where fractional SEO leadership delivers the best economics. Below that, growth tactics other than SEO often pay back faster. Above $50M, full-time in-house is usually right. Avoid hiring an SEO expert as your first marketing hire pre-product-market-fit - product and positioning matter more.

Step 2: Verify Portfolio Proof

Ask the candidate to walk through three sites with before-and-after data. Look for specifics: what they did, when they shipped, what the result was, what the constraint was. Reject candidates who show tools, certifications, or generic case study summaries.

Step 3: Test Technical Depth

A 2026 SEO expert must explain Core Web Vitals optimization, schema priority, indexation control, JavaScript rendering, and AI search optimization in plain English. If they default to tool screenshots or jargon, they are a content marketer with an SEO title - not an SEO expert.

Step 4: Confirm AI Search Literacy

The next 18 months of search reach runs through ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews. Ask: what is your approach to GEO and AEO? How do you set up Wikidata entities? What is in llms.txt and why? If they have no thoughtful answers, they are operating on 2023-2024 best practices.

Step 5: Insist on Execution Capacity

Strategy without execution does not ship. Confirm whether the candidate or vendor bundles execution (content production, technical implementation, link earning campaigns) or sells strategy only. A strategy-only engagement requires the client to coordinate operators separately - which is harder than it sounds.

Step 6: Negotiate Engagement Terms

Standard structure: monthly retainer, quarterly renewable, 90-day trial period. Reject vendors who require annual lock-in or who refuse a 30-day notice clause. The engagement should be structured so the client can exit at quarter end if outcomes do not materialize.
