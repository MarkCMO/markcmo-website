B2B Paid Media Agency: The 2026 Buyer's Guide

B2B paid media is its own discipline. Channel mix (LinkedIn-heavy), ABM-aware targeting, intent data integration, and pipeline accountability separate B2B specialists from generalist paid shops. Below: top 9 B2B paid agencies, channel allocation by ACV, and the operator-led alternative.

The Short Answer

Most B2B paid waste happens at the strategy layer, not the bid layer. If your paid agency talks about CPC + CPL but not pipeline conversion + ICP fit + sales handoff, fire them. The next agency should report on opportunity-to-close revenue per ad dollar, not just MQL volume.
