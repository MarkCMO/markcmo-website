The rules

Risk discipline is the whole game.

The rules mirror what real prop firms enforce, so winning here proves you are ready there.

Profit target

Grow the simulated balance to the division target during the week to qualify for the leaderboard.

Trailing maximum drawdown

A floor that trails your highest balance. Fall below it and you are out of the running for the week.

Daily loss limit

Measured from your start-of-day balance. Cross it and the account locks for the day.

Ranked by consistency

Among traders who pass, the leaderboard ranks by drawdown control and consistency, not by who took the wildest swing.

Minimum trading days

Trade at least the minimum days before a result counts. No one-shot gambles win an account.

Instruments

Trade micro and mini futures: MES, ES, MNQ, NQ, MCL, CL, MGC, GC. Position size is capped per division.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is the prize a real funded account? +

Yes. You compete on a trading simulator, but the prize is a real funded or evaluation account at a top-rated prop firm such as Apex. Winners verify their identity, then receive the account. The competition itself involves no real-money trading and no capital at risk.

How do I win? +

Reach your division's profit target during the week without breaching the drawdown or daily-loss rules. Everyone who passes is then ranked by consistency and risk discipline. The top 3 each week win a funded account. It rewards trading like a funded trader, not gambling.

Why is this cheaper than a normal challenge? +

A prop firm challenge charges you up front whether you pass or not. Here you pay $5 a week to compete, and the account is the prize for winning. You only need the skill, not the fee.

Is there a free way to enter? +

Yes. A free alternative method of entry is available for every competition, with no purchase necessary. Full details are in the official rules for each season.

Do I need a brokerage account or a download? +

No. Everything runs in the browser. You never connect a broker, fund an account, or place a real market order to compete.
