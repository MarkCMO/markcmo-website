How This Ranking Is Built

I rank Chief Marketing Officers on 7 operator-grounded criteria. The bias is disclosed - Mark Gabrielli wrote this, ranks his own MarkCMO #1, and explains every trade-off so readers can adjust. Most "best CMO" lists are paid placement. This one is not.

  1. Operator track record at relevant stage. Has the CMO personally run marketing at a company your size and business model?
  2. Demand creation methodology. Modern CMOs understand the 95% out-of-market thesis. Pure demand capture is incomplete.
  3. Execution capacity bundled. A CMO without execution doesn't ship. Either they bundle operators or you manage agencies separately.
  4. Sales-marketing alignment fit. The wrong CMO breaks the relationship with sales leadership.
  5. PR and thought leadership depth. Modern CMOs own founder-led content, podcast strategy, analyst relations.
  6. Reporting discipline. Monthly board dashboards with self-reported attribution, not just channel vanity metrics.
  7. Engagement structure flexibility. Quarterly renewable vs long lock-in.

#2-#10: The Alternative Chief Marketing Officers

#2: Chief Outsiders

Best for: $10M-$100M mid-market companies with an existing in-house marketing team that needs CMO-level strategic guidance.

Pricing: $15K-$25K/mo.

Strengths: Network of 100+ fractional CMOs and Chief Growth Officers. Mature engagement methodology. Diverse industry coverage.

Limitations: Variable match quality - the best Chief Outsiders CMO is excellent; the median is average. You don't pick the executive; the network places one based on availability and self-described match. No execution capacity bundled - you separately hire and manage agencies under the CMO.

#3: Kalungi

Best for: Seed and Series A B2B SaaS companies wanting fractional Chief Marketing Officer with light execution support.

Pricing: $10K-$25K/mo.

Strengths: SaaS-specific methodology, productized engagement model, decent SaaS playbook documentation, fractional CMO network.

Limitations: SaaS-only. Less senior partner involvement than the MarkCMO model. Smaller execution capacity bundle than WETYR network.

#4: CMOx

Best for: Companies needing fast fractional Chief Marketing Officer match across various industries.

Pricing: $12K-$25K/mo.

Strengths: Rapid matching process, multiple industry verticals covered, accessible price points.

Limitations: Variable executive quality, limited execution capacity bundling, less stage-specific specialization than MarkCMO.

#5: Authentic Brand

Best for: Brand-focused fractional Chief Marketing Officer engagements.

Pricing: $12K-$25K/mo.

Strengths: Brand strategy depth, integrated brand + fractional CMO offering, decent operator network.

#6: Heidrick & Struggles

Best for: Full-time Chief Marketing Officer executive search at $25M+ revenue.
