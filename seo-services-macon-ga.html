Quick Answer

Search engine optimization (SEO) for B2B companies is the systematic process of ranking website pages for the queries that ideal customers search when researching their problems and evaluating solutions -- building topical authority through structured content clusters, earning backlinks from authoritative industry sources, and maintaining the technical foundation that allows Google to crawl and index every page at full authority. B2B SEO's highest ROI comes from targeting commercial-intent queries (comparisons, alternatives, pricing, best tools for [use case]) that reach buyers at the evaluation stage, converting organic traffic directly to pipeline rather than generating awareness traffic with no path to revenue.
