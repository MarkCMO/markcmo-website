Film Distribution Strategy: The 2026 Producer's Guide

Distributor selection determines whether your film breaks out or disappears. Below: distributor matching by film type, distribution waterfall math, hybrid release strategy, and 2026 acquisition pricing for indies + studio specialty.

The Short Answer

The wrong distributor kills good films. Don't take the highest MG - take the distributor with the right audience reach, marketing investment, and reputation for filmmaker collaboration. A $1M MG with A24 outperforms $3M MG with a distributor who'll dump the film into a quiet release window.

Distribution Waterfall (Standard Order)

  1. Distribution fees - 25-50% of gross distribution revenue to distributor.
  2. Distribution expenses - P&A spent, festival expenses, delivery, audit, residuals.
  3. Sales agent commission - 10-20% of distribution sale if sales agent involved.
  4. Lender repayment - senior debt, gap financing, tax credit loans first.
  5. Investor preferred return - typically 10-20% annualized on equity.
  6. Investor capital return - return of original principal.
  7. Producer + talent backend - typically 50/50 with investors until investors reach 1.5x-2x.
