SPEAKER BIO

Mark Gabrielli is a fractional CMO and COO with 15+ years of executive marketing experience across aerospace, healthcare, SaaS, fintech, and automotive sectors. He has served as Global CMO for companies ranging from Series A startups to multinational corporations, built marketing functions from zero, and managed multi-million dollar budgets across every major paid and organic channel.

Mark holds a degree in Biological Sciences, is a Certified Surgical Technologist, and brings a data-driven, systems-first approach to marketing that is grounded in operational reality - not theory. His audiences describe his talks as "uncomfortably honest" and "the best 45 minutes I've spent on marketing in years."

Based in Cape Canaveral, FL. Available in-person or remote. 4,000+ LinkedIn followers. Clients across North America, Europe, and Australia.
