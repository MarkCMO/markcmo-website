Arizona Marketing Agency: The 2026 Buyer's Guide

AZ is one of fastest-growing US economies. Semiconductor boom (TSMC Phoenix $40B+ fab, Intel Chandler $20B+ expansion), Honeywell Aerospace HQ, Raytheon Tucson, American Express + Schwab + Wells Fargo Phoenix campuses, Banner Health + Mayo Phoenix. Grand Canyon + Sedona tourism. Below: top AZ agencies.

The Short Answer

Arizona's semiconductor boom (TSMC + Intel + ON Semi + Microchip) is reshaping AZ marketing demand toward B2B industrial + chip industry + supplier ecosystem. Phoenix-Scottsdale also growing as retiree + lifestyle + golf + resort destination. Tucson defense + aerospace heavy.

AZ's 6 Dominant Industries

  1. Semiconductors + chip manufacturing. TSMC Phoenix $40B+, Intel Chandler $20B+ expansion, ON Semi HQ, Microchip Technology HQ.
  2. Aerospace + defense. Honeywell Aerospace HQ, Raytheon Tucson, Boeing AH-64 Apache Mesa, Lockheed Martin Tucson, Northrop Grumman Tucson.
  3. Tech + fintech. American Express Phoenix, Charles Schwab Phoenix campus, Wells Fargo Tempe.
  4. Healthcare. Banner Health HQ, Mayo Clinic Phoenix, Dignity Health.
  5. Tourism + hospitality. Grand Canyon (4.7M+ visitors), Sedona, Phoenix-Scottsdale luxury resorts, Tucson, Lake Havasu.
  6. Real estate + retirement. Phoenix fastest-growing US metro, retiree migration.
