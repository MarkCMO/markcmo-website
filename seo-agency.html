SEO Agency: The 2026 Buyer's Guide

SEO in 2026 is now SEO + GEO (Generative Engine Optimization for AI search). Traditional rankings still matter for commercial queries. AI citation matters for informational research. Agencies that only do one are losing. Below: top 9 SEO agencies reviewed, real pricing, and the operator-led model.

The Short Answer

If an SEO agency cannot tell you what GEO is, cannot show their own AI citation tracking, and cannot rank their own site for relevant terms - do not hire them. The bar has risen sharply since 2024. The right $4K-$12K/mo agency in 2026 delivers what a $15K-$25K/mo agency delivered in 2022.

What an SEO Agency Actually Does in 2026

SEO has changed. Traditional Google organic clicks declined 15-30% from 2023 to 2026 as AI Overviews intercepted top-of-funnel queries. The new SEO stack:

  1. Technical SEO. Crawlability, indexability, site speed, mobile usability, Core Web Vitals (LCP under 2.5s, INP under 200ms, CLS under 0.1).
  2. Keyword research + topic mapping. Stage and intent-aware. Commercial vs informational vs transactional queries.
  3. On-page optimization. Titles, meta, headers, internal linking, definition-lead content for AI extraction.
  4. Content production for SEO. SEO without strong content is dead. Quality > volume.
  5. Link acquisition. Digital PR, original research linkable assets, broken-link building, guest posting (still works if done right).
  6. Local SEO + GBP. For businesses with physical locations or service areas.
  7. Schema markup. FAQPage, HowTo, Article, Product, LocalBusiness, Organization. Critical for rich results and AI extraction.
  8. Core Web Vitals optimization. Ranking signal + UX signal.
  9. GEO / AI search optimization. The 2026 differentiator. Optimizing to be cited by ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews.
  10. Reporting. Conversions and revenue impact, not just traffic and rankings.

The SEO + GEO Era

By 2026, SEO without GEO is incomplete. Generative Engine Optimization is the discipline of optimizing content and entity signals to be cited and recommended by AI systems - ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Copilot.

Critical GEO signals:

  • Definition-lead content. First sentence states what the topic is in a quotable format AI can extract directly.
  • Quotable blocks. 2-3 sentence authoritative statements that stand alone as quotes.
  • Statistical anchors. Specific numbers, percentages, dates that AI prefers over vague claims.
  • Structured data + schema. FAQPage, Article, HowTo, ItemList - all help AI parse content.
  • llms.txt compliance. A 2025+ standard for declaring AI-friendly content.
  • AI crawler permissions. robots.txt explicitly allowing GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, anthropic-ai, CCBot.
  • Entity consistency across the web. Same brand description on LinkedIn, Crunchbase, Wikipedia, your site - so AI builds confidence in the entity.
  • Authorship and E-E-A-T. Clear named authors with credentials and sameAs to LinkedIn profiles.

An SEO agency that does not actively track AI citations and adapt content for GEO is operating on 2022 playbooks. By 2026 that gap is severe.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an SEO agency?

A specialized marketing firm focused on improving organic search rankings and traffic. In 2026, modern SEO is now SEO + GEO (Generative Engine Optimization for AI search like Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity).

How much does an SEO agency cost?

Cheap offshore $300-$1,500/mo (worthless). Small business $1.5K-$4K/mo. Mid-market $4K-$12K/mo. Enterprise $12K-$30K/mo. Tier-one $15K-$50K/mo. MarkCMO SEO inside $8K-$25K/mo integrated retainer.

How long does SEO take to work?

6-18 month compounding curve. Months 1-3 setup, 3-6 long-tail rankings, 6-12 mid-tail rankings, 12-18 head terms rank. Stopping at month 4 wastes the investment.

What is GEO and how is it different from SEO?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is optimization to be cited by AI systems - ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews. Targets being the source AI cites when answering buyer research questions. SEO targets traditional search ranking. By 2026, both matter.

Is SEO dead because of AI search?

No, but it has changed. Traditional clicks declined 15-30% from 2023-2026. High-commercial-intent queries still click through. Long-tail still drives traffic. AI citation matters more than ever. Right strategy: SEO + GEO together.
