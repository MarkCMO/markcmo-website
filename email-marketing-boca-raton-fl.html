Email Marketing - Frequently Asked Questions

What email marketing services do Boca Raton businesses actually need?

Most Boca Raton businesses need three things: properly configured email infrastructure (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, domain warming so emails actually land in the inbox), a segmented list strategy (so you are sending the right message to the right contact), and revenue-driven sequences (automated workflows tied to specific pipeline stages that move contacts toward a buying decision). MarkCMO builds all three as an integrated system.

Why are my emails going to spam and how do I fix it?

Deliverability failures are almost always caused by one of four issues: missing or misconfigured SPF/DKIM/DMARC records, a cold domain being used for high volume without warming, sending to an unclean list with high bounce rates, or content triggering spam filters. MarkCMO audits your entire sending infrastructure, fixes the DNS configuration, runs domain warming protocols, cleans your list, and monitors deliverability rates across all major ISPs.

What is the difference between cold email and warm email infrastructure?

Warm email is sent to opted-in contacts -- your list, your nurture sequences, your customer communications. Cold email is sent to prospects who have not opted in -- outbound prospecting. Cold email should NEVER run through your main domain or ESP. MarkCMO sets up dedicated cold email infrastructure: separate subdomain, separate IP, warmed gradually, with tools like Instantly or Smartlead handling the sending so your main domain reputation stays protected.

How do I build a high-converting email sequence for Boca Raton buyers?

A high-converting email sequence for Boca Raton B2B buyers follows a specific structure: the first email delivers value without asking for anything, the second establishes credibility with a relevant proof point, the third introduces the problem you solve in concrete terms, the fourth presents the offer with a clear and low-friction CTA. MarkCMO writes, tests, and optimizes every sequence against reply rates and click-to-meeting conversions -- not vanity opens.

What email platforms does MarkCMO work with in Boca Raton?

MarkCMO works with the full ESP ecosystem: HubSpot (preferred for B2B CRM-integrated email), ActiveCampaign (preferred for complex nurture logic at scale), Klaviyo (preferred for e-commerce), Mailchimp (for basic list management), and Instantly/Smartlead/Apollo for cold outbound infrastructure. Platform recommendation depends on your stack, deal size, and sending volume.
