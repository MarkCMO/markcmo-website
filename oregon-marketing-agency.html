OR's 6 Dominant Industries

  1. Sportswear + outdoor. Nike HQ (Beaverton, world's largest sportswear), Adidas NA HQ (Portland), Columbia Sportswear HQ, Keen HQ, Hi-Tec, LaCrosse Footwear.
  2. Tech. Intel Hillsboro (largest Intel site globally, 23K+ employees), Tektronix, Mentor Graphics (Siemens).
  3. Craft beverage. Deschutes, Widmer, Rogue, plus distilleries (Aviation Gin) + Willamette Valley wine.
  4. Forestry + lumber. Weyerhaeuser HQ historically, Roseburg Forest Products.
  5. Healthcare. OHSU, Providence Health, growing biotech.
  6. Tourism. Crater Lake, coast, Cascades, Portland food + Powell's Books.
