PPC Agency: The 2026 Buyer's Guide

PPC in 2026 is now creative-bound, not bidding-bound. AI-driven bid optimization closed most of the targeting gap; the brands that win are the ones with the best creative and the best attribution. Below: top 9 PPC agencies reviewed, real pricing, the media-markup problem, and the operator-led alternative.

The Short Answer

Always ask "is media spend marked up?" Agencies that mark up 15-25% on media add hidden cost the CEO often does not see. On $50K/mo spend, that is $7.5K-$12.5K/mo extra. MarkCMO/WETYR charges 0% markup on media as standard.

What a Modern PPC Agency Does

PPC has changed structurally since 2022. Three forces reshaped the discipline:

  1. iOS 14 broke pixel attribution. Modern PPC requires server-side tracking (Conversion API, Enhanced Conversions), MMM (marketing mix modeling), and triangulated attribution.
  2. AI-driven bid management. Google's Performance Max, Smart Bidding, and Meta's Advantage+ have automated most of the bidding work. The targeting gap closed. The new edge is creative and conversion data quality.
  3. Creative volume as bottleneck. PPC is now creative-bound. A brand spending $50K/mo on Meta needs 20-40 new creative units monthly. The brands that win are the ones with creative engines, not bidding wizards.

An agency that talks about "manual bid management" and "exact-match targeting" without addressing creative volume and post-iOS 14 attribution is operating on 2020 playbooks. By 2026 that is severe.

The Media Markup Problem

Many PPC agencies charge management fees PLUS a markup on media spend, typically 15-25%. The structure is opaque: the agency books the media at marked-up rates, the CEO sees one bill that combines management + media + markup, and the markup is hidden in the media line. On a $50K/mo media spend with 20% markup:

  • Actual media to Google/Meta: $50,000
  • Markup retained by agency: $10,000
  • Plus management fee: $5,000
  • CEO sees one $65,000 bill
  • Real cost: $15,000 of services for $50,000 of media

On a 12-month engagement at $50K/mo media, the markup alone is $120,000 - more than the average mid-tier engagement's entire management fee. Always ask in writing: "Is media spend marked up, and if so by how much?" Agencies that dodge this are doing it. Agencies that charge zero markup (MarkCMO/WETYR is one) deliver 30-50% lower true cost than agencies that mark up media.

  8. Negative keyword management. Underrated discipline - bad negatives leak money.
  9. Search query mining + optimization. Where the real wins come from.
  10. Reporting against unit economics. ROAS, CAC, payback - not vanity metrics.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a PPC agency?

A specialized firm focused on paid advertising across search engines (Google, Bing) and increasingly paid social (Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok). Delivers strategy, keyword research, ad copy, attribution, optimization.

How much does a PPC agency cost?

Two components: management fee + media markup. Small business $500-$2.5K/mo. Mid-market $1.5K-$8K/mo. Enterprise $5K-$25K/mo. Plus 0-25% media markup. MarkCMO charges 0% markup.

What is the media markup problem?

Many agencies mark up media 15-25% as hidden cost on top of management fees. On $50K/mo media, 20% markup = $10K/mo extra. Always ask: "Is media spend marked up?"

What is the difference between PPC and SEO?

PPC: pay for clicks, fast feedback, traffic stops when budget stops. SEO: invest in content + technical, compounds over 9-18 months, continues working after work stops. Right strategy uses both.

What ROAS should I target?

Target derives from unit economics. B2B SaaS CAC payback under 18 months. DTC blended ROAS 3-5x. Local services CPA tied to LTV. Agencies that target generic ROAS without your unit economics are working from averages.
