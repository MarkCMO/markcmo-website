Mississippi's Dominant Industries

Mississippi hosts Wayne-Sanderson Farms (Laurel - poultry, formed by Cargill + Continental Grain acquisition of Sanderson Farms 2022, third-largest US chicken producer), Cal-Maine Foods HQ (Ridgeland - largest US egg producer). Plus Toyota Mississippi (Blue Springs - Corolla + Corolla Cross assembly), Nissan Canton plant (Frontier + Titan trucks + Murano + Pathfinder SUVs), Yokohama Tire (West Point Mississippi tire plant). Plus Mississippi shipbuilding (Ingalls Shipbuilding Pascagoula - US Navy + Coast Guard ships, Huntington Ingalls Industries - one of largest US Navy shipbuilders, builds destroyers + amphibious assault ships + Coast Guard cutters, 11K+ employees). Mississippi gaming (Gulf Coast casinos in Biloxi + Tunica + Vicksburg - early casino legalization made MS a major US gaming destination). Plus University of Mississippi Oxford (Ole Miss) + Mississippi State University Starkville - SEC football powerhouses. Cotton + soybeans + rice agriculture. Mississippi River barge logistics. Plus Camp Shelby Hattiesburg (largest US Army National Guard training base). Plus B.B. King + blues music heritage (Mississippi Delta = birthplace of blues).
