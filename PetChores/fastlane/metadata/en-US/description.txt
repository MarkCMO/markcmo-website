Pet Chores helps your family answer one question before you bring an animal home: is this child ready for this pet, right now?

Unlike virtual pet games, Pet Chores trains your child to do the real, everyday care of a real animal, on a real schedule, with the real cost of ownership in plain sight. At the end of a short practice window, you get a clear readiness verdict to guide the decision.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT
- Real, species-specific care. Each animal has its actual daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly routine, with the real action to perform and what happens if it is skipped. Not a generic "feed the pet" checkbox.
- Real cost of ownership. See startup, monthly, and first-year supply costs for each animal, so the budget conversation happens before the purchase, not after.
- A readiness report. After the window, see completion rate, on-time rate, streaks, and a plain-language verdict for the family.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Your child picks an animal to practice with, from dogs and cats to fish, reptiles, birds, and more.
2. The app schedules that animal's real care tasks and reminds your child at the right times.
3. Your child does the real chore, then taps Done.
4. You verify it in Parent Mode.
5. Care Points, streaks, and a Trust meter reward steady, kind, consistent care.
6. A living scene shows the pet's wellbeing rise and fall with the care it receives, always gently and never scary.

BUILT FOR FAMILIES
- Parent Mode behind a PIN: verify chores, adjust settings, and read the report.
- Choose whether missed tasks carry over or simply pass, like real care.
- Optional photo proof, kept on the device only.
- Quiet hours so reminders never arrive at bedtime.

PRIVACY FIRST
No accounts. No ads. No trackers. Nothing leaves the device. Photos, if you enable them, stay on your phone.

FREE AND FAIR
Train one pet completely free. To train more pets at once, parents can start an optional subscription, always behind the parental gate so a child can never trigger it.
