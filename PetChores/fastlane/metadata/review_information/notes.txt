No account or login is required; the app is fully offline.

How to review:
1. Complete onboarding: enter a child name and age, then set a 4 digit Parent PIN
   (you choose it on the setup screen, remember it for the next step), pick any pet,
   and choose a training length.
2. The Home tab shows today's care tasks. Tap a task, then tap Done to complete it.
3. Parent Mode: tap the "Grown-ups" lock button on the Home screen and enter the PIN
   you set. From there you can Verify Tasks, change Settings, view the Readiness
   Report, Manage Pets, and reach Unlock.
4. In-app purchase: the single non-consumable unlock ("petchores.unlock.full") and the
   Restore Purchases button live only inside PIN-gated Parent Mode -> Unlock. A child
   cannot reach any purchase UI.

Privacy: no data is collected and nothing leaves the device. Optional photo proof is
stored locally only. The app contains no ads and no third party SDKs.
