Fractional CISO Cost in 2026

What a fractional CISO costs, by engagement model, and how it compares to a full-time Chief Information Security Officer.

Quick Answer

A fractional CISO costs $7,000-$18,000 per month in 2026, vs $240,000-$450,000 all-in for a full-time Chief Information Security Officer - 40-70% less. MarkCMO bundles fractional leadership with WETYR execution. Book a call.

Cost by engagement model

Hourly/advisory: $200-$500/hr for ad hoc work. Retained fractional: $7,000-$18,000 per month for ongoing ownership of security strategy, compliance (SOC 2, HIPAA, ISO 27001), and risk management. Full-time hire: $240,000-$450,000 all-in (base, bonus, equity, benefits, payroll taxes). The retained fractional model is the best value for $2M-$25M revenue companies that need senior leadership but not full-time coverage.

What drives the price

Three factors set fractional CISO pricing: time commitment (1-3 days/week), seniority (years and exit track record), and scope (single-function vs cross-functional).
