Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a Fractional COO cost in Mount Pleasant, SC?

A Fractional COO in Mount Pleasant typically costs $3,000 to $15,000 per month depending on scope and engagement hours. Most Greater Charleston businesses engage at 15-20 hours per week. This compares to $200,000 to $450,000+ per year for a full-time executive.

Do you work on-site in Mount Pleasant or remotely?

We work in the model that best fits your business - remote, hybrid, or on-site in Mount Pleasant and the Greater Charleston area as needed. Most engagements are primarily remote with on-site sprints for key milestones and team sessions.

How is a Fractional COO different from a consultant?

A Fractional COO is an embedded executive who owns the operational leadership function - they lead your team, manage vendors, and are accountable to outcomes. A consultant advises from the outside and delivers reports. The fractional model means direct execution responsibility, not recommendations you have to implement yourself.

What industries do you serve in the Greater Charleston area?

In Mount Pleasant and the broader Greater Charleston market, we serve healthcare, technology, professional services. We bring experience from 15+ years of C-suite engagements across multiple industries and apply that cross-sector pattern recognition to your specific market context.
