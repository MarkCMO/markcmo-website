Burlington's Business Landscape

Burlington's economy combines institutional scale with entrepreneurial energy in a way that's unusual for a city of its size. UVM Medical Center and the University of Vermont provide employment and research anchors. GlobalFoundries' semiconductor operations represent advanced manufacturing at a global scale. A thriving startup ecosystem has emerged around UVM research programs and Vermont's quality-of-life advantages for recruiting technical talent. Tourism, food, and hospitality complete a diverse economic picture that rewards versatile, values-aware marketing.

Healthcare and Life Sciences

UVM Medical Center is Vermont's largest employer and the anchor of a healthcare ecosystem that extends throughout the state. Marketing to this system and to the vendors that serve it requires a deep understanding of academic medical center culture, research priorities, and the administrative decision-making processes that govern major purchasing. Life sciences companies, clinical technology vendors, and healthcare services firms must position their capabilities with clinical credibility, regulatory awareness, and the long-term relationship orientation that healthcare buyers require.
