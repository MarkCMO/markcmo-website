Will County's Logistics Economy Operates at a National Scale

CenterPoint Intermodal Center in Elwood is the largest inland port in North America - a 6,500-acre complex connecting BNSF and Union Pacific rail to I-80 and the broader national highway system. The warehousing, distribution, and logistics ecosystem surrounding this facility handles goods for Amazon, Walmart, Target, and hundreds of other national retailers. Third-party logistics companies, warehouse management software vendors, labor staffing agencies, and logistics technology providers all compete for a supply chain procurement market that processes billions of dollars in freight annually.

Companies serving this ecosystem need B2B marketing strategies built around supply chain buyer psychology, operations and procurement decision-maker targeting, and the trade association presence (CSCMP, MHI, IWLA) that logistics companies use to evaluate vendors. A Fractional CMO who understands this B2B dynamic builds marketing programs that generate qualified pipeline rather than unqualified website traffic.

Will County's Core Industry Sectors

Logistics and distribution anchor Will County's economy. The I-80 corridor from Joliet to Bolingbrook through Romeoville is lined with distribution centers for national retailers, e-commerce fulfillment companies, and third-party logistics providers. Logistics technology companies - TMS, WMS, ERP, and supply chain visibility platforms - compete for the attention of operations leaders and IT buyers at these distribution centers. Marketing to logistics buyers requires operational credibility, ROI-focused content, and reference customer networks that speak the language of throughput, accuracy, and cost per unit.

Manufacturing maintains a significant presence in Joliet and the surrounding industrial zones. Caterpillar's manufacturing operations, chemical manufacturers, food processing companies, and light industrial producers all have Will County facilities. These companies need B2B marketing for supply chain relationships, industrial equipment procurement, and professional services contracts.

Healthcare in Joliet is anchored by AMITA Health Saint Joseph Medical Center and Advocate Christ Medical Center (in Oak Lawn on the Cook County border). These facilities create B2B demand for healthcare technology vendors, staffing companies, and clinical services providers operating in the Will County market.

Professional services in Joliet and the growing Bolingbrook and Naperville-adjacent communities serve the county's rapidly expanding residential and commercial market. Accounting, legal, insurance, and financial advisory firms all compete for the county's growing professional client base as population growth brings more business activity to Will County.
