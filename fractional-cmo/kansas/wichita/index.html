Wichita's Business Landscape

Wichita is Kansas' largest city and one of the most industrially concentrated metros in the central United States. The aviation and aerospace manufacturing sector - Boeing's commercial aviation division, Textron Aviation's Cessna and Beechcraft brands, Spirit AeroSystems, and dozens of Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers - defines the city's identity and drives enormous supply chain activity. Oil and gas remains an important economic pillar, with exploration, production, and energy services companies operating across south-central Kansas. Via Christi and Wesley Medical Center anchor a growing healthcare sector. Agriculture, always present in Kansas' economy, contributes through grain, cattle, and agricultural technology businesses based in or near the city.
