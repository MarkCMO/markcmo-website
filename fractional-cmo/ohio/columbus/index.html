Columbus's Business Landscape

Columbus is the 14th-largest city in the United States by population and one of the few major metros that grew consistently through both the 2008 recession and the 2020 pandemic. Ohio State University's presence generates continuous innovation, research commercialization, and a deep talent pool that feeds Columbus's growth engine across multiple industries.
