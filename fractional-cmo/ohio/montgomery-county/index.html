Montgomery County's Business Landscape

Montgomery County's economy is defined by Wright-Patterson AFB's dominance, complemented by GE Aviation, Premier Health, and a University of Dayton and Wright State University innovation ecosystem that continuously commercializes aerospace, defense, and materials science research.

Aerospace and Defense

Wright-Patterson Air Force Base houses the Air Force Research Laboratory and multiple major program offices, making it one of the nation's most important defense technology centers. The base's procurement activity supports hundreds of defense contractors from small businesses to major primes. Marketing to defense buyers in Dayton requires understanding the nuanced differences between program office marketing, SBIR/STTR outreach, and prime contractor relationship development - each requiring a different approach to earning the right to compete for the county's most valuable defense contracts.

Healthcare

Premier Health - Montgomery County's largest health system - anchors the healthcare sector alongside several competing systems that create a competitive patient acquisition market across the Dayton metro. Healthcare marketing in Dayton requires strong community presence, digital patient acquisition programs, and the kind of specialist marketing that highlights clinical excellence in the procedures that drive complex care decisions. The county's significant military and veteran population also creates specialized healthcare marketing opportunities for providers with military health expertise.

Technology and Manufacturing

Montgomery County's technology sector has grown substantially around the defense ecosystem - cybersecurity companies, systems integrators, and engineering technology firms that do dual-use commercial and government work have built significant presences in the Dayton innovation district. The county's manufacturing base - diversified from its earlier automotive and industrial roots into advanced manufacturing and defense component production - creates B2B marketing demand from suppliers seeking positions in the aerospace and defense supply chain that Wright-Patterson anchors.
