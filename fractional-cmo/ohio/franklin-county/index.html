Franklin County's Business Landscape

Franklin County's economy spans state government, major financial services corporations, world-class healthcare systems, Ohio State University's innovation engine, and a rapidly growing technology sector that has made Columbus one of the Midwest's most dynamic business markets.

Financial Services

Nationwide Insurance and JPMorgan Chase's major Columbus operations make Franklin County one of Ohio's most important financial services markets. The presence of these giants draws a substantial ecosystem of fintech companies, financial technology vendors, and professional services firms. Marketing for financial services companies in Franklin County requires both the institutional credibility to win enterprise accounts and the thought leadership depth to differentiate in a market where national competitors constantly compete for the same clients and talent.

Healthcare

OhioHealth and Nationwide Children's Hospital are Franklin County's healthcare anchors - both nationally recognized systems that generate enormous vendor market activity and drive competitive patient acquisition across the county. The county's large population and OSU Wexner Medical Center's academic research programs add substantial depth to the healthcare market. Healthcare marketing in Columbus requires both institutional B2B sophistication and compelling consumer health messaging for a competitive multi-system market where patients exercise genuine choice.

Technology and State Government

Ohio state government is Franklin County's largest employer, creating significant market for technology vendors, professional services firms, and consultants that understand government procurement processes. OSU's technology commercialization programs have spawned dozens of tech startups, and Columbus's growing tech scene - bolstered by Intel's major semiconductor investment nearby - is attracting venture capital and established technology companies. Marketing for technology companies here must balance government sales expertise with the faster-moving startup ecosystem's marketing requirements.
