Iowa City's Business Landscape

Iowa City's economy is fundamentally shaped by the University of Iowa and its hospital system - two institutions that together employ a significant share of the city's workforce and drive the majority of its economic activity. The university's research enterprise generates dozens of startup companies and licensing opportunities each year through the UI Research Foundation. University of Iowa Hospitals and Clinics serves as one of the state's largest hospitals and one of the nation's premier academic medical centers, drawing patients and recruiting talent nationally. Around these two anchors, a dense ecosystem of technology, healthcare, and professional services companies has developed - creating a knowledge economy that is disproportionately sophisticated relative to the city's size.
