Worcester's Business Landscape

Worcester's economy has undergone a significant transformation over the past two decades - shifting from a post-industrial manufacturing center to a diversified economy with strong healthcare, bioscience, and technology sectors. The City Square redevelopment and the growth of UMass Medical School have attracted significant investment and reshaped the city's commercial identity.

Healthcare and Life Sciences

UMass Memorial Medical Center is Central Massachusetts's largest employer and one of New England's most important academic medical systems. The UMass Chan Medical School drives research activity that has spawned a growing cluster of biotech and health technology companies in the Worcester area.

Academic MedicineHealth Tech

Advanced Manufacturing and Technology

Worcester's manufacturing heritage is transforming through advanced materials, precision manufacturing, and engineering technology. WPI's co-op programs feed a steady pipeline of engineering talent into local manufacturers who compete globally on quality, precision, and delivery reliability.

Precision ManufacturingAdvanced Materials

Worcester is one of the most college-dense cities in New England - home to WPI, Clark University, Holy Cross, and several other institutions.
