Norfolk County's South Shore Economy

Quincy is Norfolk County's largest city and a significant commercial hub with a downtown office market that houses financial services operations, regional healthcare organizations, and professional service firms. The Quincy Center redevelopment has attracted younger companies and innovation-adjacent businesses that previously defaulted to Boston proper. Braintree and Dedham serve as regional retail and office centers with easy T access to Boston, making them attractive locations for companies balancing urban market access with suburban operating costs.

The Route 1 and I-95 corridor through Norwood, Walpole, and Foxborough supports an industrial and manufacturing base alongside a growing commercial real estate and professional services sector. Gillette Stadium in Foxborough is the home of the New England Patriots, generating sports marketing, hospitality, and event-related business activity across the county. South Shore Hospital, the Brigham and Women's/Mass General Brigham network, and numerous specialty practices serve a large, relatively affluent patient population that demands sophisticated healthcare marketing to reach and retain.

Financial Services and Insurance

Norfolk County's Quincy-anchored financial services community includes regional banks, insurance operations, and wealth management firms. B2B and B2C financial marketing strategy builds client acquisition pipelines, brand authority, and digital presence in a competitive suburban Boston market.

Healthcare and Specialty Medicine

South Shore Hospital, Beth Israel Deaconess Milton, and a network of specialty practices serve Norfolk County's growing population. Patient acquisition marketing, physician referral development, and digital presence strategy drive volume for healthcare organizations competing against Boston's major academic medical center brands.

Technology and Professional Services

Route 128 south corridor technology and professional service firms benefit from demand generation, thought leadership, and account-based marketing programs that position them as category leaders against larger Boston-based and national competitors.
