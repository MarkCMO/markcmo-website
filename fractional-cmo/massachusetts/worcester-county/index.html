Worcester County's Diversified Business Landscape

Worcester is the second-largest city in Massachusetts and the commercial hub of a county that spans from the Connecticut border north to New Hampshire. The healthcare economy is anchored by UMass Memorial, St. Vincent Hospital (Tenet), and a network of community health centers that together employ a significant portion of the county's workforce. The Worcester Biotech Park has attracted life sciences companies drawn by proximity to UMass Medical's research capabilities and the availability of laboratory and manufacturing space at a fraction of Cambridge's cost.

Advanced manufacturing remains a cornerstone of Worcester County's economy, with precision machining, plastics, and specialty chemicals companies serving aerospace, defense, and automotive supply chains nationally. The Route 9 corridor through Shrewsbury, Northborough, and Westborough hosts technology companies and financial services firms that benefit from Boston proximity without Boston real estate costs. WPI graduates increasingly stay in the county to launch engineering and technology ventures, adding entrepreneurial energy to an already diverse economic base.
