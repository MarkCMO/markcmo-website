The Middlesex County Innovation Economy

Middlesex County's economic identity is defined by the proximity of world-class research institutions and the private companies that commercialize their output. Biogen, Moderna, and Takeda operate major facilities in Cambridge alongside hundreds of biotech and pharmaceutical companies that depend on proximity to MIT and Harvard research networks. The Broad Institute, the Whitehead Institute, and MIT's Lincoln Laboratory generate defense and intelligence contracting activity that supports engineering firms across the Route 128 corridor.

Beyond life sciences, Middlesex County hosts a substantial enterprise technology sector. The medical device sector, represented by companies with distribution networks serving hospitals nationally, adds another dimension of B2B marketing complexity.
