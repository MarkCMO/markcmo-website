Why Florida Companies Hire Fractional CMOs

Florida is one of the fastest-growing business environments in the United States. With over 3 million small businesses and one of the highest rates of new business formation in the country, Florida companies face intense competition across every sector - from professional services and real estate to hospitality, healthcare, and technology.

In this environment, marketing is not optional. Companies that lack clear brand positioning, consistent demand generation, and a senior leader accountable for marketing results fall behind competitors who do. But most Florida businesses between $1M and $15M in revenue cannot justify a full-time CMO at $350,000 to $500,000 per year in total compensation.

A fractional CMO gives Florida companies access to the same quality of C-suite marketing leadership at 60% to 75% lower cost. The engagement starts within 1 to 2 weeks - not 4 to 6 months of recruiting - and scales up or down based on what the company needs each quarter.

Does a Florida fractional CMO need to be in person?
No. Florida fractional CMO engagements are primarily remote, with weekly video calls, shared dashboards, and asynchronous collaboration tools. What is the difference between a fractional CMO and a marketing agency in Florida?
A fractional CMO owns the marketing strategy, manages agency relationships, and is accountable to the CEO for marketing's contribution to revenue. A Florida marketing agency delivers execution in specific channels without owning the overall strategy or being accountable for business outcomes. Many Florida companies use both: a fractional CMO to set strategy and manage an agency that executes it.
Can a fractional CMO in Florida help with national marketing?
Yes. While the engagement is based in Florida, the marketing strategy covers all relevant markets - national, international, or state-specific - depending on where the company's customers are. Florida-based companies frequently serve national customer bases, and the fractional CMO engagement is designed to match the geographic scope of the business.
