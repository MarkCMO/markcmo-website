Denver County's Diversified Knowledge Economy

Denver's economic transformation from an oil and gas hub into a technology and knowledge economy has made it one of the country's most competitive markets for talent and business growth. The Denver Tech Center south of the city and the LoDo and RiNo districts in the urban core have become magnets for technology companies, startups, and professional services firms drawn by Denver's quality of life, lower cost structure than coastal markets, and strong talent pipeline from the University of Denver, Colorado State, and the University of Colorado system.

Healthcare is the other anchor sector. UCHealth University of Colorado Hospital, National Jewish Health, and the Children's Hospital Colorado complex in Aurora constitute a world-class academic medical center cluster that attracts clinical research, health technology, and life sciences companies from across the country. The energy sector, while more diversified than in previous decades, still supports a significant oil and gas services, renewable energy, and energy technology ecosystem that requires sophisticated B2B marketing to reach corporate procurement and engineering decision-makers. This combination of sectors creates a Denver market that rewards marketing investment with measurable returns across a broad range of industries.
