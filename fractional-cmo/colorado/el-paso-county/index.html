El Paso County's Business Landscape

No county in Colorado - and few in the nation - matches the density of military and space operations infrastructure that defines El Paso County's economy. Fort Carson, Peterson Space Force Base, Schriever Space Force Base, NORAD/NORTHCOM, and the U.S. Air Force Academy make Colorado Springs the undisputed defense and space operations capital of the American West. That concentration draws the full range of defense prime contractors: Lockheed Martin, Boeing, Northrop Grumman, L3Harris, Raytheon, and SAIC all maintain significant operations here, creating a layered supply chain of mid-market and small business contractors competing for subcontract and direct government work. Beyond defense, the county's healthcare systems, tech ecosystem, and tourism economy represent billions in additional commercial market opportunity for businesses that can build marketing engines capable of reaching both government and civilian buyers.

Defense and Space Installations

Fort Carson is one of the Army's largest installations, home to the 4th Infantry Division and more than 30,000 soldiers with 20,000 additional civilian employees and contractors on the surrounding installation. Peterson Space Force Base serves as headquarters for U.S. Space Operations Command and hosts NORAD and NORTHCOM - making it arguably the single most strategically important military installation in the continental United States for space domain awareness and continental air defense. Schriever Space Force Base, located east of Colorado Springs, operates the satellite command and control infrastructure for GPS, missile defense early warning, and military satellite communications systems. Companies supporting these installations navigate a procurement environment where past performance, technical approach, and security posture are evaluated as carefully as price - requiring marketing that speaks the language of federal acquisition professionals and cleared program managers.

Aerospace and Cybersecurity

The concentration of classified space programs and satellite operations at Schriever and Peterson has made El Paso County one of the most active cybersecurity markets in the country. Companies providing zero-trust network architecture, endpoint detection and response, cleared IT staffing, and CMMC compliance consulting find strong and consistent demand from both the installations themselves and the growing commercial defense tech sector that supports them. The U.S. Air Force Academy produces engineers, technologists, and future acquisition officers - many of whom remain in the Colorado Springs area after graduation and populate the leadership of the local defense tech ecosystem. Aerospace companies including Ball Aerospace, Lockheed Martin, L3Harris, and dozens of smaller prime and subcontract performers maintain significant engineering and program management teams in El Paso County, all of whom require vendors and partners who can communicate technical capability with precision and market their differentiators clearly to evaluating officers and source selection authorities.

Healthcare and Higher Education

UCHealth Memorial Hospital and Penrose-St. Francis Health Services anchor El Paso County's healthcare sector, serving a rapidly growing population that has made Colorado Springs the second-largest city in Colorado with nearly 480,000 metro residents. The healthcare market supports a broad ecosystem of medical technology companies, specialty practices, behavioral health providers, and healthcare IT firms that require digital marketing, physician outreach, and patient acquisition strategies calibrated to a highly competitive regional landscape. The University of Colorado Colorado Springs, Colorado College, and Pikes Peak State College contribute a combined enrollment of more than 25,000 students and generate demand for education-adjacent technology, professional development services, and workforce solutions. Tourism anchored by Pikes Peak, Garden of the Gods, and the Air Force Academy visitor center draws millions of visitors annually, supporting hospitality, retail, and recreation businesses that need strong digital presence and local SEO to capture high-intent travel demand from across the country.
