Adams County's Industrial and Commercial Economy

Denver International Airport, the fifth-busiest airport in the United States and the largest by land area, is Adams County's defining economic asset. The airport's cargo, maintenance, and ground services operations support a large aviation services ecosystem, while the planned airport city development is attracting hotels, logistics centers, and technology companies to a zone that benefits from unmatched national and international air connectivity. Companies in freight forwarding, cargo logistics, aviation catering, and airport technology find in Adams County a location that provides direct DIA access without the industrial land cost premiums of other Denver metro submarkets.

Commerce City's industrial complex, including the Suncor Energy refinery and the Rocky Mountain Arsenal National Wildlife Refuge, creates a backdrop for industrial services, environmental consulting, and petrochemical supply companies who serve both the facility operators and the remediation and environmental management industries that have grown in the area. Thornton is Adams County's largest city and a substantial retail and healthcare market, with SCL Health St. Anthony North Hospital and a large professional services community serving one of the Denver metro's fastest-growing residential populations. This economic diversity makes Adams County a market where marketing strategy must address multiple buyer types and business categories simultaneously.
