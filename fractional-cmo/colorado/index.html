What a Fractional CMO Does for Colorado Companies

Colorado has one of the most dynamic growth-stage business ecosystems in the US. Denver has become a top-10 city for venture investment, with particular strength in SaaS, healthtech, clean energy, and aerospace. Boulder hosts a world-class outdoor and lifestyle brand cluster including companies like Crocs, Vail Resorts, and dozens of emerging DTC brands. Colorado Springs has a significant defense and aerospace presence anchored by major military installations and defense contractors.

Colorado companies between $2M and $50M face the same challenge as growth-stage businesses nationally: the need for senior marketing strategy outpaces the budget for a full-time CMO. A fractional CMO at $8,000 to $15,000 per month provides C-suite marketing direction at a cost that fits the stage of the business - and brings cross-industry experience from working with multiple companies simultaneously.

Fractional CMO Colorado - Frequently Asked Questions

What does a fractional CMO in Colorado cost?

A fractional CMO in Colorado typically costs $8,000 to $20,000 per month on retainer, with project sprints starting at $8,000. Pricing scales with engagement scope and hours per week.

Do you work with Denver tech startups?

Yes. Denver's tech and SaaS ecosystem has grown significantly over the past decade. Many engagements are with Series A and B companies in the Denver metro that need a senior marketing leader without a full-time CMO cost.

Can you help Boulder outdoor and lifestyle brands?

Yes. Boulder has one of the densest concentrations of outdoor, wellness, and consumer lifestyle brands in the country. Engagements in this sector typically focus on brand architecture, DTC digital acquisition, and retail channel strategy.

How quickly can a fractional CMO start in Colorado?

Typically 1-2 weeks from first conversation to active engagement - versus 4-6 months for a full-time CMO hire.

Do you serve all Colorado markets including Colorado Springs and Fort Collins?

Yes. All Colorado markets are served - Denver metro, Colorado Springs, Boulder, Fort Collins, Pueblo, Greeley, and all markets statewide. Engagements are remote-first with on-site availability for key sessions.
