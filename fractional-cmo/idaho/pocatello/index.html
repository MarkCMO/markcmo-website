Pocatello's Business Landscape

Pocatello has a layered economic history that continues to define its present. From its origins as a Union Pacific railroad hub to its current role as a university city and manufacturing center, Pocatello has built a resilient and diversified economy. Idaho State University's health sciences programs, engineering departments, and research activities generate both employment and technology commercialization opportunities. The healthcare sector around Portneuf Medical Center supports a wide range of medical practices and service businesses. Manufacturing - particularly ON Semiconductor's significant local operation - creates industrial supply chain opportunities for local vendors.
