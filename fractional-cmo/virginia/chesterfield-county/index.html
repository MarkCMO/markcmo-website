Chesterfield County's Suburban Richmond Economy

Chesterfield County's growth story is driven by its position as the preferred suburban destination for Richmond families and businesses seeking lower taxes, newer infrastructure, and better access to I-95 and I-288 than Richmond city proper provides. The Midlothian corridor has become one of Virginia's premier retail and professional services destinations, with healthcare, financial services, legal, and home services businesses building large customer bases serving the county's affluent residential population. The Chester and Walthall industrial areas support light manufacturing, distribution, and logistics operations with Interstate access to Mid-Atlantic markets.

The healthcare sector is particularly significant. Financial services, real estate, and construction businesses have thrived as Chesterfield's development boom has sustained residential and commercial construction activity at levels that outpace Virginia state averages.
