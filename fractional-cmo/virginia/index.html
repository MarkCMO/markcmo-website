Frequently Asked Questions

Can a fractional CMO help Virginia defense contractors with commercial market expansion?

Yes. Many Virginia government contractors reach a ceiling on federal revenue and want to enter commercial markets with their technology or capabilities. A fractional CMO can build the commercial go-to-market strategy, create buyer personas for private sector audiences, and develop the messaging and channels required to compete outside the federal space.

How much does a fractional CMO cost in Virginia?

Fractional CMO engagements in Virginia start at $8,000 for a focused sprint. Monthly retainers range from $8,000 to $20,000 based on scope, hours, and team leadership responsibilities. Equity-blended structures are available for high-growth technology companies and funded startups.

How quickly can a fractional CMO engagement start in Virginia?

Most engagements launch within one to two weeks. A discovery call, a scope summary, and a retainer agreement are all that is required - no lengthy procurement processes or vendor registration requirements.

Do you work with Northern Virginia technology companies targeting government contracts?

Yes. Marketing to federal buyers requires a different approach than commercial B2B demand generation. That includes understanding procurement timelines, GSA schedule positioning, capability statement development, and the role of industry days and trade publications in federal pipeline development.

What size Virginia company benefits most from a fractional CMO?

The strongest fit is typically a company with $2M to $50M in revenue that has outgrown a marketing manager but cannot justify a full-time CMO. This includes Northern Virginia IT services firms, Richmond financial services companies, and Hampton Roads defense technology businesses expanding commercial revenue.
