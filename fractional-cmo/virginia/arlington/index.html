Arlington's Business Landscape

Arlington's economy operates at one of the most complex intersections of government and private enterprise in the world. The Pentagon's location in Arlington anchors a defense ecosystem that extends across Northern Virginia, while the Rosslyn-Ballston corridor is one of the densest concentrations of professional services and technology employment in the country. Amazon's decision to locate HQ2 in Arlington's National Landing has added a commercial technology dimension that is reshaping the talent market and attracting related tech businesses across cloud, e-commerce, and logistics technology.

Defense Contracting and Federal IT

The Pentagon, located in Arlington, is the physical and symbolic center of one of the largest procurement ecosystems in the world. Defense contractors ranging from the largest prime contractors to small businesses with specialized capabilities compete for billions in annual DoD spending.
