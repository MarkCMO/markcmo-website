Norfolk's Business Landscape

Norfolk's economy is anchored by two institutions of global significance - Naval Station Norfolk and the Port of Virginia - that collectively drive more commercial activity in Hampton Roads than any other economic forces. The city's downtown has undergone significant revitalization, and a growing technology and professional services sector is emerging alongside the traditional defense and maritime economy. Old Dominion University's research programs and business school contribute both innovation and talent to an economy that is actively diversifying beyond its defense and port foundations.

Naval Station and Defense Industry

Naval Station Norfolk is the world's largest naval installation, homeporting more than 75 ships and 150 aircraft and supporting tens of thousands of active duty personnel and their families. The economic ripple effects of this installation extend across the entire Hampton Roads region and create demand for defense technology, maintenance and repair services, logistics support, professional training, and information technology across dozens of specialized verticals. Companies serving the Naval Station Norfolk mission compete in one of the most demanding procurement environments in the country, where performance reliability and mission-critical credibility are non-negotiable marketing prerequisites.

Port of Virginia and Maritime Commerce

The Port of Virginia, operating primarily through Norfolk International Terminals and the Newport News Marine Terminal, is one of the fastest-growing containerized cargo ports in the United States. The port's deepwater channel accommodates the world's largest container ships, and its connectivity to the inland rail network through the Norfolk Southern and CSX systems makes it a critical gateway for Midwest and Southeast import and export commerce. Maritime services companies, freight forwarders, customs brokers, logistics technology vendors, and port operations specialists all compete actively in this ecosystem for commercial accounts and government contracts associated with port security and operations.

Healthcare and Academic Medicine

Sentara Norfolk General Hospital and Eastern Virginia Medical School jointly operate one of the most respected academic medicine programs in the South Atlantic region. EVMS's research programs and Sentara's health system scale create a healthcare ecosystem that demands sophisticated vendor and technology partners. The intersection of military medicine - with the Navy's large patient population in Hampton Roads - and academic clinical care creates a unique market environment where vendors must demonstrate both clinical excellence and operational efficiency standards relevant to high-volume, multi-payer healthcare delivery systems.
