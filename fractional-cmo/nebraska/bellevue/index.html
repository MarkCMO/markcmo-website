Bellevue's Business Landscape

Bellevue's business landscape is shaped almost entirely by Offutt Air Force Base and the contractor ecosystem that has grown up around it over decades. USSTRATCOM, which manages the nation's nuclear forces and space capabilities, drives billions of dollars in annual contracting activity across information technology, cybersecurity, intelligence analysis, engineering, and operational support. The civilian workforce at Offutt supplements military personnel in roles requiring specialized technical expertise. Healthcare, retail, and professional services businesses serve the residential population - a highly educated, security-cleared workforce with higher-than-average incomes - that has made Bellevue one of Nebraska's most prosperous communities.

Defense and Intelligence Contracting

USSTRATCOM and Offutt AFB generate substantial annual contracting activity across IT systems, intelligence analysis, program management, engineering, and operational logistics. The prime contractors and subcontractors operating in this environment compete for task orders under major contract vehicles with highly specific technical and security requirements. Marketing in the defense contracting space means building capability statements, maintaining compliance posture, and developing the relationship infrastructure with program offices and contracting officers that leads to competitive advantage on future awards.

Cybersecurity and Information Technology

USSTRATCOM's mission requires world-class cybersecurity and information systems capabilities, creating one of the highest concentrations of cleared cybersecurity professionals outside of Washington DC and Northern Virginia. Technology companies - both prime contractors and commercial firms hoping to expand into the defense market - operate in Bellevue's military corridor with a focus on FedRAMP compliance, DoD IL4/5 capabilities, and Zero Trust architecture implementation. These companies require marketing that demonstrates technical depth, compliance credentials, and mission-aligned positioning.

Healthcare and Civilian Services

The residential population surrounding Offutt - predominantly active duty military, veterans, and defense contractor employees with strong employment stability - creates a healthy demand for healthcare, financial, and professional services. The VA Nebraska-Western Iowa Health Care System, along with commercial providers serving the military community, anchors a healthcare market with unique needs around military-specific health conditions, benefit navigation, and transition services for separating service members. Companies serving this population benefit from marketing that demonstrates genuine understanding of the military community.
