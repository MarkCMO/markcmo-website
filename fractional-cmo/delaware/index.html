What a Fractional CMO Does for Delaware Companies

Delaware is the legal home of more than 60% of Fortune 500 companies, making it one of the most important corporate registration states in the US. Beyond the legal services sector, Delaware has a significant real business economy anchored by Wilmington's financial services cluster - including major credit card companies like JPMorgan Chase and Bank of America's card divisions - as well as a growing biotech and pharmaceutical sector in the Wilmington and Newark corridor. DuPont's long industrial legacy has seeded a strong advanced materials and chemicals sector as well.

Delaware companies between $2M and $30M in revenue benefit from a fractional CMO who understands both the highly competitive Mid-Atlantic market and how to build marketing programs that compete against the large national and global companies Delaware companies frequently work alongside.
