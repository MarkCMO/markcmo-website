Spartanburg's Business Landscape

Spartanburg County's economy is a testament to what focused economic development and a commitment to manufacturing excellence can produce. Starting from a textile base that largely disappeared in the 1990s, Spartanburg rebuilt around automotive supply chain, advanced materials, and international business development - achieving a global business density that rivals cities many times its size. Companies from over 40 nations operate here, drawn by the BMW anchor, the region's skilled workforce, and business incentives that make South Carolina one of the most competitive manufacturing states in America. The result is a B2B market where technical standards are high, relationships matter, and quality is not negotiable.

Automotive Supply Chain

Spartanburg is ground zero for BMW's South Carolina supply chain, with dozens of Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers located within a 60-mile radius. Companies selling into this ecosystem need marketing that communicates manufacturing excellence and quality system compliance in language that resonates with German-influenced procurement culture.
