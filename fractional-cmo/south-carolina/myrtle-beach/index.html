Myrtle Beach's Business Landscape

Myrtle Beach has evolved from a pure leisure destination into a genuine multi-sector economy. The tourism foundation remains dominant, but the city's rapid population growth - driven by retirees, remote workers, and families attracted by affordable coastal living - has generated significant demand for healthcare, professional services, and retail that serves a permanent population rather than just seasonal visitors. The Grand Strand's 90+ golf courses, hundreds of hotels, and thriving entertainment district create a B2B ecosystem that is both large and concentrated enough for specialized vendors to build sustainable businesses without needing to go national.
