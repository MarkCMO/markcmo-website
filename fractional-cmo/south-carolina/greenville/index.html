Greenville's Business Landscape

Greenville's economic story is one of the most compelling transformations in American manufacturing history. A city that once anchored the US textile industry rebuilt itself around precision manufacturing, automotive production, and international business development - becoming one of the most globally connected mid-sized cities in North America in the process. Today's Greenville economy is defined by quality, technical sophistication, and international commerce. The downtown revitalization - consistently ranked among the best in the country - has created a quality-of-life foundation that helps the region attract the skilled workforce that advanced manufacturing requires, and that increasingly draws technology and financial services companies as well.

BMW's largest global manufacturing plant and the supplier ecosystem it anchors have made Greenville a world-class advanced manufacturing hub.
