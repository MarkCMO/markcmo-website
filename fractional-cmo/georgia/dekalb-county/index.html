DeKalb County's Business Landscape

DeKalb County serves as Atlanta's eastern anchor, combining institutional giants like Emory and CHOA with a thriving small business ecosystem and an increasingly vibrant technology sector. The county's proximity to downtown Atlanta, combined with its own strong commercial base in Decatur and Tucker, creates diverse marketing opportunities across B2B and B2C markets.

Healthcare and Life Sciences

Emory Healthcare is one of the Southeast's largest health systems, and Children's Healthcare of Atlanta consistently ranks among the nation's top pediatric hospitals. This concentration drives significant vendor and supplier marketing activity, plus a robust health-tech startup ecosystem. Companies selling into healthcare institutions here face complex procurement processes that reward patient, relationship-driven marketing strategies.

Emory EcosystemHealth-Tech

Higher Education and Research

Emory University and Georgia State University create substantial markets for educational technology, student services, research tools, and professional development. The academic calendar drives unique marketing rhythms, and reaching decision-makers in academia requires an entirely different playbook than corporate B2B - one built on thought leadership, peer credibility, and long-term relationship nurturing.

Ed-TechResearch Services

Professional Services and Technology

DeKalb County hosts a growing ecosystem of professional services firms, technology companies, and creative agencies - many of which serve the county's large institutional employers. The Decatur business district in particular has become a hub for boutique consulting, marketing, and technology companies that benefit from proximity to Emory and Atlanta while maintaining lower overhead than downtown Fulton County locations.
