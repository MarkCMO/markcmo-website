Fulton County's Business Landscape

Fulton County is Georgia's commercial capital, spanning Atlanta's urban core through affluent northern suburbs. The county hosts more Fortune 500 headquarters than any other county in the Southeast, with dominant clusters in technology, financial services, and healthcare. The presence of Hartsfield-Jackson International Airport - the world's busiest - makes Fulton County a global logistics and business hub with unmatched connectivity.

Technology and SaaS

Atlanta has earned the nickname "Transaction Alley" for its dominance in fintech, and Fulton County sits at its center. Companies like NCR, Global Payments, and hundreds of SaaS firms call this county home.
