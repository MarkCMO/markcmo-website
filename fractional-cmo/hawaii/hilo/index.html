Hilo's Business Landscape

Hilo is Hawaii County's commercial and government hub, with a diverse economy that blends research infrastructure, agricultural production, healthcare services, and nature-based tourism. The city's economy is shaped by institutions and sectors with distinct marketing needs that require specialized expertise.
