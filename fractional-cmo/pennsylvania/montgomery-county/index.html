Montgomery County's Business Landscape

Montgomery County's economy is defined by its pharmaceutical and life sciences concentration, supported by major financial services and technology operations along the King of Prussia corridor. Pharmaceuticals and Life Sciences

Merck, GSK, and their vast vendor ecosystems make Montgomery County a global pharmaceutical hub. Contract research organizations, contract manufacturing organizations, laboratory services companies, regulatory consultants, and clinical trial management firms all cluster here to be close to their largest clients.
