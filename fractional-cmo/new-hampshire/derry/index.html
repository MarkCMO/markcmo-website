Derry's Business Landscape

Derry's economy reflects its dual identity as a Rockingham County community with deep roots in New England manufacturing and as a Route 93 corridor node serving the broader southern New Hampshire and northern Massachusetts technology ecosystem. The town's population of approximately 34,000 supports a diverse commercial economy ranging from electronics manufacturing and plastics fabrication to healthcare services, retail, and professional services. Derry's proximity to Manchester-Boston Regional Airport, its access to both Interstate 93 and Route 28, and its commuting distance from the Massachusetts border give Derry companies geographic flexibility that many similarly sized New England communities cannot match.
