What is the difference between a fractional CMO and a marketing consultant in Texas?
A marketing consultant delivers a recommendation or report. A fractional CMO owns the marketing strategy, manages the team and agencies, is accountable to the CEO for revenue outcomes, and stays engaged to execute - not just advise. The fractional CMO is an executive relationship, not a vendor relationship.
