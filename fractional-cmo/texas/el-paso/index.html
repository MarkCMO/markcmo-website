El Paso's Business Landscape

El Paso is the largest US city on the US-Mexico border and a critical node in the North American supply chain. Its economy is powered by cross-border manufacturing, military operations at Fort Bliss, healthcare, and a retail sector serving a binational consumer base of more than 2.7 million people in the Paso del Norte region.

Cross-Border Manufacturing and Logistics

El Paso's proximity to Juarez maquiladora parks makes it a critical logistics and supply chain hub for manufacturers in automotive, electronics, and consumer goods. Companies operating cross-border need marketing that speaks to procurement officers on both sides of the border.

Military and Defense Contracting

Fort Bliss is one of the largest US Army installations in the country, generating significant defense contracting activity across logistics, training, technology, and facilities services. Marketing to government and military buyers requires credentialing, compliance, and relationship-first strategy.

The Paso del Norte region healthcare market serves a large and medically underserved binational population. Major health systems compete for patients from both El Paso and Juarez. The retail sector similarly serves a dual-market consumer base with significant cross-border purchasing patterns.
