What a Fractional CMO Does for Arizona Companies

Arizona has been one of the fastest-growing state economies in the US over the past decade. The Phoenix metro - now the fifth-largest city in the country - has attracted a significant wave of corporate relocations from California and the Midwest, bringing financial services companies, technology firms, and professional services businesses that have shifted their headquarters or established major operations there. The semiconductor manufacturing boom anchored by TSMC and Intel has added engineering and industrial depth to a market already strong in real estate, healthcare, and tourism.

Tucson adds aerospace and defense manufacturing, a University of Arizona research anchor, and a growing technology sector. Scottsdale's concentration of wealth management, real estate, and hospitality companies rounds out one of the most diverse state economies in the Sun Belt.

For Arizona companies between $2M and $50M in revenue, a fractional CMO provides the senior marketing leadership this rapidly maturing market increasingly demands - without the full-time cost that a company at this stage typically cannot absorb.
