Pierce County's Industrial and Military Economy

Joint Base Lewis-McChord anchors Pierce County's economy with tens of thousands of active-duty personnel, a large civilian workforce, and a contractor ecosystem that spans logistics, engineering, IT services, and professional support. The Port of Tacoma, one of the top container ports on the West Coast, drives international trade and creates sustained demand for freight, warehousing, customs brokerage, and supply chain services. MultiCare Health System and other regional healthcare providers represent a growing civilian employment base that extends well beyond the military community.

Tacoma's downtown and the surrounding suburban cities of Puyallup, Lakewood, and Federal Way house a diverse small and mid-size business community in construction, retail, professional services, and food manufacturing. Companies here often find themselves competing for both military-adjacent contracts and conventional commercial customers, requiring marketing strategies that speak to government procurement officers and private buyers simultaneously. That dual-market complexity is where a seasoned fractional CMO delivers the most leverage.

In Pierce County's mixed economy, that means building differentiated positioning whether you are pursuing federal contract vehicles, commercial logistics accounts, or healthcare patients.
