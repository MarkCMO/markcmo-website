Frequently Asked Questions

How do you position a Washington state company against Amazon or Microsoft?

Competing against platform giants requires sharp category positioning, a well-defined buyer persona, and a differentiated value proposition that goes beyond feature comparison. Successful positioning often focuses on specialization, service depth, integration flexibility, or vertical expertise that platforms cannot replicate at scale.

How much does a fractional CMO cost in Washington state?

Fractional CMO engagements in Washington start at $8,000 for a sprint. Monthly retainers range from $8,000 to $20,000 based on scope, hours, and team leadership. Equity-blended structures are available for Seattle and Bellevue startups and high-growth technology companies.

How quickly can a fractional CMO engagement start in Washington state?

Most engagements launch within one to two weeks. A brief discovery call, a scope summary, and a retainer agreement are all that is required to get started.

Do you work with Washington state e-commerce and consumer brands?

Yes. Washington has a strong cohort of consumer product and e-commerce companies that have outgrown their initial growth channels and need a senior marketing operator to build the next stage of customer acquisition, retention, and brand equity. This includes DTC brands, marketplace-native companies, and omnichannel retailers.

What size Washington company benefits most from a fractional CMO?

The strongest fit is typically a company with $2M to $50M in revenue that has outgrown a generalist marketing manager but cannot justify a full-time CMO. This includes Bellevue SaaS companies, Seattle consumer brands, Spokane healthcare firms, and eastern Washington agriculture and food businesses.
