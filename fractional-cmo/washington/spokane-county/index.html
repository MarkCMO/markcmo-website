Spokane County's Regional Economy and Business Landscape

Spokane is Washington's second-largest city and the commercial center for a regional economy that draws customers and clients from a wide geographic footprint. The healthcare sector is the dominant employer, anchored by Providence Sacred Heart Medical Center, MultiCare Deaconess, and the WSU Elson S. Floyd College of Medicine. The Spokane area has become a national model for regional medical school development, with the health sciences campus creating a research and clinical trial ecosystem that supports biotech and life sciences growth.

Fairchild Air Force Base adds a defense dimension, supporting contracting and logistics businesses. The agricultural processing industry connects Spokane to the wheat fields of the Columbia Basin, generating demand for food manufacturing, equipment, and input supply companies. Technology has grown substantially through the University District innovation hub where WSU and Gonzaga graduates launch software, engineering, and professional services firms. This diverse economic base creates a Spokane business environment that rewards marketing sophistication and regional brand authority.
