Snohomish County's Aerospace and Technology Economy

The Boeing Everett Delivery Center is the physical and economic spine of Snohomish County. The 787 Dreamliner, 777X, and other programs generate supply chain demand that ripples across the county and supports hundreds of tier-one and tier-two suppliers who need differentiated positioning to win Boeing work and diversify into adjacent aerospace markets. Naval Station Everett adds a defense dimension, while the Port of Everett handles cargo and industrial marine activity that connects regional manufacturers to global markets.

Technology has become a second anchor sector. Alstom, Premera Blue Cross, and a growing cluster of software and engineering firms have chosen Snohomish County for headquarters and operations. Healthcare systems including Providence Regional Medical Center Everett and Seattle Children's outpatient locations serve a rapidly growing residential population that has made cities like Bothell and Mill Creek among the fastest-growing in western Washington. The result is a county economy that requires marketing leadership capable of operating across aerospace, technology, and healthcare verticals.
