Clark County's Bi-State Business Advantage

Clark County's economy is fundamentally shaped by its position at the Washington-Oregon border. Businesses here serve customers in both states and compete with companies based in both Portland and Seattle. Legacy manufacturing anchored by businesses in Ridgefield and Battle Ground coexists with a growing technology and professional services sector in Vancouver proper. PeaceHealth Southwest Medical Center and Legacy Salmon Creek Medical Center serve a healthcare economy that draws patients from both sides of the Columbia River.

The county's rapid population growth, driven by Oregonians seeking Washington's tax environment, has created a consumer and B2B market that demands marketing sophistication. Companies relocating from Portland often need to rebuild brand awareness in a new primary market while retaining their Oregon customer base. Companies long established in Clark County often need to expand their reach northward toward Seattle or southward into Portland's larger B2B economy. A fractional CMO brings the strategic framework to navigate both challenges simultaneously.

Technology and Professional Services

Clark County's growing technology sector spans managed IT, software, engineering, and financial services firms. B2B marketing strategy targeting both Portland and Seattle enterprise buyers builds pipeline across the broader Pacific Northwest economy from a lower-cost Clark County base.

Healthcare and Medical Services

PeaceHealth, Legacy, and a growing network of specialty practices serve Clark County's rapidly growing population. Healthcare marketing, patient acquisition, and physician referral development programs build volume in a market underserved relative to its population growth trajectory.

Manufacturing and Industrial

Clark County's industrial base includes food processing, metal fabrication, and building materials companies with regional distribution footprints. Marketing strategy that builds distributor relationships, drives direct commercial accounts, and establishes category authority accelerates revenue growth in these sectors.
