Montgomery's Business Landscape

Montgomery's economy is defined by the intersection of government authority and industrial manufacturing - two sectors with very different buying behaviors, procurement timelines, and vendor evaluation criteria. State government generates consistent, predictable procurement activity across technology, professional services, and facilities management. Alabama state government employs tens of thousands in Montgomery, and Maxwell Air Force Base and Gunter Annex add significant military and defense contractor presence.
