Birmingham's Business Landscape

Birmingham is Alabama's largest city and most economically diverse metro - a place where a world-class academic medical center, a Fortune 500 bank, and a growing startup ecosystem coexist within a few miles of each other. The city's transformation from industrial to knowledge economy is largely complete, and the companies that have emerged from that transition are increasingly ready to compete on the national stage. What most of them lack is the marketing infrastructure to do it.

Healthcare and Medical Research

UAB Hospital is one of the top-ranked academic medical centers in the Southeast, and the ecosystem surrounding it - including medical device companies, healthcare IT vendors, and clinical research organizations - represents billions in procurement activity. Companies selling into this ecosystem need marketing that communicates clinical evidence, compliance credentials, and the ability to integrate with complex hospital information systems. The UAB brand carries significant weight, and vendors who can demonstrate deep familiarity with academic medical center buying processes have a significant advantage.
