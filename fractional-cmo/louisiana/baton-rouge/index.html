Baton Rouge's Business Landscape

Baton Rouge sits at the convergence of industrial might, government authority, and university-anchored research. The city's economy is one of the most concentrated in the South - dominated by the petrochemical industry along the river corridor, complemented by state government as a major employer and procurement organization, and diversified by LSU's research programs and healthcare systems serving over one million people in the greater metro area.

Petrochemical and Industrial

The Baton Rouge petrochemical corridor is one of the densest concentrations of industrial manufacturing in the world. ExxonMobil, Shell, and BASF maintain refineries and chemical plants here that require thousands of vendors across safety, maintenance, engineering, and technology. Companies selling into this market need marketing that leads with technical capability, regulatory compliance credentials, and verifiable safety track records - the things industrial procurement teams actually evaluate.

Government and Professional Services

As Louisiana's capital, Baton Rouge hosts state government operations, the state legislature, and dozens of professional services firms serving government procurement. The state is one of the largest buyers of technology, consulting, and professional services in Louisiana. Companies targeting government contracts need marketing that builds the name recognition and trust required to win through competitive procurement processes - a discipline that requires specific expertise in public sector positioning.

Higher Education and Healthcare

LSU's flagship campus and the LSU Health Sciences Center anchor significant research and healthcare activity. Our Lady of the Lake Regional Medical Center and Baton Rouge General are major health systems driving B2B demand for clinical technology, medical supplies, and healthcare services. The university itself is a major buyer of technology, professional services, and facilities solutions - making the education and healthcare sectors a significant opportunity for well-positioned vendors in the Baton Rouge market.
