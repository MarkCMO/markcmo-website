Lafayette's Business Landscape

Lafayette sits at the center of the Gulf of Mexico offshore energy ecosystem - the most technically complex and commercially significant offshore oil and gas market in North America. The city's economy is anchored by hundreds of oilfield services companies that support deepwater and shelf production, complemented by a healthcare system serving the Acadiana region, a growing startup community built around energy technology, and the hospitality and retail economy that serves the region's working population.

Oilfield Services and Energy Technology

Lafayette is the undisputed hub of Gulf of Mexico offshore services.
