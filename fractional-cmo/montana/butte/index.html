Butte's Business Landscape

Butte's economic identity is inseparable from its mining past - but the city has built a forward-looking economy around the same industrial capabilities that made it the "Richest Hill on Earth." Montana Tech University's engineering and environmental science programs create a pipeline of specialized talent. The Berkeley Pit Superfund cleanup, one of the largest in US history, has generated a permanent environmental services industry. The energy transmission infrastructure that traverses Silver Bow County positions Butte for the grid investments that are reshaping the western United States. Heritage tourism draws visitors who appreciate the city's extraordinary Victorian-era architecture and labor movement history.
