What Entertainment Marketing Is + Why It Matters

Entertainment marketing covers film + TV + streaming content marketing, music + concert + festival marketing, theatrical + Broadway + live theater marketing, sports + esports entertainment marketing, theme park + experience marketing, gaming entertainment marketing, and celebrity + influencer + creator entertainment marketing. The discipline lives at the intersection of brand marketing, audience development, fan psychology, and rights + licensing + IP management. US entertainment industry is $700B+ annually.

When Entertainment Marketing Is The Right Fit

Entertainment marketing engagements fit: indie film + streaming + TV production companies, music labels + artist management, festivals + live events + venues, theatrical productions + Broadway shows, theme park + experience operators, gaming + esports operators, content creators + influencers + talent management, and entertainment-adjacent ventures (ticketing, merch, fan engagement tech).

Entertainment Marketing FAQ

What is the difference between entertainment marketing and regular marketing?

Entertainment marketing has unique characteristics: (1) launch-driven cadence (movie release, album drop, season premiere, festival weekend) vs steady-state product marketing, (2) audience + fan psychology dominates over transactional buyer psychology, (3) IP + rights + licensing constraints govern what can be said + shown + used, (4) press + critics + reviews materially affect commercial outcomes, (5) word-of-mouth + social + earned media often outperform paid for entertainment products, (6) talent involvement (actors, musicians, creators) drives marketing access + tone, (7) PR + publicity is as important as paid marketing.

How do streaming platforms market their original content?

Streaming originals marketing playbook: (1) Trailer + teaser drops on YouTube + social with 30-60 day pre-launch window, (2) PR + press junkets + cast interviews to drive earned coverage in entertainment trade press (Variety, Hollywood Reporter, Deadline) + mainstream media, (3) social campaign + memeable moments to drive organic spread, (4) targeted ads to lookalike audiences based on subscriber data, (5) influencer + creator partnerships for reaction + review content, (6) festival + premiere events for industry buzz, (7) post-launch sustaining campaign if content is performing. Netflix + Disney+ + HBO Max spend $50M+ per major original.

How do indie films market on small budgets?

Indie film marketing on $25K-$200K budget: (1) Film festival circuit (Sundance, SXSW, Tribeca, Cannes, Toronto) builds buzz + sells distribution, (2) Press junkets + journalist interviews at festivals + post-acquisition, (3) Social-first launch with trailer + clips on YouTube + Instagram + TikTok, (4) Influencer + film TikTok + YouTube creator partnerships, (5) Targeted Letterboxd + Reddit r/movies + film Twitter community engagement, (6) Strategic limited theatrical (NYC + LA + 5-10 cities) to qualify for awards + reviews, (7) Streaming exclusive deal or VOD launch with platform-specific marketing support.

What is the role of TikTok in entertainment marketing?

TikTok is now the #1 entertainment discovery platform for Gen Z + younger Millennials. Movies, TV shows, music releases, books, and games regularly go viral on TikTok months after their original release ("Stranger Things" Kate Bush "Running Up That Hill" effect). Entertainment marketing on TikTok requires: working with creators native to the platform vs forcing celebrities to post, allowing creators creative freedom to participate authentically, embracing meme + trend culture rather than fighting it, building hashtag challenges + sounds + filters, and accepting that some campaigns will not work while a single piece of content can drive 100M+ views organically.
