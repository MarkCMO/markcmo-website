New York Marketing Agency: The 2026 Buyer's Guide

NYC is the global capital of finance + media + advertising. Goldman, JP Morgan, BlackRock, WPP, Omnicom, IPG, Conde Nast, NYT, plus DTC ecommerce + fashion + pharma. Below: top 9 NY agencies, holding company AOR landscape, DTC specialists.

The Short Answer

NYC is the most expensive US marketing market - tier-one agencies command $100K-$500K/mo retainers. For $2M-$25M companies, the right move is usually operator-led remote with NYC specialist firms layered in for finance + fashion + media-specific work. Holding company AOR is overkill below $500M revenue.

NY's 5 Dominant Industries

  1. Finance. Goldman Sachs HQ, JP Morgan Chase HQ, Morgan Stanley HQ, Citigroup HQ, BlackRock HQ ($10T+ AUM), KKR, Blackstone, Apollo, Carlyle.
  2. Media + advertising HQ. WPP, Omnicom, IPG, Publicis, Dentsu NYC operations. Conde Nast, Hearst, NYT, Bloomberg, WSJ, Fox, NBC, Disney/ABC.
  3. Fashion. 7th Ave Fashion District, plus fashion media (Vogue, ELLE, Harper's Bazaar).
  4. Tech + DTC. Google NYC (10K+), Meta, Amazon, Apple. DTC capital (Warby Parker, Casper, Glossier HQ, Allbirds NY, Peloton).
  5. Pharma. Pfizer HQ, Regeneron (Tarrytown), BMS significant NY operations.
