5-Step Prep

Do this before the call.

You don't need a deck. You don't need a presentation. You need clear answers to these 5 things so Mark can spend the 30 minutes diagnosing your business, not interviewing you.

1
Know your numbers
Current annual revenue range, monthly MRR if SaaS, and your primary growth metric (pipeline, leads, conversions). Round numbers are fine - you don't need to pull dashboards.
2
Name the bottleneck
What's the ONE thing that, if fixed, unlocks the next stage of growth? Lead volume? Conversion rate? Sales cycle? Retention? Pick the biggest one - that's where Mark goes deep.
3
List what you've already tried
Agencies, hires, channels, tools. What worked, what didn't, what's still running. This stops Mark from suggesting things you've already burned cycles on.
4
Define the 12-month goal
Where do you want to be by next year - revenue, headcount, market position? A specific number anchors the entire conversation. "Grow" isn't a goal. "$5M ARR by Q3" is.
5
Have your calendar open
If there's a clear fit, Mark may suggest a follow-up working session. Having availability in front of you lets you lock the next step without a 6-email back-and-forth.
