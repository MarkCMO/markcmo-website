Community Marketing Agency: The 2026 Guide

Owned communities create network effects that compound for years and become moats competitors cannot replicate. Below: top 9 community marketing agencies, platform decision matrix (Slack vs Discord vs Circle vs Skool), real pricing, and the community-led growth thesis.

The Short Answer

For B2B SaaS customer communities, Slack wins. For developer + creator communities, Discord. For paid courses + memberships, Circle or Skool. For course + mobile-app communities, Mighty Networks. Skip Facebook Groups - the platform captures the data, you don't.
