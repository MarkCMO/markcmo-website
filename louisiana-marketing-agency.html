Louisiana's Dominant Industries

Louisiana runs on oil + gas + petrochemicals. Refineries + LNG export terminals along Mississippi River + Gulf Coast (ExxonMobil Baton Rouge - one of largest US refining complexes, Shell Norco, Chevron Pascagoula, Marathon, Valero, plus Cheniere Energy LNG export Sabine Pass + Plaquemines - Cheniere is largest US LNG exporter). Plus Entergy HQ (New Orleans - utility serving Louisiana + Mississippi + Arkansas + Texas, $12B+ revenue). New Orleans tourism + hospitality + Mardi Gras + French Quarter + jazz heritage + Saints (NFL) + Pelicans (NBA). Port of New Orleans + Port of South Louisiana = largest US tonnage ports (grain export + petrochemical export + bulk + container). Film tax credits drive Louisiana film production (Hollywood South historical - True Detective, NCIS New Orleans, dozens of films). LSU Baton Rouge (Tigers football powerhouse, major university). Louisiana has unique French civil law tradition (vs common law everywhere else in US). Plus Tulane University New Orleans. Plus Mississippi River barge logistics. Plus Avondale + Edison Chouest shipyards.
