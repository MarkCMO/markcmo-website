The problem most consultants + coaches have with on-camera content

The hardest part about recording video is not the camera. It is the script. Expertise demonstration on camera, structured framework delivery, looking like an authority - and the result is take after take after take, choppy editing, or worse, content that never gets recorded because the friction is too high. Most consultants + coaches either avoid video entirely or hire a producer + editor to mask the awkwardness in post.

Prompterly fixes this. It turns the iPhone or iPad already in your hand into a professional teleprompter. Write the script, hit record, and read it off the screen while filming. The text scrolls at your pace (or scrolls based on your voice via voice-sync mode). Take 1 becomes the keeper. Editing time collapses. Video shipping becomes possible.

What consultants + coaches use Prompterly for

The most common Prompterly workflows for consultants + coaches: expertise videos, case study breakdowns, course modules, sales videos, free training videos. These are video formats consultants + coaches create on a regular basis - and Prompterly makes them shippable in one take instead of fifteen.

How Prompterly works

  • Write or paste a script. Type directly in the app or paste from notes, docs, email - whatever your workflow already is.
  • Tap record. The script scrolls in front of the camera while video records.
  • Read it. Look at the camera. Read the text. The text follows your pace.
  • Save the video. Saved to your Photos app. Ready to upload to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, your website - wherever.

Why Prompterly beats other teleprompter apps

1. Built by an operator, not an agency

Prompterly was built by Mark Gabrielli + WETYR Corp - the same people running fractional CMO work for $1M-$100M companies. The app was built because Mark needed it himself for client video work. It is opinionated about what consultants + coaches actually need: scripts, scrolling, recording, saving. No bloat.

2. Voice-sync mode (Pro)

Most teleprompter apps scroll at a fixed speed. If you talk faster than the scroll, you run out of text. If you talk slower, you wait awkwardly. Voice-sync mode listens to your voice and matches the scroll to your pace - the only way to record natural video that does not sound like a teleprompter read.

3. Multi-platform export ready

Records in the format consultants + coaches need for the platform they post on - vertical for TikTok + Reels + Shorts, horizontal for YouTube + LinkedIn + websites. No conversion step required.

4. Privacy

Scripts stay on the device. Nothing uploaded to a server. No account required to use the app. Built without third-party data brokers or open-source AI APIs that send your scripts elsewhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I really record professional video with just my iPhone?

Yes. Most consultants + coaches who use Prompterly report their first video out of the app looks more professional than their previous attempts with a camera + tripod + manual reading. The friction killer is the script. Once scripts scroll in front of the camera while recording, the production value jumps immediately.

Does Prompterly work with external cameras or just the iPhone camera?

Prompterly records using the device camera (front or rear). For consultants + coaches using external cameras (Sony, Canon, Blackmagic), Prompterly works as the teleprompter only - the script displays on the iPhone or iPad next to the external camera. Many consultants + coaches use a tablet mount that puts the iPad in front of the camera lens for true eye-contact reading.

Can I cancel Prompterly subscriptions?

Yes. Cancel anytime in iOS Settings → Subscriptions → Prompterly. No friction. No retention prompts. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.
