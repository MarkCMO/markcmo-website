Illinois Marketing Agency: The 2026 Buyer's Guide

Illinois has 37 Fortune 500 HQs. McDonald's, Walgreens, Caterpillar, Abbott, AbbVie, Allstate, Mondelez, ADM, Kraft Heinz. Plus Chicago ad agency heritage: Leo Burnett, FCB, Ogilvy, DDB, Energy BBDO. CME Group futures capital. Below: top 9 IL agencies.

The Short Answer

Chicago is the historic US ad capital outside NYC. Leo Burnett created the most iconic ad characters in American history (Marlboro Man, Tony the Tiger, Doughboy, Jolly Green Giant). For $2M-$25M companies, holding company AOR is overkill. Operator-led with Chicago specialist firms layered in works better.
