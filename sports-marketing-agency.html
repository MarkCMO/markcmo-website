Sports Marketing Agency: The 2026 Guide

Sports marketing covers athlete endorsements ($10K-$100M+ deals), team sponsorships ($250K-$50M+/year), league partnerships, and the new NIL economy ($1K-$5M+ per college athlete deal). Below: top 9 firms, deal sizing by tier, and the operator-led alternative.

The Short Answer

NIL deals have fundamentally changed college + pro sports marketing. A top NIL earner makes more in college than mid-tier pros make in NBA/NFL salary. Brands now layer NIL deals + pro endorsements + retired-athlete deals to reach fans across the entire athlete career arc.
