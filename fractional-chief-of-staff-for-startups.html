Fractional Chief of Staff for Startups

When a startup should hire a fractional Chief of Staff, by funding stage and runway.

Quick Answer

Post-PMF startups at $1M-$10M revenue benefit most from a fractional Chief of Staff - senior ownership of executive leverage, operating cadence, and cross-functional execution at $5,000-$12,000/month without a full-time hire. Pre-PMF should usually wait. Book a call.

By funding stage

Pre-PMF: focus on product and runway, not senior functional hires. Post-PMF seed ($1M-$5M): bring on a fractional Chief of Staff for focused senior leadership. Series A+ ($5M+): scale the engagement. The fractional model lets startups access Chief of Staff-caliber judgment without committing $180,000-$320,000 a year.

Why fractional fits startups

Startups need senior judgment in bursts, not always full-time presence. A fractional Chief of Staff delivers exactly that - $5,000-$12,000/month, flexible, renewable - so the company gets executive-grade ownership of executive leverage, operating cadence, and cross-functional execution while preserving runway.
