Fractional SEO Expert vs In-House SEO Team

The hire vs fractional decision for SEO splits cleanly by revenue and stage. Build the math, not the gut call.

Quick Answer

Below 25 million dollar revenue, a fractional SEO expert almost always wins on math: 4,000 to 12,000 dollars per month bundled vs 140,000 to 220,000 dollars all-in for a full-time SEO Director plus the recruiting cost. Above 25 million dollar revenue, in-house typically wins because SEO needs daily ownership and dedicated focus on the single brand. Many companies in the 25 to 50 million dollar revenue band run a hybrid: in-house SEO Director plus a fractional advisor or top-tier agency.

Cost Breakdown

Fractional SEO expert (MarkCMO): $48K-$144K per year all-in, no recruiting cost, no tool stack to license separately. In-house SEO Director: $140K-$220K base comp + $20K-$40K recruiting + $10K-$30K tools = $170K-$290K minimum first year, $150K-$250K ongoing.

When Fractional Wins

$2M-$25M revenue, single brand, founder-led company that values direct senior access, situations where SEO is one of several growth levers rather than the dominant channel.

When In-House Wins

$25M+ revenue, multi-brand or multi-region, SEO is the primary growth channel and needs daily ownership, board mandate for a full marketing department.

Hybrid Models

Some 25M-50M companies run a hybrid: in-house SEO Director for daily ownership plus a fractional advisor (like MarkCMO) for strategic guidance and AI search optimization rollout. Cost: $200K-$300K total. Best of both worlds for the right stage.
