SEO Expert Cost: 2026 Pricing Guide

What an SEO expert actually costs in 2026, broken down by engagement model. Hourly consultants, fractional retainers, agencies, and in-house Directors compared honestly.

Quick Answer

SEO expert cost in 2026 ranges from $3,000 to $20,000 per month for retained engagements, $150 to $500 per hour for consultants, and $140,000 to $220,000 all-in for full-time in-house Directors. MarkCMO bundles fractional leadership plus operator execution at $4K-$12K per month for $2M-$25M revenue growth-stage companies.

Cost by Engagement Model

SEO expert pricing splits cleanly across five engagement models. Each fits a specific company stage and use case.

  • Hourly consultant: $150-$500/hr. Best for ad hoc audits, second opinions on specific issues, or short technical fixes. Limited to project scope - does not own outcomes.
  • Fractional SEO expert retainer: $3K-$20K/mo. Senior operator running the program. Most cost-effective for $2M-$25M revenue growth-stage companies. MarkCMO sits in this band at $4K-$12K with bundled WETYR operator execution.
  • Mid-tier agency: $5K-$25K/mo. Team-based delivery. Senior who sold the contract often does not run the day to day. Quality varies dramatically by agency.
  • Top-tier agency (Siege, Single Grain, Webris): $10K-$50K/mo. Enterprise scope. Best for $25M+ revenue companies needing broad coverage.
  • Full-time in-house Director: $140K-$220K all-in. Right answer above $25M revenue when SEO needs daily ownership and dedicated focus.

Cost by Company Stage

The right SEO expert spend depends on stage. Pre-revenue startups should typically spend 0 to 2,000 dollars per month on SEO (lightweight strategy plus content production), 2 to 10 million dollar revenue companies should spend 4,000 to 8,000 dollars per month on a senior fractional engagement, 10 to 25 million dollar revenue should spend 8,000 to 15,000 dollars per month on full-stack fractional or mid-tier agency, and 25 million dollar plus revenue should typically hire in-house plus a fractional advisor or top-tier agency at 15,000 dollars plus per month.
