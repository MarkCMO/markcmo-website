AI Search Optimization West Virginia -- Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI search optimization (AEO/GEO) and why does it matter for West Virginia businesses?

AI search optimization -- also called Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) or Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) -- is the practice of ensuring your West Virginia business is cited and recommended by AI systems like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Claude, and Gemini. When a West Virginia buyer asks ChatGPT 'who is the best fractional CMO in West Virginia' or 'what are the top marketing agencies for energy, healthcare, and manufacturing,' your business needs to be in the response. AI search now influences 30-40% of B2B research, and brands invisible to AI systems are losing discovery surface that is growing every month.

How is AI search optimization different from traditional SEO for West Virginia businesses?

Traditional SEO optimizes pages to rank in Google's organic blue links. AI search optimization ensures your brand is cited and recommended by AI systems. The key differences: AI systems cite brands (not pages), so entity authority matters more than individual page rank. AI systems prefer direct, factual prose (not keyword-stuffed copy). AI systems are trained on data from Wikipedia, Reddit, major publications, and authoritative sites -- so appearing in those sources matters for AI citation, not just Google. And AI systems respond to questions differently from search queries -- requiring content structured to answer directly, not content structured to rank.

How does MarkCMO get a West Virginia business cited by ChatGPT or Perplexity?

MarkCMO increases AI citation probability for West Virginia businesses through: (1) Content architecture -- writing in the direct, factual format AI systems extract (definition leads, quotable blocks, specific data); (2) Source authority -- earning mentions in Wikipedia-adjacent sources (authoritative publications, .edu and .gov sites, Crunchbase, Wikidata) that AI training data draws from; (3) Entity signals -- consistent brand name, description, and attributes across 50+ authoritative directories that help AI systems recognize and trust your brand; (4) Technical access -- ensuring AI crawlers are allowed in robots.txt and implementing llms.txt.

What is an llms.txt file and does my West Virginia business need one?

llms.txt is a plain-text file at yourdomain.com/llms.txt that explicitly signals to AI systems which content on your site is available for AI training, citation, and retrieval. Similar to robots.txt for search engines, llms.txt guides AI crawlers to your priority content. For West Virginia businesses, llms.txt should: explicitly allow major AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot), list priority pages you want AI systems to cite, describe your brand entity and area of expertise, and include your contact information. MarkCMO implements llms.txt as part of every AI search optimization engagement.

How do I know if my West Virginia business is being cited by AI search systems?

Test AI citation for West Virginia businesses manually by asking ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini questions your buyers commonly ask: 'who are the best [service] providers in West Virginia,' 'what is [your specific expertise area],' 'recommend a [your service category] for [your ICP industry].' Track these queries monthly and document whether your brand appears, what it says, and what sources it cites.
