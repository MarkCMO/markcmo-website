Long Bio (150 words)

Mark Gabrielli is the founder of WETYR Corp and the fractional executive practice MarkCMO, where he serves as outsourced CMO and COO for growth-stage B2B companies across the United States. With over 15 years of executive marketing and operations experience spanning SaaS, healthcare technology, aerospace, fintech, cybersecurity, and manufacturing, Mark has built and scaled marketing functions at companies ranging from pre-revenue startups to $100M+ enterprises. His Revenue Architecture Framework has generated $135M+ in qualified pipeline across client engagements, with clients achieving an average of 300% revenue growth during his tenure. Mark holds a Biological Sciences degree, is a Certified Surgical Technologist, and has completed executive leadership certifications in marketing operations and revenue strategy. He is based in Cape Canaveral, Florida and serves clients nationwide through fractional, interim, and advisory engagements.
