Music Marketing Agency: The 2026 Artist's Guide

TikTok is the new radio. Spotify editorial is the new chart. Below: how indie + mid-tier artists build streaming momentum, the TikTok playbook, top 9 music marketing firms, and the operator-led alternative for artists who can't justify major-label spend.

The Short Answer

Songs go viral on TikTok before they chart anywhere else. If your release strategy doesn't lead with a 15-second hook + creator activation plan, you're working backwards. Build for TikTok first, then optimize for Spotify editorial + press + radio.

Music Marketing Channel Stack

  1. TikTok - primary discovery surface. Sound submission, creator activation, BTS content, snippet strategy, trend riding.
  2. Spotify - streaming platform priority #1. Editorial pitch via Spotify for Artists, Marquee + Discovery Mode, pre-save campaigns.
  3. Apple Music - secondary streaming priority. Editorial pitch, Shazam integration, Beats 1 radio.
  4. YouTube + YouTube Music - long-tail discovery + music videos.
  5. Instagram + Reels - BTS content, fan engagement, music video clips.
  6. Music PR - Pitchfork, Rolling Stone, Billboard, Complex, Stereogum, NME, regional outlets.
  7. Radio promotion - commercial radio (declining importance), college, Sirius/SXM (still meaningful for breaking artists).
  8. Brand partnerships + sync - film, TV, ad, game placements drive both revenue and discovery.
  9. Tour marketing - tour as marketing event, not just revenue. Festival positioning (Coachella, Lollapalooza, ACL, etc.).
