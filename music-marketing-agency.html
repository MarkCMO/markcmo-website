TikTok is the new radio. Spotify editorial is the new chart. Below: how indie + mid-tier artists build streaming momentum, the TikTok playbook, top 9 music marketing firms, and the operator-led alternative for artists who can't justify major-label spend.

The Short Answer

Songs go viral on TikTok before they chart anywhere else. If your release strategy doesn't lead with a 15-second hook + creator activation plan, you're working backwards. Build for TikTok first, then optimize for Spotify editorial + press + radio.

Music Marketing FAQ

How do I market my music as an independent artist in 2026?

DIY artist marketing playbook 2026: (1) consistent release cadence - drop new music every 4-8 weeks to feed streaming + social algorithms, (2) short-form video content on TikTok + Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts (this is where music discovery happens for Gen Z + Millennials), (3) playlist pitching via SubmitHub + Groover + direct curator outreach, (4) email list + Bandcamp for true fans (10x more revenue per fan than streaming), (5) merch + direct-to-fan revenue (touring + merch + Patreon outearn streaming for most independents), (6) live shows + house concerts + small venues to build IRL fanbase, (7) sync licensing pitches for film + TV + ads.

What is the role of Spotify in modern music marketing?

Spotify is dominant but problematic. Pros: 600M+ users, algorithmic discovery via Discover Weekly + Release Radar + Daily Mix, editorial playlists drive massive plays, payouts to artists are predictable per-stream. Cons: per-stream payouts are tiny ($0.003-$0.005 per stream), Spotify favors high-engagement listeners + frequent releases, editorial playlist placement requires industry relationships + Spotify for Artists pitches, fake stream + bot streaming has gotten Spotify aggressive about flagging artists. Best approach: Spotify is one channel - not your business.

How do I build a fanbase from zero as an indie artist?

Build fanbase from zero: (1) niche down - serve a specific subculture + sound + community rather than trying to appeal to everyone, (2) consistent content cadence on the platform your audience uses (TikTok for Gen Z, Instagram for Millennials, YouTube for deeper community), (3) genuine engagement - reply to comments, DM new fans, share other artists in your scene, (4) live shows in your scene - even house concerts for 30 people build true fans, (5) collaborate with other artists in your niche for cross-pollination, (6) capture email + phone numbers at every show + on Bandcamp - those are your real fans. Real fans > streaming numbers.

What is sync licensing and how does it work?

Sync licensing is the practice of licensing your music for use in film, TV, ads, video games, trailers, social media campaigns, and corporate videos. A single sync placement can pay $500-$50,000+ depending on usage. Sync market is dominated by music supervisors (people who pick songs for film + TV) + sync libraries + publishing companies. Indie artists access sync through Songtradr + MusicGorilla + Music Vine + direct music supervisor pitches + sync libraries. Build a catalog of high-quality recordings + register copyrights + master rights with SoundExchange + ASCAP/BMI for collection.
