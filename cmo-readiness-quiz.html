Quick Answer

A CMO Readiness Quiz helps founders and CEOs determine whether their business needs a fractional CMO, a full-time marketing hire, or a marketing agency. Companies typically need a fractional CMO when they have $1M-$50M in revenue, are preparing to scale, have a marketing team but no strategic leadership, or are entering new markets.
