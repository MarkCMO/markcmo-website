Georgia Marketing Agency: The 2026 Buyer's Guide

GA is 8th-largest US economy + 18 F500 HQs. Atlanta: Coca-Cola HQ, Delta HQ, Home Depot HQ, UPS HQ, Cox HQ, CNN HQ. #1 US film state (Marvel, Stranger Things, Tyler Perry Studios). Mailchimp + Calendly fintech. Below: top GA agencies + Hollywood South film specifics.

The Short Answer

Georgia has the most aggressive US film tax credit (30%, no cap), making it #1 US state for film production spend. Atlanta has 18 Fortune 500 HQs - more than any Southeast city. Pick by industry: CPG (Coca-Cola adjacent), logistics (UPS adjacent), film, fintech (Mailchimp + Calendly adjacent), or media.

GA's 6 Dominant Industries

  1. Major brand HQs. Coca-Cola, Delta, Home Depot, UPS, Aflac, Southern Company, Genuine Parts, Truist Atlanta, Cox Enterprises, Coca-Cola Bottling Consolidated.
  2. Film + TV (Hollywood South). #1 US film production state. GA tax credit 30% no cap. Marvel, Stranger Things, Walking Dead, Tyler Perry Studios.
  3. Tech + fintech. Mailchimp HQ (Intuit $12B acquisition), Calendly HQ, MailerLite, Microsoft + Google Atlanta growing.
  4. Media. CNN HQ (Turner Broadcasting heritage), Cox Communications.
  5. Logistics. UPS HQ + Atlanta Southeast distribution hub, Port of Savannah.
  6. Tourism. Savannah historic, Atlanta + coast + mountains.
