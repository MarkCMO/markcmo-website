Marketing Consulting: The 2026 Buyer's Guide

Marketing consulting is advisory work that diagnoses, recommends, and delivers plans - but doesn't execute. Consultants are right when you have a team and need strategic guidance. Fractional CMOs are right when you need ownership. Below: top 9 marketing consulting firms, real pricing, when to hire each type, and the operator-led alternative.

The Short Answer

If you have an in-house team and need strategic guidance only, hire a consultant ($5K-$60K project). If you need someone to own the marketing function, hire a fractional CMO ($8K-$20K/mo). Most companies hire the wrong type and get a brilliant plan that nobody operationalizes. MarkCMO bundles both: consulting-tier depth + ongoing ownership at $8K-$15K/mo.

1. MarkCMO (Operator-Led)

2. McKinsey Marketing & Sales Practice

Best for: Enterprise tier-one strategic consultancy.

Pricing: $200K-$2M per engagement.

Strengths: Best-in-class strategic frameworks, global reach, board-level credibility.

Limitations: Strategy hands off to clients - no execution. Wrong for sub-$100M revenue.

3. Bain Marketing Excellence

Best for: Enterprise B2C strategic consultancy.

Pricing: $200K-$2M per engagement.

Strengths: Strong consumer + retail expertise.

4. BCG Marketing

Best for: Enterprise digital + martech consultancy.

Pricing: $200K-$2M per engagement.

Strengths: Digital transformation + martech depth.

5. Ambient Strategy (April Dunford)

Best for: Tier-one B2B SaaS positioning consultancy.

Pricing: $50K-$250K per positioning project.

Strengths: Canonical B2B SaaS positioning framework, deep methodology.

6. Reforge

Best for: Growth marketing consultancy + program.

Pricing: Program $5K-$10K per cohort, advisory $15K-$50K project.

Strengths: Brian Balfour's growth framework, strong alumni network.

7. Forty Lights

Best for: Growth-stage marketing strategy + positioning.

Pricing: $25K-$150K per project.

Strengths: Growth-stage focus, integrated strategy.

8. MarketBridge

Best for: Mid-market B2B consulting.

Pricing: $50K-$500K per engagement.

Strengths: B2B go-to-market consulting depth.

9. Demand Curve

Best for: Growth marketing consultancy + agency hybrid.

Pricing: Course $1.5K-$3K, agency $8K-$30K/mo.

Strengths: Accessible for early-stage, strong growth content.
