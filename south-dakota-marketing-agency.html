South Dakota's Dominant Industries

South Dakota hosts Citigroup major Sioux Falls credit card operations (Citi moved its credit card subsidiary to SD because of favorable usury laws starting in the 1980s - SD has no usury cap, making it the credit card capital of America alongside Delaware). Wells Fargo Sioux Falls operations (major bank servicing center). Plus Sanford Health HQ (Sioux Falls - one of largest US rural health systems, 40+ hospitals + 200+ clinics across SD + ND + MN + Iowa + Nebraska, Sanford-branded after Denny Sanford $1B+ donations). Plus Black Hills Corporation HQ (Rapid City - utility serving SD + WY + MT + CO + NE + IA + KS). Plus Raven Industries (Sioux Falls - precision agriculture + plastic film + engineered films, acquired by CNH Industrial 2021 for $2.1B). Plus Mount Rushmore + Black Hills tourism (Mount Rushmore National Memorial, Crazy Horse Memorial, Badlands National Park, Custer State Park, Deadwood historic gold mining town). Plus Sturgis Motorcycle Rally (annual August event drawing 500K+ attendees - one of largest US motorcycle rallies). Plus Ellsworth AFB (Rapid City - B-1 Lancer bomber base, future B-21 Raider home). No state income tax. SD trust law (popular for high net worth tax planning, dynasty trusts, private placement life insurance - SD has trust assets in the trillions due to favorable trust law).
