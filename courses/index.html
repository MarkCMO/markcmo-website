Questions
FAQ
Do I need videos to learn? ▼
Every lesson is delivered as a dynamically generated 1,500+ word deep-dive created by AI in real-time. Video slots are built into the platform — as video content is recorded and published, it will automatically appear alongside the text lesson. You can learn fully without video. Video is an enhancement, not a requirement.
Why is every lesson different? ▼
The AI generates fresh lesson content every time based on the topic, your student ID, and a unique session seed. This means no two students get the same content, retakes get entirely new material, and the platform never gets "gamed" by sharing answers. This is how education at scale should work.
What if I fail the final exam? ▼
You pay a discounted retake fee (typically 30–40% of the original price, depending on the course). Your retake gets a completely new set of 50 final exam questions generated by AI. Your progress (completed lessons and module scores) is preserved, so you only need to retake the final — not the entire course.
Is this accredited? ▼
MarkCMO Academy is a professional development platform, not an accredited academic institution. The diploma is a credential of professional excellence from Mark Donnigan, a recognized Fractional CMO and business growth expert. The rigor is Harvard/MIT level by design — the value comes from what you actually learn and prove you know.
How long do courses take? ▼
Most courses are 35–80 hours of study time. You go at your own pace — there are no deadlines or time limits. The average student completes a course in 4–12 weeks. Access is lifetime, so you can return and review any lesson whenever you need a refresher.
Can I take multiple courses? ▼
Absolutely. Each course is a separate enrollment. There are no bundles or packages — each course stands alone at its own price. Many students start with the CMO or COO course and add LinkedIn, Digital Marketing, and Revenue Architecture as they scale their practice.
