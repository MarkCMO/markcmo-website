OVERVIEW
WETYR Film Intel is a professional market-intelligence app for film producers and
distributors. Four of the five tabs (Titles, Box Office, Briefing, Casting) work
immediately with no account and show live, read-only public data. The fifth tab
(Rolodex) is a private contact directory that requires a sign-in.

HOW TO REVIEW THE PUBLIC TABS
1. Launch the app. The Titles tab loads trending film data. Tap a poster to switch.
2. Box Office shows in-theaters and trending lists. Tap any row for detail.
3. Briefing shows live industry headlines and festival deadlines. Tap to open.
4. Casting shows open scripted and commercial roles from public feeds.

HOW TO REVIEW THE ROLODEX (sign-in)
1. Open the Rolodex tab and sign in with the demo account in the credentials fields.
2. You can search contacts, filter by type or department, and open a record to see
   contact details. Adding, editing, and enrichment actions are also available.

DATA AND PRIVACY
The app talks only to the developer's own backend at markcmo.com. Public tabs read
public film data (sourced via TMDB and public RSS). The Rolodex contains the
developer's own business contacts and is access-controlled. No third-party
advertising or tracking SDKs are present; no IDFA is used.
