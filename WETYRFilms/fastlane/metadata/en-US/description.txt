WETYR Film Intel puts the signals film producers, packagers, and distributors track every morning on one fast, native screen.

It is the companion app to the WETYR Film Intel platform. Open it with your coffee and see what moved overnight: what is winning at the box office, which titles are trending, what the trades are reporting, which festival deadlines are closing, and which roles are casting now.

WHAT IS INSIDE

Title intelligence
Pull full detail on any trending film: poster, rating, runtime, budget and revenue, top cast, key crew, production companies, where to watch, and a one-tap link to the trailer and IMDb. Auto-rotates the top trending titles, tap to hold one.

Box office
A clean read on what is in theaters now and trending this week, with popularity direction and audience scores at a glance.

Daily briefing
A live feed of industry headlines from the major trades, plus a rolling list of festival deadlines with days-left countdowns so nothing slips.

Casting calls
Open scripted and commercial roles aggregated from public casting feeds, tagged by union status, location, role, and rate.

Industry rolodex
A private, sign-in-only contact book for companies and people across the business: search, filter by type or department, view emails, phones, IMDb, and production credits, and keep records current.

WHO IT IS FOR
Producers, development and acquisitions execs, packagers, agents, and anyone who needs the state of the market in their pocket.

NOTE
Title, box office, news, festival, and casting data is read-only market intelligence drawn from public sources. The rolodex is private and requires an account.
