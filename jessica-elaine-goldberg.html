Professional Overview

A Marketer Built Across
Every Channel That Matters

Jessica Elaine Goldberg-Albers is a senior marketing professional based in San Antonio, Texas, with more than two decades of hands-on experience driving growth-focused campaigns across both business-to-consumer and business-to-business environments. Her career is defined by its extraordinary breadth: beginning in radio promotion and broadcast sales, moving through theme park entertainment, climbing into full-service advertising agency work, leading digital and terrestrial marketing for one of the nation's leading homebuilders, and consistently delivering measurable outcomes at every stop along the way.

Where many marketing professionals specialize in a single vertical or medium, Jessica has done something rarer and more valuable: she has built fluency in nearly every major marketing discipline over a 20-plus year span. She understands traditional broadcast advertising from the inside, having worked on-staff at Sinclair Broadcast Group and iHeartMedia predecessors. She understands the promotional mechanics of live entertainment and consumer experience from her time at Six Flags Fiesta Texas. She understands agency-side client management, media buying, strategic campaign development, and creative execution from her years at The PM Group, San Antonio's largest advertising and marketing agency. And she understands the complete, end-to-end marketing ecosystem of a publicly traded homebuilder from nearly eight years at Meritage Homes, one of the country's most recognized residential construction companies.

The result is a marketing leader who can connect the dots between digital and physical touchpoints, between brand strategy and field execution, between creative ideation and budget accountability. Her expertise in omnichannel campaign management, agent and realtor relationship development, sales model merchandising, community launch strategy, and people-centered team leadership makes her an exceptionally versatile asset in any senior marketing role.

Jessica's promotion to Senior Marketing Manager at Meritage Homes recognized nearly a decade of sustained performance and deep institutional knowledge within one of the most competitive homebuilding markets in the United States. As a senior leader, Jessica was also responsible for mentoring and developing junior marketing team members and building the kind of cross-functional relationships with sales leadership, construction management, and corporate marketing that translate marketing investment into signed contracts. 2018 — 2024 ~6 Years
Meritage Homes
Marketing Manager

Jessica joined Meritage Homes as a Marketing Manager in 2018 and spent six years in the role before her promotion, building out what would become an eight-year tenure at the company. Meritage Homes is a publicly traded homebuilder (NYSE: MTH) headquartered in Scottsdale, Arizona, with active operations in Texas, California, Arizona, Colorado, Georgia, North Carolina, and Tennessee. The Texas market, and San Antonio in particular, is among the most important geographies in the company's portfolio.

Her day-to-day responsibilities as Marketing Manager were broad and demanding. Omnichannel campaign management at a homebuilder means designing and executing marketing programs that move prospective buyers through a multi-month, high-consideration purchase journey. Unlike fast-moving consumer goods marketing, homebuilder marketing must sustain awareness and intent across a long nurture window while simultaneously responding to macro factors like mortgage rate changes, inventory fluctuations, and new community openings.

Jessica also led agent relationship development, a specialized discipline within homebuilder marketing that requires building trust and engagement with the external real estate agent community. Third-party agents and realtors are a significant source of buyer traffic for production homebuilders, and the most effective homebuilder marketing teams treat agent outreach as a parallel marketing channel alongside their direct-to-consumer efforts. Jessica developed and maintained programs to keep Meritage's San Antonio communities top-of-mind with the local agent community through events, co-op programs, communications, and relationship-building initiatives.

One of her most notable contributions during this period was her leadership in launching Meritage Homes' first family-focused Employee Resource Group. This initiative reflected her broader commitment to organizational culture and people-centered leadership beyond the marketing function itself. Building an ERG from scratch requires organizational navigation, stakeholder buy-in, program design, internal communications, and sustained community management, all skills that translate directly into her marketing toolbox.

Additional responsibilities included sales office and model home merchandising, which involves the physical presentation, signage, staging, and collateral environment of model homes and sales centers. Model home merchandising is a critical touchpoint in the homebuyer journey and a discipline that blends spatial storytelling with brand consistency and buyer psychology. Strategic planning for new community launches rounded out her scope, requiring collaboration with land, sales, construction, and corporate teams to bring new neighborhoods to market successfully.

Leadership Philosophy

People-Centered
Marketing Leadership

Marketing leadership is not simply the management of campaigns, budgets, and channels. At its best, it is the creation of an environment in which talented people can do their best work, aligned behind a strategy that serves both the business and the customer. Jessica Goldberg-Albers approaches marketing leadership through a distinctly people-centered lens, and this is reflected not only in professional recommendations about her but in the concrete organizational actions she has taken throughout her career.

The most direct evidence of her commitment to people-centered leadership is her initiative in launching Meritage Homes' first family-focused Employee Resource Group. ERGs are internal organizational communities that serve employees who share a common identity, life stage, or experience. A family-focused ERG addresses the needs of parents, caregivers, and employees navigating the complex intersection of demanding professional roles and personal family responsibilities. Building this ERG from the ground up at a major publicly traded homebuilder required vision, organizational courage, and the ability to build internal coalitions and secure leadership sponsorship.

The fact that Jessica initiated and led this effort while simultaneously managing a full marketing portfolio speaks to her capacity to operate beyond her defined role and contribute to organizational culture at a systemic level. Leaders who invest in employee community building are leaders who understand that durable business performance is built on durable human foundations.

Her LinkedIn learning certifications further illuminate her leadership philosophy. The decision to pursue training in emotional intelligence, high-pressure decision-making, and change leadership while already holding a senior title suggests a leader who is deliberately building the full toolkit required at the executive level. Emotional intelligence, in particular, is one of the most consistently cited determinants of leadership effectiveness, and the willingness to formalize its development through structured learning signals self-awareness and intentionality.
