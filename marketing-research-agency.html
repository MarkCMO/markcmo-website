Marketing Research Agency: The 2026 Buyer's Guide

Marketing research informs strategy with six methods: ICP research, voice-of-customer (VoC), competitor analysis, brand tracking, market sizing, pricing research. Below: top 9 research agencies, real pricing ($5K-$100K+ projects), why VoC is the highest-leverage research, and the operator-led alternative.

The Short Answer

Voice-of-customer (VoC) research is the highest-leverage marketing research. 5-15 customer interviews transcribed and themed give you the actual language customers use - which becomes the source material for positioning, messaging, content, and ads. Skip VoC and your marketing language is invented, not earned. MarkCMO runs VoC in the first 30-60 days of every engagement.

The 6 Marketing Research Types

  1. ICP + persona research. Firmographic + behavioral + psychographic profiles of ideal customers.
  2. Voice-of-customer (VoC). What customers actually say. Interviews, review mining, sales call analysis, churn interviews, win/loss.
  3. Competitor analysis. Positioning, pricing, messaging, market share, share-of-voice.
  4. Brand tracking. Awareness, perception, sentiment, NPS over time.
  5. Market sizing. TAM, SAM, SOM with assumptions documented.
  6. Pricing research. Willingness to pay, price elasticity, package configuration.

Why Voice-of-Customer Is the Highest-Leverage Research

Voice-of-customer (VoC) research captures customer language verbatim. The output: a thematic analysis of what customers say about their problems, solutions, and buying decisions in THEIR words, not yours.

Why VoC matters more than any other research:

  • Source material for positioning. April Dunford's framework requires knowing the alternatives customers consider - VoC reveals this directly.
  • Source material for messaging. The exact phrases customers use become headlines, ad copy, landing page copy.
  • Source material for content. The questions customers actually ask become the article topics that drive organic + AI search citations.
  • Source material for ads. Creative testing winners are usually the ones using customer language verbatim.
  • Source material for sales enablement. Sales team uses VoC quotes as social proof in deals.

VoC methodology: 5-15 customer interviews (30-60 minutes each, recorded + transcribed), supplemented with review mining (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot), sales call analysis (Gong/Chorus), churn interviews (departing customers), and win/loss analysis.
