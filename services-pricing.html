Pricing FAQ

Can the audit cost apply toward a retainer?

Yes. Companies that complete the Revenue Leak Audit and move to a monthly retainer within 30 days can apply the $7,500 audit fee toward their first month's retainer.

What's the minimum retainer commitment?

3 months minimum. Real marketing results require at least one full strategy-build-execute-measure cycle. 90 days is the absolute minimum for meaningful impact. Most clients stay 12-24 months.

Do you work with companies outside the US?

The primary focus is US-based B2B companies, but remote engagements with international companies who sell into the US market are considered on a case-by-case basis.

What size companies do you typically work with?

Companies with $1M to $30M in revenue - typically B2B SaaS, professional services, PE-backed, and venture-backed companies. The sweet spot is companies that have product-market fit and are ready to scale marketing deliberately.
