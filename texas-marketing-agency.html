The Short Answer

Texas is 4 marketing markets in one: Austin B2B SaaS, DFW corporate HQs, Houston energy + medical, San Antonio insurance + military. Pick by region AND industry. No state income tax + California migrant talent makes Austin uniquely competitive with SF.

Texas Marketing FAQ

How does MarkCMO compare to a tier-one Texas agency?

Tier-one Texas AOR shops charge $25K-$100K/mo and bring brand creative + production scale that small in-house teams cannot match. They are the right answer for Fortune 500 Texas brand work where the budget supports it. For $2M-$25M growth-stage Texas companies, the AOR model is overkill - the spend goes to creative production rather than pipeline-accountable execution. MarkCMO bundles fractional CMO leadership + WETYR operator execution at $8K-$25K/mo, focused on measurable pipeline + revenue impact rather than brand awards.

Can MarkCMO replace my in-house Texas marketing director?

MarkCMO replaces (or augments) the senior marketing leadership tier - VP Marketing, CMO, Head of Marketing. We do not replace marketing managers, content writers, designers, or paid media specialists at the execution layer. For Texas companies that have a marketing manager or coordinator already in seat but need senior strategy + leadership, MarkCMO drops in above the existing team. For Texas companies with no marketing team at all, MarkCMO provides both leadership and execution via the bundled WETYR operator network.

How quickly can MarkCMO start in Texas?

Standard MarkCMO Texas engagement timeline: 30-minute scoping call within 48 hours, full proposal within 5 business days, signed engagement within 10 business days, first leadership session within 14 days, MAGNET Framework diagnostic complete within 30 days, first measurable execution within 30-60 days. For urgent Texas engagements (rescue work, pre-funding-round preparation, founder transition) we can compress this further with expedited scoping.

Does MarkCMO need to be in Texas in person?

No. MarkCMO is remote-first with quarterly on-site visits to Texas when the client wants them. Mark personally serves every Texas engagement remotely with weekly leadership cadence calls, in-person quarterly business reviews at the client's Texas office or chosen location, and ad-hoc travel for major launches, board meetings, or industry events. Most Texas clients prefer this model over hiring a local-only CMO because it gives them senior fractional leadership without limiting their talent pool to whoever happens to live in their metro.

What industries does MarkCMO NOT serve in Texas?

MarkCMO does not serve highly regulated tier-one Fortune 500 pharma + insurance + banking direct accounts where the client needs in-house FDA medical affairs review or DoD classified-cleared marketing teams. MarkCMO also does not serve pure agency referral work (we are not a marketing agency - we provide fractional CMO leadership + bundled execution). MarkCMO does not work with cannabis, gambling, adult, or firearms verticals. For everything else in Texas - B2B services, SaaS, healthcare-adjacent, manufacturing, consumer brands, professional services, real estate, financial services - MarkCMO is a strong fit at the $2M-$25M revenue band.
