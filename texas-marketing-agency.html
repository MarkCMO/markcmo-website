The Short Answer

Texas is 4 marketing markets in one: Austin B2B SaaS, DFW corporate HQs, Houston energy + medical, San Antonio insurance + military. Pick by region AND industry. No state income tax + California migrant talent makes Austin uniquely competitive with SF.

TX's 4 Major Hubs

Austin - Silicon Hills

Tesla HQ, Oracle HQ, Apple $1B+ campus, Meta, Google, Amazon, Indeed, Bumble HQ, Whole Foods Market HQ. UT Austin + SXSW + California migrant tech.

Dallas-Fort Worth

AT&T HQ, ExxonMobil HQ (Spring), Texas Instruments HQ, Southwest Airlines HQ, American Airlines HQ, Toyota North America HQ (Plano), Charles Schwab HQ, McKesson + Caterpillar relocated HQs.

Houston - Energy + Medical

ExxonMobil + Chevron + ConocoPhillips + Halliburton + SLB + Baker Hughes. Texas Medical Center (largest medical complex in world, 50+ institutions, 100K+ employees).

San Antonio

USAA HQ, Valero Energy HQ, military bases (Lackland, Randolph, Fort Sam Houston), tourism (Alamo, River Walk, Six Flags).
