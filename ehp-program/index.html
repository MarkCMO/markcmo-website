How It Works

Money goes out one door, and comes back the other

Here is the whole thing in plain words. A piece of each paycheck is set aside for a health program before taxes are counted. Then that exact same money is handed right back to your employee. It goes out one door and comes back the other, so their take-home pay does not change.

  • ✓Because the money moved before taxes, there is less pay to tax, so you both pay less.
  • ✓You keep about $640 a year for every employee.
  • ✓The savings cover the small fee to run it, so it costs you nothing.
