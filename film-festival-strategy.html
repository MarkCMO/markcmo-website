Film Festival Strategy: The 2026 Producer's Guide

Sundance, Cannes, TIFF, Venice, SXSW, Tribeca, Berlin determine distribution outcomes for most indie films. Below: festival calendar + submission deadlines, top sales agents, festival publicity strategy, and how to position for distribution deals.

The Short Answer

Attach a sales agent 6-12 months BEFORE festival submission, not after acceptance. The sales agent's network drives festival positioning AND determines who shows up to buy your film. Indie producers who submit without sales agent attached land at festivals with no buyers in the room.
