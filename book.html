Common Questions

Before you book.

The free discovery call is 30 minutes. Mark uses this time to understand your current marketing situation, growth goals, and whether a fractional CMO or COO engagement is the right solution for where you are right now. There is no pitch and no obligation - it is a genuine diagnostic conversation.
Come with your current revenue range, your primary growth goal for the next 12 months, and the specific marketing or operational challenge you are trying to solve. The more specific you are, the more useful the 30 minutes will be. You do not need a deck or prepared presentation.
Most engagements start within 2 weeks of initial agreement. The first week is typically onboarding - access to CRM, analytics, and marketing accounts, introduction to the team, and beginning the marketing audit. Full strategic output begins in week 2-3.
That is exactly what the discovery call is for. Mark will give you an honest assessment of whether fractional CMO or COO services are the right solution - or whether something else (an agency, a marketing director hire, or a standalone audit) would serve you better. He turns down engagements that are not a strong fit because bad-fit clients do not get good results.
Fractional CMO retainers typically range from $5,000 to $20,000 per month depending on scope and hours. This compares to a full-time CMO at $280,000-$450,000 per year in salary and benefits alone - making the fractional model 60-80% more cost-efficient. Scope and pricing are discussed on the discovery call based on your specific situation.
