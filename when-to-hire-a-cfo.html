When to Hire a Fractional CFO

The right time to bring on a fractional CFO - and when to wait or go full-time.

Quick Answer

Hire a fractional CFO when you need senior ownership of financial strategy, FP&A, cash management, fundraising support, and board reporting but cannot justify a full-time Chief Financial Officer yet. Sweet spot: $2M-$25M revenue. Book a call.

The signs

You need a fractional CFO when: the function bottlenecks growth, an executive is stretched covering it, a milestone (raise, scale, transaction) demands senior leadership, or junior in-house ownership stalled. These are the moments a fractional CFO pays for itself.

When to wait or go full-time

Wait if you are pre-product-market-fit and the function is premature. Go full-time if you are past $25M-$50M revenue and the role needs daily ownership. In between, a fractional CFO - or a fractional-plus-junior hybrid - is usually right.
