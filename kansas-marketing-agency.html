The Short Answer

Wichita aviation marketing requires deep general aviation + aerostructures + Boeing-Airbus supply chain fluency that takes years to build - Wichita is uniquely the "Air Capital of the World" with concentrated GA manufacturing. Olathe Garmin GPS marketing requires consumer electronics + fitness + aviation + B2B + global distributor fluency. Kansas agriculture requires Farm Bureau + co-op + agtech marketing. For $2M-$25M Kansas companies, operator-led fractional execution covers the supplier + adjacent tier.

Kansas's Dominant Industries

Kansas hosts Garmin HQ (Olathe - GPS + fitness + aviation + marine + automotive electronics, $5B+ revenue, dominant in handheld GPS + smartwatch + cockpit avionics). Spirit AeroSystems HQ (Wichita - one of largest US aerostructures makers, supplies Boeing 737/777/787 fuselage + wing components + structures, $5B+ revenue). Textron Aviation (Wichita - includes Cessna + Beechcraft, the world's general aviation capital, Wichita is called "Air Capital of the World" with concentrated aircraft manufacturing including Spirit + Textron + Bombardier Learjet). Plus YRC Worldwide HQ (Overland Park - trucking + logistics). Sprint historically HQ'd Overland Park (now T-Mobile post-2020 merger). Plus Embarq + CenturyLink + Lumen historic Kansas operations. Plus University of Kansas (Lawrence) + Kansas State University (Manhattan - top US agricultural research). Plus Kansas wheat + cattle agriculture. Kansas City Kansas + Missouri = major logistics + intermodal hub. McConnell AFB Wichita. Fort Riley Manhattan area Army base.
