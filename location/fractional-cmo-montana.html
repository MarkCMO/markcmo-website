Frequently Asked Questions

What does a fractional CMO do in Montana?

A fractional CMO in Montana provides part-time Chief Marketing Officer services — full strategic leadership, go-to-market execution, and revenue-focused marketing systems — without the $250K–$400K full-time salary. Mark Gabrielli serves Montana businesses remotely and in-person, embedded directly into your executive team.

How much does a fractional CMO cost in Montana?

Fractional CMO engagements in Montana typically range from $5,000–$20,000/month depending on scope, versus $250,000–$400,000+ for a full-time hire when you factor in salary, benefits, and equity. Mark's engagements are month-to-month with no long-term contracts.

Who needs a fractional CMO in Montana?

Companies in Montana between $1M–$50M revenue that need senior marketing leadership but aren't ready for a full-time C-suite hire. Businesses stuck on a revenue plateau, between CMOs, or scaling fast without strategic infrastructure.

Can a fractional CMO work remotely for my Montana business?

Yes. Mark serves clients across the United States and globally, including Montana, operating remotely with regular strategy sessions, embedded execution, and full executive accountability — exactly like an in-house CMO, without the geographic constraint.
