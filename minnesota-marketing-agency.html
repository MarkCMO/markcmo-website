Minnesota Marketing Agency: The 2026 Buyer's Guide

MN has 19 Fortune 500 HQs - one of the highest US concentrations per capita. Target, UnitedHealth, 3M, US Bank, General Mills, Best Buy, Land O'Lakes, Medtronic, Hormel. Mayo Clinic. Below: top 9 MN agencies + MN healthcare + CPG marketing specifics.

The Short Answer

Minnesota has unusually strong independent agencies (Carmichael Lynch, Olson, Periscope, Colle McVoy) competing with holding company shops. Mayo + UHG + Medtronic anchor healthcare marketing. General Mills + Hormel + Land O'Lakes anchor CPG. Target + Best Buy anchor retail. Pick by industry, not generic.

MN's 6 Dominant Industries

  1. Healthcare. Mayo Clinic (Rochester), UnitedHealth Group HQ (Minnetonka, $400B+ revenue), Medtronic US HQ (largest med device company globally), Boston Scientific, regional systems.
  2. Retail. Target HQ (Minneapolis), Best Buy HQ (Richfield), Supervalu/UNFI.
  3. CPG + food. General Mills HQ (Cheerios, Pillsbury), Land O'Lakes HQ, Hormel Foods HQ.
  4. Financial services. US Bank HQ (Minneapolis), Ameriprise Financial HQ, Thrivent Financial.
  5. Manufacturing. 3M HQ (Maplewood), Polaris Industries HQ, Toro Company HQ, Cummins.
  6. Energy. Xcel Energy HQ, Travelers Insurance MN operations.
