SEO Expert vs SEO Consultant

The terms SEO expert and SEO consultant get used interchangeably but the engagement models are different. Strategic operator running outcomes vs project-based advisor delivering reports.

Quick Answer

An SEO expert is typically retained for ongoing strategic and operational ownership of the SEO program (4,000 to 20,000 dollars per month). An SEO consultant is typically engaged for a defined project scope - audit, recommendations, strategic plan - at 150 to 500 dollars per hour or 5,000 to 25,000 dollars for a project. The expert owns the outcome over time. The consultant delivers an artifact and exits. Both have valid use cases.

Engagement Structure

SEO consultant: hourly billing or fixed project fee for specific deliverables (audit, strategy doc, recommendations). Defined scope and end date. SEO expert: monthly retainer for ongoing program ownership. Renews quarterly or annually. The expert owns outcomes; the consultant owns artifacts.

Cost Comparison

SEO consultant: $150-$500 per hour or $5K-$25K per project. SEO expert (fractional): $3K-$20K per month retainer. SEO expert (MarkCMO): $4K-$12K per month with bundled execution.

When Each Wins

Consultant wins for: specific deliverable, time-bounded need, second opinion, due diligence, one-time strategic planning. Expert wins for: ongoing program ownership, AI search optimization rollout (multi-month entity work), full-stack SEO, growth-stage scaling.
