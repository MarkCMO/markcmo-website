Marketing Attribution: The Complete 2026 Guide

Tracking-based attribution misses 30-50% of touches in 2026 - iOS privacy, ad blockers, dark social, cross-device gaps. Below: the triangulation method (MTA + SRA + MMM), top attribution platforms, and how to actually know what's driving revenue.

The Short Answer

Pure tracking-based attribution lies in 2026. Best practice: triangulate three sources - multi-touch attribution (MTA) for digital touches, self-reported attribution (SRA) for influence channels, media mix modeling (MMM) for spend-to-revenue regression. When all three agree, you can trust the answer. When they disagree, dig.

The Triangulation Method (MarkCMO Standard)

  1. Multi-touch attribution (MTA) via HockeyStack or Dreamdata - position-based model for B2B.
  2. Self-reported attribution (SRA) via signup/demo form - "How did you first hear about us?"
  3. Media mix modeling (MMM) - regression analysis of spend vs revenue by channel quarterly.
  4. Compare all three. When they agree, trust the answer. When they disagree, investigate.
  5. Decision criterion: never make a >$50K reallocation based on a single attribution source.
