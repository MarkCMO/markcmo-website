SEO Expert for Startups

When startups should hire an SEO expert, when to wait, and how to structure the engagement to fit a startup growth model.

Quick Answer

Most pre-product-market-fit startups should NOT hire an SEO expert. SEO timeline (6-12 months to compounding returns) often outpaces seed runway. Spend the early stage on product, positioning, and direct sales motion. Post-PMF startups at $1M-$5M ARR should hire a fractional SEO expert at $3K-$6K per month focused on category capture and topic authority foundation. Series A and beyond ($5M+ ARR) should run full-stack SEO via fractional or in-house plus operator network.

Pre-PMF Startups

Spend $0-$2K per month on SEO. Founder-led content, basic technical hygiene via Squarespace or Webflow defaults, light Google Business Profile setup if applicable. Focus engineering and design time on product, not SEO. Most SEO investments pre-PMF are wasted because the offer is not yet validated.

Post-PMF Seed ($1M-$5M ARR)

Hire a fractional SEO expert at $3K-$6K per month. Focus on category capture: 5-10 pillar pages on category-defining keywords, basic technical SEO foundation (Core Web Vitals, schema), Wikidata entity setup for AI search visibility. Avoid programmatic SEO at this stage - too easy to build thin content debt.

Series A ($5M-$15M ARR)

Scale to $6K-$12K per month full-stack fractional SEO. Add link earning campaigns (PR, podcast tours), topic cluster expansion, programmatic SEO where data supports it, full AI search optimization (GEO and AEO). MarkCMO at $6K-$10K per month with bundled WETYR execution typically fits this band.

Series B+ ($15M+ ARR)

Hybrid model: in-house SEO Manager or Director plus fractional advisor or top-tier agency. Total spend $15K-$30K per month. SEO becomes a primary growth channel with daily ownership and dedicated focus. AI search optimization rolls into entity management as part of broader brand work.
