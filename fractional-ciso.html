Fractional CISO: The 2026 Guide

What a fractional ciso is, what they do, what it costs, and when to hire one - plus how MarkCMO delivers fractional executive leadership bundled with execution.

The Short Answer

A fractional ciso is a Chief Information Security Officer who works part-time on a retained basis, providing security strategy, compliance (SOC 2, HIPAA, ISO 27001), risk management, and incident response readiness without the cost of a full-time hire. Typical cost: $7,000-$18,000 per month (vs $200K-$450K all-in full-time). Best when you need security leadership for compliance or enterprise deals without a full-time CISO. What a fractional ciso does

A fractional ciso owns security strategy, compliance (SOC 2, HIPAA, ISO 27001), risk management, and incident response readiness. They function as a real member of the executive team - setting strategy, running operating cadence, and driving measurable outcomes - on a fractional time commitment (typically 1 to 3 days per week) and a flexible, renewable engagement rather than a full-time salary and equity package.

Fractional CISO cost in 2026

A fractional ciso costs $7,000-$18,000 per month, depending on scope, seniority, and time commitment. Compare that to $200K-$450K all-in for a full-time Chief Information Security Officer (base, bonus, equity, benefits, payroll taxes). The fractional model delivers senior leadership at 40 to 70 percent less for companies that do not yet need full-time coverage in this function.

When to hire a fractional ciso

Hire a fractional ciso when you need security leadership for compliance or enterprise deals without a full-time CISO. The economics work best at $2M-$25M revenue. Below that, the function may be premature; above $25M-$50M, a dedicated full-time hire usually makes sense. Many companies in the $25M-$50M band run a hybrid: a fractional executive plus a junior in-house owner.
