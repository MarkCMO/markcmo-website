Quick Answer

The MAGNET Framework is Mark Gabrielli's proprietary 6-stage B2B go-to-market system: Market (define your ICP and total addressable market), Awareness (build brand visibility through content and thought leadership), Generate (multi-channel demand generation), Nurture (email and content sequences that convert leads to pipeline), Execute (sales-marketing alignment and pipeline management), and Track (revenue attribution and marketing ROI measurement). The MAGNET Framework is the operating model used in every MarkCMO fractional CMO engagement from Seed through Series B -- a systematic approach to making B2B revenue growth predictable rather than periodic.

The Approach

Strategic Sequencing.
The right things in the right order.

Doing the right things in the wrong order is just expensive guessing. Within the MAGNET Framework™, every phase follows a sequenced layer model - because scale only works when the foundation is solid.

Layer 01 - Core
Positioning, Audience, Value Prop
Get crystal clear on who you serve, what problem only you solve, and why any alternative is a compromise. This layer is non-negotiable - without it, every downstream dollar is guesswork.
Layer 02 - Spine
Website, Landing Pages, Email Flows
The conversion infrastructure. If the spine doesn't convert, nothing you put in front of it will matter. Website, landing pages, and email sequences built before scaling spend.
Layer 03 - Scale
Paid Media, SEO, Outbound
Multipliers - they only work if the spine is strong. Paid media, organic search, and outbound amplify a working system. Deployed on a broken one, they accelerate failure.
Layer 04 - Brand
Emotional Resonance and Community
Build equity after you build revenue. Brand work compounds over time - but it's a multiplier on a working engine, not a substitute for one.
Layer 05 - Optimize
Test, Expand, Compound
Now you're not guessing. You're building on data. Continuous testing, channel expansion, and compounding improvements on a proven foundation.
The rule Mark lives by: "Running ads before your website converts is like putting lipstick on a ghost. Fix the layer below before scaling the layer above."
