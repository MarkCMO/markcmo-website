Fractional CHRO vs Full-Time Chief Human Resources Officer

The hire-vs-fractional decision for Chief Human Resources Officer, by revenue stage and cost.

Quick Answer

Below $25M revenue, a fractional CHRO wins on math - $6,000-$15,000/month vs $220,000-$400,000 all-in full-time. Above $25M-$50M, full-time often makes sense. Book a call to confirm fit.

The cost math

Fractional: $6,000-$15,000 per month, no recruiting cost, no equity dilution, month-to-month flexibility. Full-time: $220,000-$400,000 all-in (base, bonus, equity, benefits, taxes) plus $20K-$60K recruiting. For $2M-$25M revenue companies the fractional path saves 40-70 percent while delivering the same caliber of leadership.

When each wins

Fractional wins for $2M-$25M revenue, single-brand, founder-led companies that value senior judgment without daily-presence overhead. Full-time wins above $25M-$50M, when Chief Human Resources Officer work is a daily core lever, or when the board mandates a permanent executive.
