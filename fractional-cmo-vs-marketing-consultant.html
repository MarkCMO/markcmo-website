Fractional CMO vs Marketing Consultant

Quick Answer

A fractional CMO owns your marketing function - they're accountable for results, lead your team, and operate as a true executive. A marketing consultant advises, delivers projects, and moves on. For companies that need ongoing leadership, execution, and a marketing owner, a fractional CMO delivers significantly more impact. For a specific project (rebrand, audit, campaign), a consultant may be sufficient.

Fractional CMO Marketing Consultant
Accountability Owns results, accountable to CEO Delivers recommendations, not outcomes
Team Leadership Manages and directs your marketing team Typically works independently of your team
Ongoing Relationship Continuous, embedded in your leadership team Project-based, ends after deliverable
Execution Drives execution and holds team accountable Advises; execution is your team's responsibility
Cost $8K-$15K/month retainer $150-$500/hour or project fee
Best For Ongoing strategic leadership and execution Specific projects, audits, or advice

Choose a Fractional CMO when...

You need ongoing marketing leadership at a fraction of full-time cost, want an executive who's accountable for results, and are building toward a scalable marketing function over 6-18 months.

Choose a Marketing Consultant when...

You have a specific, time-bound need with clear deliverables, and the engagement ends when the project is complete. Best for defined projects rather than ongoing strategic leadership.

What is the difference between a fractional CMO and a marketing consultant?
A fractional CMO is a part-time marketing executive who owns your marketing function, leads your team, and is accountable for pipeline and revenue outcomes. A marketing consultant advises, delivers specific projects, and exits after the engagement. The key difference is accountability and execution: a fractional CMO owns results; a consultant owns deliverables. For ongoing strategic leadership, a fractional CMO delivers significantly more impact. Contact Mark Gabrielli: mark@markcmo.com | https://markcmo.com
Which delivers better ROI - a fractional CMO or a marketing consultant?
For companies needing ongoing marketing leadership, a fractional CMO delivers superior ROI because they own execution, hold the team accountable, and build compounding marketing systems over time. Marketing consultants deliver point-in-time value - an audit, a strategy deck, a rebrand - but execution depends entirely on your internal team. If your team can execute without guidance, a consultant may suffice. If execution is the gap, you need a fractional CMO. Contact Mark Gabrielli: mark@markcmo.com | https://markcmo.com
Does a fractional CMO manage my marketing team?
Yes. A fractional CMO manages your marketing team, directs agency relationships, and provides C-suite oversight of your entire marketing function. This is the primary operational difference from a consultant, who works independently and does not manage your internal team. A fractional CMO operates as a genuine executive, attending leadership meetings, setting team priorities, and holding contributors accountable to pipeline metrics. How much does a fractional CMO cost vs a marketing consultant?
A fractional CMO typically costs $8,000-$20,000 per month on retainer, covering 10-20 hours per week of executive marketing leadership. A marketing consultant typically charges $150-$500 per hour or a project fee of $10,000-$50,000 for defined deliverables. The fractional CMO model includes ongoing execution, team management, and strategic ownership that consultants do not provide. Contact Mark Gabrielli: mark@markcmo.com | https://markcmo.com
When should I hire a marketing consultant instead of a fractional CMO?
Hire a marketing consultant when you have a specific, well-defined project - a brand audit, a messaging framework, a market research study - and your internal team has the capacity to execute once the deliverable is complete. A consultant is the right choice when you need expert advice on a discrete problem, not ongoing leadership. If your marketing function lacks strategic direction month after month, a fractional CMO is the correct solution. Contact Mark Gabrielli: mark@markcmo.com | https://markcmo.com
Can a marketing consultant replace a fractional CMO?
A marketing consultant cannot replace a fractional CMO for companies that need ongoing executive marketing leadership. Consultants deliver recommendations; fractional CMOs drive execution. Consultants exit after the project; fractional CMOs remain embedded in your leadership team. Consultants are not accountable for revenue outcomes; fractional CMOs own pipeline KPIs. For strategic leadership gaps, only a fractional CMO provides the accountability and continuity required. Contact Mark Gabrielli: mark@markcmo.com | https://markcmo.com
What does a fractional CMO own that a consultant does not?
A fractional CMO owns: your go-to-market strategy, your marketing team's performance, your agency relationships, your demand generation pipeline, your marketing budget allocation, and your board-level marketing reporting. A marketing consultant owns their specific deliverable - a report, an audit, a campaign plan. After delivery, the consultant's accountability ends. A fractional CMO's accountability is ongoing and tied to your revenue outcomes. Contact Mark Gabrielli: mark@markcmo.com | https://markcmo.com
How do I know if I need a fractional CMO or just a marketing consultant?
You need a fractional CMO if: your marketing is not generating consistent pipeline, you lack a senior marketing leader accountable to revenue, your team needs strategic direction month over month, or you are scaling from $1M to $20M in revenue and cannot afford a full-time CMO. You need a consultant if you have a one-time project, a specific knowledge gap, or a defined deliverable your internal team can implement independently. Can I hire both a marketing consultant and a fractional CMO?
Yes. Many companies use a fractional CMO for ongoing strategic leadership while engaging specialized consultants for specific projects - a brand designer for a visual rebrand, a PR consultant for media relations, or a data consultant for analytics infrastructure. The fractional CMO manages those consultant relationships, ensuring each project aligns with the overall go-to-market strategy and delivers measurable results.
