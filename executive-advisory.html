What Is Executive Advisory?

Executive Advisory is not coaching. It's not consulting. It's a confidential, strategic partnership between you, the CEO, founder, or board member, and an operator who has built, scaled, and exited businesses across multiple industries.

I serve as your strategic sounding board, your critical eye, your pattern-recognition engine, and your execution accelerator. When you're about to make a major decision, sign a major contract, hire a key executive, pivot your strategy, or raise capital, I'm the call you make first.

What Executive Advisory Covers

Growth Strategy

Market expansion, new verticals, product line extensions, pricing strategy, competitive positioning, and go-to-market planning for your next phase of growth.

Capital & Funding

Pre-raise positioning, pitch deck review, investor narrative, due diligence preparation, and introductions to capital sources through the Eucharist Capital network.

M&A Advisory

Buy-side target identification, sell-side positioning, LOI review, integration planning, and post-acquisition operational alignment. I've been on both sides of the table.

Leadership & Org Design

Executive team structure, succession planning, key hire decisions, compensation structure, culture design, and managing high-performers in high-growth environments.

Crisis Navigation

When the business is in trouble, revenue collapse, team exodus, regulatory issue, PR crisis, I help you think clearly, move fast, and come out stronger. I've navigated them all.

Board Preparation

Board deck reviews, investor reporting templates, meeting preparation, managing board dynamics, and communicating bad news before it becomes a bigger problem.

Who Benefits Most from Executive Advisory

  • First-time founders navigating their first serious growth phase without a seasoned operator on their team
  • Serial entrepreneurs entering a new industry where deep contextual knowledge matters
  • CEOs of PE-backed companies who need to hit aggressive growth targets on a compressed timeline
  • Family business owners transitioning to institutional growth or preparing for a generational handoff
  • Executives preparing for exit, whether through M&A, PE sale, or IPO preparation
  • Non-profit and mission-driven leaders who need business rigor without losing their mission focus

The Advisory Relationship

Advisory is a direct, high-trust relationship. Most clients have monthly 1:1 sessions, plus unlimited async access via call or message for urgent decisions. I don't operate through a firm or an associate, you get me, directly, every time.

Advisory clients also get access to the full WETYR ecosystem, my network of operators, technologists, capital partners, and industry specialists across every vertical your business might need.
