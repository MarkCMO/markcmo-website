What Is Executive Advisory?

Executive Advisory is not coaching. It's not consulting. It's a confidential, strategic partnership between you, the CEO, founder, or board member, and an operator who has built, scaled, and exited businesses across multiple industries.

I serve as your strategic sounding board, your critical eye, your pattern-recognition engine, and your execution accelerator. When you're about to make a major decision, sign a major contract, hire a key executive, pivot your strategy, or raise capital, I'm the call you make first.

What Executive Advisory Covers

Growth Strategy

Market expansion, new verticals, product line extensions, pricing strategy, competitive positioning, and go-to-market planning for your next phase of growth.

Capital & Funding

Pre-raise positioning, pitch deck review, investor narrative, due diligence preparation, and introductions to capital sources through the Eucharist Capital network.

M&A Advisory

Buy-side target identification, sell-side positioning, LOI review, integration planning, and post-acquisition operational alignment. I've been on both sides of the table.

Leadership & Org Design

Executive team structure, succession planning, key hire decisions, compensation structure, culture design, and managing high-performers in high-growth environments.

Crisis Navigation

When the business is in trouble, revenue collapse, team exodus, regulatory issue, PR crisis, I help you think clearly, move fast, and come out stronger. I've navigated them all.

Board Preparation

Board deck reviews, investor reporting templates, meeting preparation, managing board dynamics, and communicating bad news before it becomes a bigger problem.

Frequently Asked Questions About Executive Advisory

What is executive advisory and how is it different from business coaching?
Executive advisory is a strategic partnership with an experienced operator who has been in C-suite roles, built and scaled companies, and navigated the decisions you're currently facing. Business coaching focuses on personal development and leadership skills. Advisory focuses on business outcomes -- deals, strategy, capital, M&A, crisis navigation, and board dynamics. Coaches ask questions to help you find your own answers. Advisors give you the answer when they've seen that movie before.
How much does executive advisory cost?
Executive Advisory engagements at MarkCMO are structured at $5,000 to $20,000 per month depending on access level, engagement intensity, and the complexity of the business situation. The ROI calculation is straightforward: if the advisory relationship improves a single major decision per quarter, the typical value is 10x to 100x the monthly cost. Start with a free diagnostic call to discuss the right engagement structure for your situation.
Who is executive advisory right for?
Executive advisory is best for CEOs and founders who are making high-stakes decisions regularly -- capital raises, strategic partnerships, M&A, key hires, market expansions, or crisis navigation -- and who want a trusted operator in their corner who has been through it before. It is particularly valuable for first-time founders in growth mode, PE-backed CEOs with aggressive targets, and executives preparing for exit or transition.
What is the difference between advisory and fractional C-suite services?
Fractional CMO, COO, CFO, and CTO services involve actively leading a functional area of the business -- setting strategy, managing team, and owning outcomes within a specific domain. Executive Advisory is broader and sits above functional execution -- it operates at the CEO level, covering cross-functional strategy, capital decisions, organizational design, and the decisions that don't fit neatly into any one functional area. Some clients start with advisory and move to fractional C-suite, or combine both for comprehensive senior leadership coverage.
How quickly can I start an executive advisory engagement?
The first session can typically be scheduled within 7 days of the initial diagnostic call. The engagement begins with a structured onboarding session covering your business model, current strategic priorities, the decisions on your horizon, and the areas where outside perspective adds the most value. From that point, the advisory relationship is live and the line is open.
