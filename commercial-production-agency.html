Commercial Production Agency: The 2026 Buyer's Guide

Commercial production runs $5K to $5M+ per spot depending on tier. Director choice defines the result. Below: top 9 commercial production companies, real budget breakdowns, large set commercial economics, and how to brief production companies for premium output.

The Short Answer

The director defines the spot. A $500K budget executed by the wrong director produces $500K of mediocre content. The right director at the right tier produces breakthrough work that pays back the budget many times over. Brief carefully, bid 3+ companies, and weight director match over price.
