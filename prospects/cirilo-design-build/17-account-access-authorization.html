2. What WETYR is authorized to do

  • Create, claim, verify, configure, and manage the accounts and properties above on Client's behalf.
  • Build, publish, edit, and optimize listings, web pages, tracking, campaigns, and creative.
  • Place and manage advertising only within the media budget approved in Schedule A. WETYR will not exceed the approved budget without written approval.
  • Set up call tracking and, with Client's approval, port or configure a local tracking number that routes to Client.
  • Respond to and manage reviews and inquiries in line with guidelines Client approves.
  • Access analytics and account data needed to report on performance.
Limits

WETYR will not make purchases or commitments outside the approved media budget, will not change account ownership, and will not use access for any purpose other than the engagement. Credentials are handled per the NDA and stored securely.

3. Data and revocation

WETYR will handle Client data and credentials confidentially and securely, and will use them only for the engagement. Client may revoke this authorization at any time in writing; WETYR will promptly cease access and assist in transferring or returning account control. On the end of the engagement, WETYR will hand back all account access and ownership to Client.
