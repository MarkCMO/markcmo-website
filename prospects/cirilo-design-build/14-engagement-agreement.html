The Agreement

Engagement Agreement

Please note. This is a working template prepared for the parties' discussion. It is not legal advice. Both parties should have it reviewed by their own attorney before signing.

This Marketing Services Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of   (the "Effective Date") by and between:

WETYR Corporation, doing business as MarkCMO ("WETYR" or "Consultant"), and

Cirilo Design + Build ("Client").

WETYR and Client are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

  1. Engagement and Scope Client engages WETYR to provide fractional Chief Marketing Officer and marketing execution services as described in the Marketing & Growth Engagement Proposal dated 2026-05-28 (the "Proposal") and the Investment, Media Budget & Compensation Schedule (the "Schedule," Schedule A), both of which are incorporated into this Agreement by reference. If there is a conflict, this Agreement governs, then the Schedule, then the Proposal. The selected tier and its scope are set in the Schedule.
  2. Term and Termination
    • Initial term: twelve (12) months from the Effective Date. Thereafter the Agreement continues month to month until terminated.
    • Either Party may terminate after the initial term with thirty (30) days written notice. Either Party may terminate for material breach that is not cured within fifteen (15) days of written notice.
    • On termination, Client pays all fees earned and costs incurred through the termination date, including any unpaid systems-build installments for work already performed. The engagement fee is non-refundable. WETYR will hand off access, files, and work product for which Client has paid.
  3. Fees, Media Budget and Payment Compensation, the advertising (media) budget, and payment timing are set in the Schedule. In summary: a one-time engagement fee, a monthly retainer, and the systems-build fee paid in monthly installments are WETYR compensation. The advertising media budget is Client's spend, paid to advertising platforms, and is not WETYR compensation. WETYR invoices amounts due, and each invoice includes ACH and wire remittance instructions. Invoices are due on receipt; balances unpaid ten (10) days past due accrue interest at 1.5 percent per month. The engagement fee is earned on signing and is non-refundable.
  4. Client Responsibilities Client will provide timely access, assets, approvals, and information needed for the work, as itemized in the Onboarding Punch List, and will fund the advertising media budget per the option selected in the Schedule. WETYR is not responsible for delays caused by Client's failure to provide access, approvals, or funding.
  5. Intellectual Property
    • On full payment of the fees attributable to a given deliverable, Client owns the final marketing deliverables created specifically for Client (website copy, page designs, photos and video produced for Client, and similar work product).
    • WETYR retains ownership of its pre-existing materials, methods, templates, frameworks, tools, and general know-how, and may reuse them. WETYR grants Client a perpetual license to use any such materials embedded in the delivered work product.
    • WETYR may describe the engagement and display non-confidential results and creative in its portfolio and marketing, unless Client requests otherwise in writing.
  6. Confidentiality Each Party will protect the other's non-public information and use it only to perform this Agreement, consistent with the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement signed by the Parties, which is incorporated by reference. This includes financial, merchant, banking, and system-access information disclosed for the engagement.
  7. No Guarantee of Results WETYR will perform the services with professional skill and care. Marketing outcomes depend on factors outside WETYR's control, including market conditions, ad-platform behavior, Client's sales execution, pricing, and capacity. WETYR does not guarantee specific lead volume, ranking, revenue, or contract counts. Targets in the Proposal are good-faith estimates, not promises.
  8. Third-Party Platforms and Ad Spend Services rely on third-party platforms (Google, Meta, Houzz, and others) governed by their own terms. Where ads run on Client's own platform accounts, Client is responsible for amounts charged by those platforms. WETYR manages the media budget at no percentage markup and is not liable for platform outages, account suspensions, or policy changes outside its control.
  9. Independent Contractor WETYR is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner, or joint venturer of Client. Each Party is responsible for its own taxes and personnel. Neither Party may bind the other except as expressly authorized in writing (see the Account Access and Marketing Authorization).
  10. Limitation of Liability To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither Party is liable for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages, or for lost profits or revenue. WETYR's total aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement is limited to the WETYR compensation (excluding media budget) actually paid by Client to WETYR in the three (3) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.
  11. Indemnification Each Party will indemnify the other against third-party claims arising from its own negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement. Client will indemnify WETYR against claims arising from Client's products, services, business operations, content it provides, or instructions it gives.
  12. Non-Solicitation During the term and for twelve (12) months after, neither Party will directly solicit for employment the other Party's personnel who were materially involved in the engagement, without written consent. General job postings are not a breach.
  13. Force Majeure Neither Party is liable for delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, outages, platform failures, or government action.
  14. Governing Law and Disputes This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of North Carolina, without regard to conflict-of-laws rules. The Parties will first attempt to resolve disputes in good faith. Unresolved disputes will be brought in the state or federal courts located in Mecklenburg County, North Carolina, and the Parties consent to that jurisdiction.
  15. Miscellaneous
    • Entire agreement. This Agreement, the Schedule, the Proposal, and the referenced authorizations are the entire agreement and supersede prior discussions.
    • Amendments. Changes must be in writing and signed by both Parties. Tier changes and added scope are handled as written change orders.
    • Assignment. Neither Party may assign without the other's written consent, except to a successor in a merger or sale of substantially all assets.
    • Severability. If any provision is unenforceable, the rest remains in effect.
    • Notices. Notices are sent to the email or address on file and are effective on receipt.
    • Counterparts and e-signature. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is an original.
