The funnel math (this is where the numbers come from)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════

Industry baseline:
    Inquiry → contract = 6 percent
    → 33 inquiries needed for 2 closes
    → roughly $9K-10K / mo at $275 CPL

Well-run program (what Tier 2 buys):
    Inquiry → qualified consult = 35 percent
    Qualified consult → contract = 35 percent
    End-to-end = 12.25 percent
    → 17 inquiries needed for 2 closes
    → $4,675 / mo in pure ad media

The Tier 2 number is calibrated to 22 to 32 deals per year (~2
to 2.7 per month). Tiffany's goal of 2 per month is the LOWER
end of Tier 2 target. We are not over-promising.

TALKING POINTS FOR THE DISCOVERY CALL
═════════════════════════════════════════════════

If Tiffany asks "how much to get 2 deals a month?":

If she asks "what if I can only do $5K a month?":

  Answer: "At $5K we cannot get to 2 deals reliably. We can run
   a foundation program at that spend that builds the GBP, the
   reviews, the trust scaffolding, and starts ranking organic.
   You will get inquiries, but probably 0-1 closes per month for
   the first 90 days. The full payoff comes when we scale spend
   in month 4. If $5K is the budget, we should talk about Tier 1
   Foundation instead — it is calibrated for 10-18 deals year 1
   which is 1 to 1.5 / month."

If she asks "what about just SEO, no paid ads?":

  Answer: "SEO compounds but takes 6 to 12 months. If you skip
   paid, you will not hit 2 deals a month until month 9 or 10.
   The recommended program is paid carrying the pipeline while
   organic builds, then organic taking over by Year 2 so paid
   can scale back. SEO-only is a 9 to 18 month wait for the
   same outcome."

If she asks "what is the videography production for?":

  Answer: "Cirilo lives or dies on visual proof. Drone reveals
   of finished pools, dusk LED shots, build-day reels of
   subcontractors. Meta CPL doubles without cinematic creative
   because the algorithm punishes static + text in the luxury
   home services category. CPC Pools is the only Charlotte
   competitor producing volume video and their quality is mid.
   A polished video program is how you take the seat that
   Lombardo and Aloha cannot — they are credentialed but
   invisible because they do not produce content."
