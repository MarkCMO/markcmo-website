PHASE 0 — PREREQUISITE SCAFFOLDING (Days 1-45)
"Build the bucket before pouring water in."
SPEND ON ADS THIS PHASE: ~$0 to $2,000 (LSA only, see below)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

These five items are the gate. Until ALL FIVE are live, do not
turn on Google Search or Meta at scale. Each one, if missing,
raises CPL 30-50%.

  [ ] 1. REVIEWS SCAFFOLD — 40+ Google reviews at 4.8 stars
         - Past-client SMS + email outreach campaign, one-tap
           review links
         - Target: 10 reviews by Day 30, 25 by Day 45, 40 by D60
         - WHY: without this, close rate is 4-6% not 12%. This
           is the single highest-leverage prerequisite.
         - BUILD: review request automation (cdb backend), past
           client list export from Tiffany

  [ ] 2. SUB-1-MINUTE LEAD RESPONSE — automated SMS + email
         - Every web form, LSA call, Houzz msg gets auto-response
           inside 60 seconds
         - WHY: 391% higher conversion at 1-min vs 5-min response
         - Tiffany currently does this MANUALLY on Outlook + phone.
           The Systems Blueprint fixes it. This is why the systems
           build matters to the ad math, not just operations.
         - BUILD: cdb contact form -> Resend + Twilio auto-reply

  [ ] 3. TRUST PAGES LIVE on the website
         - Financing page (Lyon Financial application link)
         - Warranty page (10-yr structural)
         - NC GC license # displayed (NEED FROM TIFFANY)
         - Bonded + insured statement
         - WHY: paid traffic bounces off a site with no trust
           signals at the $150K+ price point
         - BUILD: 4 audit-fix pages (process/financing/warranty/faq)
           — ALREADY QUEUED in the website rebuild

  [ ] 4. GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE claimed + verified + 25 photos
         - Categories, service area, booking link, Q&A
         - WHY: LSA eligibility REQUIRES a verified GBP. No GBP =
           no Local Service Ads = lose the cheapest qualified
           lead channel.
         - BUILD: claim + verify (Tiffany has to confirm postcard
           or video verification), 25 photos from portfolio

  [ ] 5. SERVICE-AREA PAGES indexed (14 Charlotte metro)
         - SouthPark, Myers Park, Ballantyne, Waxhaw, Weddington,
           Marvin, Davidson, Cornelius, Mooresville, Huntersville,
           Matthews, Tega Cay, Fort Mill SC, Charlotte core
         - WHY: lifts both LSA proximity ranking and organic. Takes
           60-90 days to rank so MUST start Day 1.
         - BUILD: 14 templated pages — ALREADY QUEUED in rebuild

  ONLY AD SPEND ALLOWED IN PHASE 0:
  - Google LSA at $2,000/mo CAN start as soon as GBP is verified
    (item 4) even before reviews hit 40, because LSA is pay-per-
    booked-call and Google Guarantee screens for us. But keep it
    capped low until reviews build.

PHASE 1 — TURN ON DEMAND CAPTURE (Days 46-90)
SPEND ON ADS THIS PHASE: ramp $8,500 -> $13,500 / mo
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Once the 5 scaffolding items are live, turn on paid in this order:

  WEEK 7-8:
  [ ] Google LSA scaled to $2,500/mo (7-9 qualified calls/mo)
  [ ] Google Search Ads launch $4,000/mo
      - Long-tail geo + suburb terms ONLY
      - AVOID head terms (Anthony & Sylvan + Blue Haven bid those
        up — we do not fight the volume players on "pool builder
        charlotte")
      - Target: "custom gunite pool waxhaw", "myers park pool
        builder", "luxury pool lake norman", etc.
      - 6-8 form-fills/mo expected

  WEEK 9-10:
  [ ] Meta paid social launch $1,500/mo
      - REQUIRES the videography production retainer to be running
        (see below) — Meta CPL doubles without cinematic creative
      - Lead-form objective, lookalikes off the past-client CRM
      - 4-6 form-fills/mo expected

  [ ] Houzz Pro Premium $400/mo (2-3 directory leads/mo)

  ONGOING PRODUCTION (turn on Week 5, runs forever):
  [ ] Videography production retainer $3,500/mo
      - 1 cinematic build reel + 4 short-form reels / month
      - This is the creative engine. Meta + Pinterest + YouTube
        + organic ALL feed off this. Skipping it doubles Meta CPL.
  [ ] SEO tools $500/mo (Ahrefs + BrightLocal + SurferSEO)
  [ ] Competitive intel $250/mo (SpyFu + Meta Ad Library + Mention)

  END OF PHASE 1: ~19-26 inquiries/mo -> 2-3 signed contracts/mo
  if scaffolding is solid. First signed contract attributable to
  the program: Month 3-4. (Sales cycle is 6-14 weeks from inquiry.)
