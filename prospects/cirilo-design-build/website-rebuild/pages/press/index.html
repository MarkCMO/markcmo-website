About Cirilo

The facts, in one place.

Cirilo Design + Build is a husband-and-wife, design-first custom pool builder in Charlotte. We are happy to provide photography, real numbers, and interviews for stories on luxury home building, real estate, and outdoor living.

  • +Based in Charlotte, NC, serving the entire metro and across North Carolina.
  • +Founded by Ramon and Tiffany Cirilo, a husband-and-wife team. Ramon leads design and build; Tiffany leads the client experience.
  • +What we build: custom concrete (gunite) pools, spas, and full outdoor living, design-build under one roof.
  • +Project range: roughly $85,000 for a starter custom pool to $400,000-plus for an integrated infinity-edge backyard.
  • +Media assets: high-resolution project photography and interviews available on request, with rights granted for features.
  • +Press contact: (910) 409-0648, via our contact page.

Story angles we can support:

  • +The economics. What a luxury pool actually costs in Charlotte, transparent tiers and line items in a notoriously opaque industry.
  • +The local builder. A husband-and-wife, design-first family business competing with national franchises.
  • +The trends. Wellness pools and cold plunges, vanishing edges on Lake Norman, and minimalist modern design for new-money buyers.
  • +The case study. A single signature install, profiled in detail across architecture, engineering, finishes, and photography.
  • +The expert. Informed commentary for any home, garden, or real estate story that touches outdoor living.
