Our Story

Get to Know Us

Cirilo Design + Build was founded by Tiffany and Ramon Cirilo with one clear goal: to create a design-build company rooted in experience, integrity, and craftsmanship. They built not only a life together, but a blended family grounded in hard work and shared values.

Their professional backgrounds span different sides of the construction industry. Tiffany brings a deep background in residential construction, custom swimming pools, outdoor living, and heavy equipment sales. Ramon brings years of experience in large-scale civil construction. Together, they bring a unique balance of structure, creativity, and real-world jobsite knowledge to every project.

After years of working within larger organizations, they recognized a gap between how projects were sold and how they were delivered. Cirilo Design + Build was created to bridge that gap, offering homeowners a more transparent, hands-on, and thoughtfully managed building experience from concept through completion.
