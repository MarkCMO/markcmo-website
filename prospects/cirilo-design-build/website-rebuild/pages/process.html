How We Build

Our Process

From the first consultation to the day you swim, you work with one accountable team. Here is exactly what to expect, stage by stage.

01

Design Consultation

On-site visit. We assess your property, discuss vision, lifestyle, budget, and timeline, and identify access, drainage, and setback considerations.

02

Design & 3D Concepts

Site evaluation, measurements, and 3D renderings in Pool Studio. We refine through revisions until the design is right.

03

Proposal & Material Selection

Final design plans, scope of work, pricing, timeline, and finishes. You see exactly what you are getting before anything breaks ground.

04

Contract & Permitting

Contract signed, deposit collected. We pull permits in our name and handle HOA approval. You work through us for the entire build.

05

Excavation

The dig. Layout staked, pool shape excavated to engineered spec.

06

Rebar & Bonding

Steel reinforcement cage tied and electrically bonded for safety and structural integrity.

07

Plumbing & Electrical Rough-In

Long plumbing runs, returns, skimmers, and electrical conduit installed before the shell.

08

Inspections

County inspections at each required stage. Nothing proceeds until it passes.

09

Shotcrete / Gunite Shell

The structural concrete shell sprayed and hand-formed. This is the pool that lasts 50+ years.

10

Tile & Coping

Waterline tile and coping installed. The detail work that defines the look.

11

Equipment Installation

Pumps, heaters, filters, automation, and lighting set and wired.

12

Decking

Travertine, pavers, or stone decking installed around the pool.

13

Interior Finish

Plaster, pebble, or quartz interior applied. The surface you actually touch.

14

Fill, Startup & Orientation

Pool filled, equipment started, water balanced, and a full homeowner orientation. Final inspection and warranty registration.

Typical timeline: 3 to 6 months from dig to fill for a custom concrete pool, depending on design complexity, weather, and permitting. We give you a milestone schedule at contract and keep you updated at every stage. Once we are engaged, you will be able to see your project status, photos, and documents in your own client portal.
