Privacy Policy

Last updated June 2026

Cirilo Design + Build ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through cirilodb.com and our client and vendor portals, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email, phone, property address, project details, budget, and timeline when you request a consultation, sign a proposal, or communicate with us.
  • Client and vendor portal data: project status, documents you upload, payment reports, bids, and account access codes.
  • Usage information: pages viewed, referring source, approximate location from IP, and device or browser details, collected to improve our site and measure marketing.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule and deliver your project.
  • To operate your private Owner Suite or Vendor Portal.
  • To process and track payments and draws.
  • To improve our website, services, and marketing.
  • To send you project updates and, with your consent, occasional follow-ups. You may opt out at any time.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our business (for example, hosting, database, payment, and email providers) and only as needed to deliver our services, or when required by law.

Data security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including access controls and encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us. You may also opt out of marketing messages at any time.
