Common Questions

Home Additions FAQ

Will the addition match my existing home?

That is the whole point. We match rooflines, finishes, and proportions so the addition reads as original architecture, not a bolt-on. This is where design-first builders separate from volume contractors.

Do additions require permits and engineering?

Yes, always. Every addition is engineered to code and permitted in our name. We manage the structural tie-in to your existing home so it is done correctly and inspected properly.

How long does a home addition take?

It depends on size and complexity, from a single room to a multi-room expansion. We give you a clear timeline at the design consultation and keep you updated through every milestone.
