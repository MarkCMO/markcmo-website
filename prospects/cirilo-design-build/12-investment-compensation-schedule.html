2. Advertising (Media) Budget Commitment · Client's spend

The media budget is Cirilo's advertising spend, paid to advertising platforms (Google, Meta, Houzz, and similar) to place ads. It is not WETYR compensation. WETYR plans, builds, and manages the spend at no percentage markup; management is included in the monthly retainer.

Committed monthly media budget (selected tier):   per month.

Ramp

To avoid spending before the trust scaffolding converts, media may ramp: approximately 50 percent of the committed budget in the first 30 to 60 days while reviews, the Google Business Profile, service-area pages, and tracking go live, then the full committed budget from channel activation (about Week 8).

How the media budget is funded

Option A (recommended): Client-billed platform accounts. Ads run on Cirilo's own Google Ads, Meta, and Houzz accounts with a Cirilo payment method on file. The platforms charge Cirilo directly. No advertising funds pass through WETYR. WETYR receives manager access to build and optimize.
Option B: WETYR-managed budget. Cirilo pre-funds the monthly media budget to WETYR by ACH or wire on the 1st, per the invoice instructions. WETYR pays the platforms and reconciles actual spend in the monthly report. Any underspend carries forward or is refunded at engagement end.

The media budget is committed for the initial 90-day foundation period, then adjustable up or down by Cirilo with 30 days written notice.

4. Terms

  • Term and renewal. 12-month initial term from the signing date. Thereafter month-to-month, cancellable by either party with 30 days written notice.
  • Billing. WETYR invoices the engagement fee at signing and the monthly retainer and systems-build installments on a monthly basis. Each invoice includes ACH and wire remittance instructions, and is due on receipt. The media budget is funded per the option selected in Section 2.
  • What is included. Everything in the selected tier of the Proposal. Third-party subscriptions named in the Proposal are included in the retainer unless noted.
  • What is separate. Advertising media spend (Section 2). Optional add-ons, paid sponsorships, print, video production beyond the included scope, and any tier upgrade are quoted as change orders.
  • Late payment. Balances unpaid 10 days past due accrue 1.5 percent per month.
  • Pause and underspend. Media can be paused with 5 business days notice; retainer continues during an active engagement. Unspent Option B media carries forward.
  • Confidentiality. Both parties keep the other's non-public information confidential, per the Engagement Agreement.
In plain terms

The media budget is your money buying ads. WETYR's compensation is the build fee plus the monthly retainer. They are tracked separately so you always know what you are paying for ads versus what you are paying us.
