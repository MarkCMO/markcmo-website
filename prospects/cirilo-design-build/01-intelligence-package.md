Cirilo Design + Build has a luxury-grade brand wrapper and zero digital traction. **That is the bad news. The good news is that this is the cleanest greenfield opportunity in the Charlotte luxury pool category in years.**

The premium tier of the market (Lombardo, Aloha, Tidal, Charlotte Pool Co.) wins on credentials but is invisible online. The volume tier (Anthony & Sylvan, CPC Pools, Blue Haven, Premier) wins on machine-marketing but reads commodity. **There is no Charlotte builder occupying "design-led modern luxury with a modern marketing presence."** Cirilo can credibly take that seat within 9 months because no one currently owns it.

Target 26-40 signed pool builds in Year 1 at a $225K blended AOV, against a fully-loaded marketing investment of $215K to $800K depending on tier.

## 4. LEAD GEN GAME PLAN (ranked by speed-to-revenue)

This is the menu. We pick the right cocktail for Cirilo's tier (see Section 6 pricing).

### TIER A: Fastest cash flow (revenue inside 30-90 days)

**A1. Google Local Service Ads + Google Guarantee** [30-60 days to live]
Pay-per-lead, fixed price per qualified call. Requires background check, license, insurance verification. Cirilo competes directly with Anthony & Sylvan and Blue Haven here. Hardest to lose if reviews are at 4.8+ stars. **Budget $2K-$5K/mo to start.**

**A2. Google Business Profile optimization + review velocity push** [30-45 days to lift]
Claim, verify, populate 25+ photos, set 60-mile service area, list categories (Swimming Pool Contractor, General Contractor, Construction Co), wire Wix Bookings link. Past-client SMS review outreach to get from 0 to 40+ Google reviews at 4.9 stars within 45 days. **Without 25+ reviews, no paid spend converts in this category.** This is the highest-leverage free action available.

**A3. Geo-targeted Google Search on long-tail** [45-90 days]
Avoid head terms ("pool builder Charlotte") that Anthony & Sylvan and Blue Haven have bid up. Target "custom gunite pool Waxhaw," "Myers Park pool builder," "luxury pool Lake Norman," "pool builder Davidson NC," "Mooresville custom pool." **Budget $4K-$8K/mo to start. Scale to $12K-$20K once landing pages are converting.**

**A4. Realtor / custom-home builder partnership program** [60-90 days]
Standard builder referral fee is 2.5-3% of project value. A $250K build pays $6,250 to an agent. **Wildly cheaper than cold acquisition.** Target list:
- Luxury real estate teams: Dickens Mitchener, Allen Tate Luxury, Cottingham Chalk, Premier Sotheby's Lake Norman
- Custom home builders: Arthur Rutenberg, Bonterra, Plattner Custom Builders, Grandfather Homes, Simonini, Andrew Roby
- Landscape architects: CR Studio, Pursley Dixon, Meyer Greeson Paullin Benson

No competitor runs this systematically. Build the formal program, give partners a co-branded one-pager, ready 3D site-fit renderings, and a quarterly "thank you" payout cadence.

### TIER B: Mid-speed compounding (revenue 60-180 days)

**B1. Meta video creative engine** [60-120 days to optimize]
CPC is the only meaningful Charlotte competitor doing video, and quality is mid. Cinematic 60-second build films, drone reveals, dusk LED reveals, founder-on-camera "why we build this way," owner UGC testimonials. Lead form objective. **Budget $3K-$8K/mo media + $4K-$8K/mo production retainer.** Targeting layered: geo radius around 28207/28211/28173/28036/28117/28031/28277, household income $250K+, lookalikes off CRM.

**B2. Houzz Pro Premium placement + portfolio build** [30-60 days for traffic, 90+ for leads]
$250-$400/mo flat. Required table-stakes for luxury credibility. Upload 40+ high-res project photos, complete profile, request reviews from past clients. **Houzz is the #1 referral channel for $150K+ pool buyers.**

**B3. Service-area page architecture (organic SEO)** [90-180 days to rank]
14 high-quality location pages with local schema, embedded maps, neighborhood-specific photos and case studies: Charlotte, SouthPark, Myers Park, Ballantyne, Waxhaw, Weddington, Marvin, Davidson, Cornelius, Mooresville, Huntersville, Matthews, Tega Cay, Fort Mill. **The single highest-leverage SEO investment** because no competitor owns this systematically.

**B4. Pinterest organic + promoted pins** [60-90 days]
Pool buying is visual. Pinterest drives more $100K+ pool inquiries than Facebook. 30-board portfolio organized by design style. $500/mo promoted pins on top 10 shots.

### TIER C: Long-term moat (revenue 180+ days, biggest payoff)

**C1. YouTube design-reveal series** [180+ days compounding]
12 episodes/year, one per finished build, 8-12 min cinematic. Builds proprietary content library that doesn't depend on ad spend. ~$3K-$5K per episode. Compounds in search and serves as sales tool inside consultations.

**C2. Long-form content / cornerstone SEO** [180-365 days]
2 cornerstone pieces per month on white-space topics: "$250K pool in Charlotte: what's inside," "Mecklenburg / Union County pool permit + HOA guide," "Lake Norman waterfront pool engineering," "Pool resale value in Myers Park." These become organic moats.

**C3. PR + magazine placement** [120-365 days]
Charlotte Magazine "Best Of" submissions for 2026-27 cycle. Founder profiles in Charlotte Business Journal, LKN Magazine, SouthPark Magazine, QC Exclusive. 2-3 podcast appearances (Carolina home builders, luxury real estate). Aloha got an LKN Magazine feature. Cirilo should target the same.

**C4. Direct mail to affluent zips** [90-day sequenced]
Luxury direct mail averages 4.3-9% response with refined lists. 6-drop oversize postcard sequence over 12 months to ~5,000 hand-selected homes (28207 Myers Park, 28211 SouthPark, 28210 Quail Hollow, 28226, 28173 Waxhaw, 28036 Davidson, 28117 Mooresville, 28031 Cornelius, 28277 Ballantyne). ~$3.50/piece all-in = ~$105K/year. **Only run if Cirilo is in Tier-2 or Tier-3 budget.**

**C5. Country club + lifestyle sponsorships** [seasonal]
Target Carmel CC, Charlotte CC, The Peninsula at Lake Norman, Cowans Ford CC. Member-guest tournament sponsorship, club magazine ads, hole sponsorships. $2,500-$25,000 per activation. Best when member-introduced, not cold-pitched.

**C6. "Build My Dream Pool" interactive web tool** [120-day build]
Style quiz + budget calculator + lead capture. Pre-qualifies budget and lifts lead quality 2-3x vs generic contact form. $15K-$35K one-time build, $1K/mo maintenance. Strong differentiator vs every Charlotte competitor.

### TIER D: Avoid or deprioritize

- **Angi / HomeAdvisor / Thumbtack:** Shared-lead, price-shopper audience. **Strongly not recommended for $150K+ work.** Industry data: $542 cost per booked job on Angi with near-zero close at luxury price.
- **Nextdoor:** cheap CPM but reaches every income tier. Useful for review trust only.
- **YouTube/IG influencer collabs:** thin Charlotte-specific creator inventory. Repurpose own content instead.

### Strategic insight: the "fastest path to a signed luxury pool"

For Cirilo's first 90 days, the highest-confidence sequence is:
**A2 (GBP + reviews) -> A1 (LSA + Google Guarantee) -> A3 (long-tail geo Search) -> A4 (realtor/builder partnerships) -> B2 (Houzz)**.

That sequence avoids the head-term bidding war, builds the trust scaffolding that makes paid spend convert, and turns on the highest-leverage referral channel that no competitor is exploiting. Meta video, SEO content, and YouTube are the compounding layer that follows.
