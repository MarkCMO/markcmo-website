Quick Answer

A fractional CEO is a part-time Chief Executive Officer who provides senior executive leadership -- strategic direction, investor relations, operational oversight, and team alignment -- on a retainer engagement rather than as a full-time $400,000 to $700,000 annual hire. Growth-stage companies typically engage a fractional CEO during leadership transitions, between funding rounds, or when scaling from $5M to $30M ARR requires more executive bandwidth than the founding team can provide.

Why Cypress Businesses Hire a Fractional CEO

Cypress is one of Houston's fastest-growing suburban communities with strong petrochemical and energy industry ties. The local business community spans energy, healthcare, retail, creating a competitive market where companies that invest in senior executive leadership outgrow those that don't. A Fractional CEO gives you the executive-level expertise to strategic direction, leadership transitions, board relations, and business growth - without the overhead of a full-time hire.

For businesses in the Greater Houston area, the fractional model makes particular sense: you get a senior executive who has built and scaled functions like yours before, available for 10-20 hours per week, at a fraction of the full-time cost. You keep the capital for growth.

What Fractional CEO Actually Involves

When companies hire a fractional ceo, they are buying senior executive accountability -- not advisory reports. Here is what every engagement covers from day one:

  • Strategic Vision and Direction - Define the 3-5 year company vision, the strategic priorities that drive it, and the operating model that makes it achievable.
  • Operational Leadership - Run the executive team, establish performance accountability, and build the operating cadences that translate strategy to execution.
  • Fundraising and Investor Relations - Lead capital raises, investor communications, and board relationships -- with the credibility that moves deals forward.
  • M&A and Partnerships - Identify, negotiate, and integrate strategic acquisitions and channel relationships that accelerate non-organic growth.
  • Crisis and Turnaround Management - Stabilize distressed companies, reallocate resources, and rebuild executive team confidence when the business needs a reset.
  • Exit and Transition Readiness - Prepare the narrative, management team, and financial story for acquisition, PE exit, or leadership succession.

Who This Is Right For

Companies in transition: CEO departure, founder burnout, rapid scale-up requiring professional management, or a turnaround situation. PE-backed companies that need an experienced operator between the exit and the next hire. Founders who are visionaries but need an executive partner to build the execution engine around them.

How much does a Fractional CEO cost in Cypress, TX?

A Fractional CEO in Cypress typically costs $3,000 to $15,000 per month depending on scope and engagement hours. Most Greater Houston businesses engage at 15-20 hours per week. This compares to $200,000 to $450,000+ per year for a full-time executive.

Do you work on-site in Cypress or remotely?

We work in the model that best fits your business - remote, hybrid, or on-site in Cypress and the Greater Houston area as needed. Most engagements are primarily remote with on-site sprints for key milestones and team sessions.

How is a Fractional CEO different from a consultant?

A Fractional CEO is an embedded executive who owns the executive leadership function - they lead your team, manage vendors, and are accountable to outcomes. A consultant advises from the outside and delivers reports. The fractional model means direct execution responsibility, not recommendations you have to implement yourself.

What industries do you serve in the Greater Houston area?

In Cypress and the broader Greater Houston market, we serve energy, healthcare, retail. We bring experience from 15+ years of C-suite engagements across multiple industries and apply that cross-sector pattern recognition to your specific market context.
