How to Hire a Fractional Chief of Staff

A 5-step checklist for hiring a fractional Chief of Staff in 2026 - and the red flags to avoid.

Quick Answer

Hire a fractional Chief of Staff in 5 steps: confirm stage fit, verify track record, test depth, confirm execution capacity, negotiate quarterly-renewable terms. A fractional Chief of Staff can start in 1-2 weeks vs 3-6 months for full-time. Book a call.

The 5-step process

1) Confirm stage fit - $2M-$25M revenue is the band where a fractional Chief of Staff delivers the best economics. 2) Verify track record - ask for three engagements with before-and-after outcomes. 3) Test depth in executive leverage, operating cadence, and cross-functional execution. 4) Confirm execution capacity - do they ship work or just advise. 5) Negotiate quarterly-renewable terms with a 90-day trial.

Red flags

Avoid a fractional Chief of Staff who cannot show specific outcomes, sells frameworks instead of results, resists a trial period, insists on annual lock-in, or cannot explain their approach in plain English. The best operators are confident enough to work on renewable terms.
